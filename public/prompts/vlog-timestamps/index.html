Vlog timestamps: my day, hour by hour

Short clips of your day, each with a time and a one-line caption. AI turns your rough notes into punchy timestamp captions.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

An Instagram Reels trends roundup updated October 23, 2026 lists "Vlog timestamps" as a new trend that week: every clip of the day gets its own timestamp, with quick swipe transitions from one moment to the next.

How to use it

  1. Film a 2–3 second clip at each moment of your day and jot down the time.
  2. Paste your notes into the main prompt to get one caption per clip.
  3. Put the time and caption on each clip, and use a quick swipe transition between them.
  4. Post with a trending sound from the app.

Prompts

Main prompt
Turn my notes into on-screen captions for a timestamp vlog. Each caption starts with the time, then a short line under 10 words. Notes: [paste your times and what happened, e.g. 6:45 a.m. alarm, 7:10 coffee spill, 12:30 lunch at desk]. Keep my real times. Make the lines honest and a bit funny, like I'm talking to a friend. Keep one calm, sweet moment near the end. No hashtags, no emojis.
Variation: a fall Saturday
Write timestamp captions for a cosy fall Saturday vlog in Canada. My real plan: [list your times and activities, like a farmers' market, raking leaves, soup on the stove]. Each line starts with the time and is under 10 words. Warm and a little funny. Give me a closing line for the last clip.
Variation: small business
I run [your type of business]. Write timestamp captions for a "day in the life" vlog of my business, from opening to close. Real moments: [list times and what happens, e.g. 7:00 a.m. ovens on, 11:30 lunch rush]. Each line starts with the time and is under 10 words. Show the work behind the scenes. Friendly, not salesy. Don't make up sales numbers, reviews or offers.

Tips

  • Use your real times. Viewers like seeing how the day actually went.
  • Keep each clip short so the video stays under 30 seconds.
  • Mix busy clips with one quiet one so the ending feels earned.
  • Only film people who have said yes.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
