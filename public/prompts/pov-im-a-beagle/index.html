POV: I'm a beagle

Film something ordinary, caption it as if a beagle is doing it, and end with a bark. AI writes the beagle's inner thoughts for you.

ChatGPTClaudeGemini

Why it's trending

A social media trends roundup dated October 21, 2026 lists "POV: I'm a beagle" as just starting to catch on: a normal everyday task with a beagle-persona twist that ends in a bark.

How to use it

  1. Pick an ordinary task you can film in 10 to 15 seconds: grocery shopping, opening the fridge, a work meeting, a walk.
  2. Ask a text AI for beagle-brain captions with the main prompt.
  3. Film the task, add two or three captions on screen, and end the clip with a loud bark (yours, or your own dog's).
  4. Post it with the caption "POV: I'm a beagle".

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm filming a short clip of me [ordinary task, for example: unpacking groceries]. Write 6 caption ideas for a "POV: I'm a beagle" video. Each caption is the inner thoughts of a very excited, food-obsessed, nose-first beagle doing that task. Keep each under 10 words. Make them build from calm to completely distracted by a smell. The last one should lead into a bark. Funny, warm, family-friendly.
Variation: your own dog
My dog is a [breed or mix] named [name] who loves [favourite thing] and hates [least favourite thing]. Write 5 short on-screen captions for a "POV: I'm [name]" video where I act out my dog's day: waking up, seeing the leash, hearing the mail carrier, dinner time and bedtime. Under 10 words each, funny and kind. End with a bark or howl cue.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: a coffee shop]. Write 5 short captions for a "POV: I'm a beagle working at [type of business]" video. The beagle notices the smells and sounds of my shop, gets very excited about [a real product we sell], and ends with a bark. Under 10 words each. No prices, no claims we can't back up.

Tips

  • The bark at the end is the punchline. Make it loud and sudden.
  • Shoot from low down, around knee height, to feel like a dog's view.
  • Short captions work best. Two or three on screen is plenty.
  • If you film your own dog, keep it calm and fun for them.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
