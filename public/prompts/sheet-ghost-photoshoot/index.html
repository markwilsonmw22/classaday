Sheet ghost photoshoot

Classic bedsheet ghosts with sunglasses, posed like a fashion shoot. Cute, funny and easy to make with friends or on your own.

ChatGPTGeminiMidjourney

Why it's trending

A Halloween TikTok and Reels trend roundup updated on October 14, 2026 lists the ghost photoshoot: people in white sheets and sunglasses posing outdoors or in groups. AI lets you make the shoot without the sheets, or try poses you can't do in real life. It's a fun, cartoonish take, different from our moonlit ghost portrait pack.

How to use it

  1. Pick a setting: a fall park, a pumpkin patch, a city street or your own living room.
  2. Paste the main prompt and choose how many ghosts you want.
  3. Make a few versions, then post them as a carousel or cut them together to a beat.

Prompts

Main prompt
A playful fashion-style photoshoot of three classic bedsheet ghosts: plain white sheets with two round eye holes, each wearing dark sunglasses over the sheet. They pose confidently in a park full of orange and red fall leaves, one leaning on a tree, one mid-step, one holding a small pumpkin. Golden late-afternoon sun, soft lens flare, shallow depth of field, shot on a 50mm lens. Looks like a real photo, cheerful not scary. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: you as the ghost
Use my attached photo for the setting and my pose only. Replace me with a classic bedsheet ghost: a white sheet with two eye holes, wearing dark sunglasses, in the exact same pose and spot. Keep the background, lighting and camera angle the same. Natural and photo-real.
Variation: 1970s album-cover style
Four bedsheet ghosts in sunglasses lined up against a warm orange backdrop, posed like a 1970s band photo: one sitting on a stool, others standing with arms crossed. Soft film grain, warm faded colours, studio lighting. Square 1:1, no text.
Variation: small business
Two bedsheet ghosts in sunglasses working behind the counter of a cosy local [café / bakery / bookshop]: one handing a customer a paper bag, one arranging the display. Warm indoor light, real-photo look, friendly and funny. Leave clear space at the top for our own text. Vertical 4:5.

Tips

  • Say "cheerful not scary" or the AI may make the ghosts creepy.
  • Ask for the same sheets and sunglasses in every image so a carousel looks like one shoot.
  • Add your shop name and hours in your editor rather than in the prompt.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
