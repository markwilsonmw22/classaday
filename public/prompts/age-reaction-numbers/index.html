How I'd react to the age you…

Numbers count up on screen while your face reacts: calm, then worried, then shocked. AI picks the topic and writes the reaction cues for each number.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trend report published October 21, 2026 lists "How I'd react to the age you…" in its October roundup: creators react as on-screen numbers rise. The same report says it also works with prices and habits.

How to use it

  1. Choose a safe, light topic: the age you first stayed up past midnight, how many coffees a day, how many tabs you keep open.
  2. Use the main prompt to get the number ladder and a reaction cue for each step.
  3. Film one take of your face, changing expression each time a number changes, then add the numbers as text in your editor.
  4. End on a big, over-the-top reaction for the last number.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a "How I'd react to the age you…" video. Suggest 8 light, family-friendly topics, like "the age you learned to ride a bike" or "the age you first saw a scary movie". Then, for the topic I pick, [topic], give me a ladder of 8 numbers from low to high and a short facial reaction cue for each one, like "proud nod", "slight squint" or "full gasp". Keep it kind, not judgy. Nothing about money worries, health or anything personal.
Variation: everyday habits
Write a "How I'd react to how many…" ladder about an everyday habit, for example: [how many browser tabs you have open]. Give 8 numbers from small to huge, a reaction cue for each, and a funny final line for the last number. Add 3 caption ideas that invite people to comment their number.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: bike repair shop]. Write a "How I'd react to how long since you…" video about a friendly maintenance habit, like [how long since you pumped up your tires]. Give 8 time steps from "last week" to "never", a reaction cue for each, and a short, honest tip at the end. No scare tactics, made-up stats or fake offers.

Tips

  • One continuous take of your face is easier than 8 separate clips. Add the numbers afterwards.
  • Exaggerate a little more with each step so the build is easy to follow.
  • Leave a gap of about one second per number so people can read it.
  • Ask viewers to comment their number. It gets people talking without any pressure.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
