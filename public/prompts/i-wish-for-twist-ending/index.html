"I wish for…": your wish comes true, just not the way you meant

Make a wish with a wishbone, a candle or a coin, snap, and the wish comes true in the most literal, ironic way. AI makes the twist shot.

ChatGPTGeminiKlingVeo

Why it's trending

Virlo's TikTok trend tracker, updated November 26, 2026, lists "I wish for…" as a fresh format: 188 creators in the last seven days, peaking on November 22. People lip-sync an "I wish for…" line while holding a small object, then snap or gesture and the wish comes true in an unexpected, ironic way.

How to use it

  1. Pick a wish with a funny literal twist, like "I wish for snow" or "I wish for a bigger kitchen". Keep it light and kind.
  2. Film or photograph yourself holding the object (a wishbone, a birthday candle, a coin by a fountain) and saying your wish.
  3. Attach that photo to ChatGPT or Gemini with the main prompt to make the twist shot, then animate it in Kling or Veo if you want movement.
  4. In TikTok or Reels, cut from the snap to the twist right on the beat, with your wish written on screen.

Prompts

Main prompt (the twist photo)
Use the attached photo of me holding [OBJECT, e.g. a wishbone]. Keep my face, hair, clothes and features exactly the same, so it clearly looks like me. My wish was: "[YOUR WISH, e.g. I wish for snow]". Show the wish coming true in the most literal, ironic way: [TWIST, e.g. a single snowflake lands on my nose while the rest of the street stays green / a tiny snow cloud hovers only over my head]. Same room or place and camera angle as the original. My expression is a slow, deadpan look at the camera. Vertical 9:16, looks like a real phone photo. No text, no logos, no other people.
Variation: the snap clip (Kling or Veo)
Use the attached photo. Five seconds, vertical 9:16, same place and angle. The person holds [OBJECT] up near their face, closes their eyes for a second and snaps it (or blows it out). A small pop of sparkle, then [TWIST] appears around them. They open their eyes, look at the twist, then look straight at the camera with a flat, unimpressed face. Keep the face the same throughout. Natural movement, no text, no logos, no other people.
Wish ideas (text)
Give me 12 short "I wish for…" ideas for a funny video where the wish comes true in a literal, ironic way. Write each as the wish, then the twist in one short line, for example "I wish for a bigger kitchen" then "I shrink to the size of a teacup". Keep them light and kind: nothing about bodies, looks, money, health or other people. Make a few fit a Canadian winter.
Variation for a small business
I run [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY, PROVINCE]. Write a plan for an "I wish for…" Reel where I make a wish about my work and it comes true in a silly, literal way (for example a baker wishes for "more rolls" and gets buried in rolling pins). Give me three wish-and-twist ideas, the shot list, one on-screen line and a caption. Keep it honest: no made-up deals, prices, countdowns, reviews or customer numbers.

Tips

  • The deadpan look at the end is the joke. Hold it for a full second before the clip ends.
  • Keep the camera in the same spot for the wish and the twist so the change feels instant.
  • Pick wishes about things, places or weather, not about anyone's looks or body.
  • Check the AI shot still looks like you. If your face changed, ask it to try again with your features unchanged.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
