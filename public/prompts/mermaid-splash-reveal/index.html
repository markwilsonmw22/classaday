Mermaid splash: one big splash and you're underwater with a tail

Pose in a leafy garden, a wall of water splashes over the lens, and you're suddenly underwater as a mermaid with a shimmering tail.

KlingVeoHiggsfield

Why it's trending

AI Trend Club lists a mermaid splash transformation traced to a TikTok tutorial posted November 16, 2026. It counted about 297K views and 12K likes on that post as of November 23. It's a very short clip, about 3 seconds, made from one photo. Our other reveal packs use a hand swipe or a light sweep; this one uses a splash of water.

How to use it

  1. Pick a clear, full-body photo of yourself outdoors, standing in good light.
  2. Paste the main prompt into an image-to-video tool with your photo.
  3. Add a splash sound right on the cut, then let a calm underwater sound take over.
  4. Post it and say it's made with AI.

Prompts

Main prompt: garden pose, splash, mermaid
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face, skin tone, hair and body exactly the same. Make a 5-second vertical clip. Seconds 0 to 2: they stand in a leafy green garden in soft daylight, strike a confident pose and smile at the camera. Second 2: a bright, clear wall of water splashes across the whole frame toward the lens. Seconds 2 to 5: the splash clears and the same person is now underwater in a calm blue sea, floating among soft sun rays and tiny bubbles. Their legs are now a long mermaid tail with shimmering teal and gold scales and a wide sheer fin. They wear a simple matching scale top. Their hair drifts slowly in the water and they give a small wave. Realistic light and water, smooth motion. No text, no logos.
Variation: pool-side to coral reef
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face and features the same. Make a 6-second vertical clip. They sit on the edge of a backyard pool in a towel wrap, swinging their feet. They look at the camera and jump in. The splash fills the frame. When it clears, they swim slowly past a bright coral reef with small striped fish, now with a deep purple and silver mermaid tail and a matching scale top. Gentle bubbles rise from their hair. Clear tropical water, soft sun rays from above. No text, no logos.
Variation: winter twist
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face and features the same. Make a 6-second vertical clip. They stand on a snowy lakeshore in a warm winter coat and toque, waving at the camera. A big splash of icy blue water bursts across the frame. When it clears, they float under clear frozen ice with light glowing through it, now as a mermaid with a pale silver-blue tail, a matching scale top and tiny frost sparkles in their hair. They smile and twirl once. No text, no logos.
Variation for a small business: swim school or pool party venue
Use this photo of our instructor or host [upload]. Keep their face exactly the same. Make a 6-second vertical clip. They stand on a bright indoor pool deck holding a clipboard and give a thumbs up. A big splash fills the frame. When it clears, they are underwater in the same clear blue pool, now as a friendly mermaid with a bright coral-pink tail and a matching scale top, waving the camera in. Clean, bright light. No readable text, no logos or brand names.

Tips

  • The splash is the cut. Ask for it to cover the whole frame so the change feels like magic.
  • Keep it short. Five or six seconds is plenty for this one.
  • Only use photos of adults who said yes.
  • Say it's made with AI in your caption.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
