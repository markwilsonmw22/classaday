Product research · Day 9 of 30

Shortlist and choose three products to test

3 hours

Score your remaining products on one sheet and commit to three you'll sample and test.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 150 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

Research can go on forever. Today it stops. You'll score your best products, check the numbers, and pick three to test.

The final scorecard

Score each product 1–5 on:

Criteria What a 5 looks like
Demand evidence Ads running 30+ days from several stores, plus organic buying comments
Margin Break-even ROAS under 1.8 at your planned price
Demo-ability The benefit is obvious in 3 seconds of video
Differentiation You have a clear angle or offer competitors don't
Supply 2+ suppliers, tracked shipping under 12 days, good reviews
Fit with niche Same customer as your other products

Maximum 30. Shortlist anything above 22.

Mix your three

Don't pick three almost-identical products. A strong mix is:

  • One evergreen product that solves a constant problem.
  • One seasonal or trending product with momentum now.
  • One with a high price or bundle potential, to raise average order value.

Run the calculator again

Put each final product through your Day 2 calculator with real supplier prices. If break-even ROAS is above 2.2, change the price, add a bundle or swap the product.

Commit

Write your three products, planned prices and break-even CPAs at the top of your sheet. From tomorrow, you stop researching and start building.

2Worked example

Priya's final three:

Product Score Price Landed cost Break-even CPA Break-even ROAS
Self-cleaning slicker brush 27 $32.99 $11.00 $20.73 1.59
Paw cleaner cup (seasonal) 24 $29.99 $9.90 $18.92 1.59
Grooming kit (brush + nail grinder + comb) 23 $59.99 $22.60 $35.35 1.70

The nail grinder has a battery, so she checks the supplier ships it with tracking to Canada and the US. It does, via a battery-approved line. She swaps it to a manual nail clipper in the kit to remove the risk.

★Tip

If you're torn between two products, pick the one you can film better. Creative wins tests more often than small differences in margin.
