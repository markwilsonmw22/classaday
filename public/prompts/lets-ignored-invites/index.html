"Let's..." ignored invites

A friend asks "Let's..." again and again and you don't budge, until one invite names the thing you love and you burst into action. AI writes the invites.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

An Instagram trend report updated October 21, 2026 lists "Let's..." in its October 14 roundup: a run of invitations the creator ignores, until one names something they love and the edit turns energetic. This pack helps you write the lines.

How to use it

  1. Pick the one thing that always gets you off the couch: a snack, a place, a hobby or your pet.
  2. Paste the main prompt into the AI and choose 4 or 5 dull invites plus the winning one.
  3. Film yourself still and bored for each dull invite, with the line as on-screen text.
  4. For the last line, cut to a fast, upbeat shot of you jumping up, grabbing your coat or running to the door.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a "Let's..." Reel. A friend keeps inviting me to things and I ignore them, until the last invite names the thing I love. The thing I love is: [for example: a hot chocolate and a box of doughnut holes]. Write 8 dull or mildly annoying invites, each starting with "Let's" and under 8 words, like "Let's clean out the garage". Then write 3 options for the final invite that names my favourite thing in a fun way. Keep it light and friendly. Don't name any real brands or people.
Variation: pet version
Write a "Let's..." Reel script from my dog's point of view. I invite my dog to 6 things it ignores, each starting with "Let's" and under 8 words, like "Let's get your nails trimmed". The final invite is the thing my dog loves most: [for example: a walk to the park with the big puddle]. Give me 3 funny final lines to choose from, plus a short caption for the post.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: bike repair shop]. Write a "Let's..." Reel script where a customer ignores 6 boring invites, each starting with "Let's" and under 8 words, like "Let's sort the recycling". The final invite is about my shop, for example a fun reason to visit this week. Keep it playful and honest. No made-up discounts, prices or claims. Add a short caption and a gentle call to visit.

Tips

  • The joke works when the dull shots are very still and the final shot is fast and loud.
  • Use the same spot and outfit for every dull shot so the change at the end pops.
  • Pick something you truly love. Real excitement reads better than acting.
  • Keep each line on screen for about two seconds so people can read it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
