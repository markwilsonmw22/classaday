# How this plan works

This plan is for busy weeks: work, school runs, late evenings. It feeds **two adults for seven days** with balanced meals, and most dinners take **20 minutes or less** on the night. Double everything for a family of four.

The secret isn't fast recipes. It's **cooking once, eating twice**: a 90-minute prep session on Sunday makes the weekdays easy.

## The rules

1. **Prep on Sunday.** Cook a grain, roast a tray of vegetables, make a soup, boil eggs and wash greens.
2. **Cook dinner three nights.** Leftovers become lunches. Two other nights take under 15 minutes.
3. **Keep breakfast boring.** Two or three good options on rotation saves daily decisions.
4. **Shop once.** One list, organised by aisle, for the whole week.
5. **Plan one flexible night.** Leftovers, eggs on toast or a night out. Real life happens.

## What you need

- 2 large sheet pans, a large pot, a large frying pan
- 8–10 food storage containers with lids, plus a few jars for oats
- A sharp knife, a large cutting board and a timer

## Sunday prep: 90 minutes

Work in this order so the oven and stove are busy at the same time.

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Put a pot of rice or quinoa on |
| 0:05 | Start the Tomato Lentil Soup (it simmers by itself) |
| 0:15 | Chop vegetables for Monday's tray and the week: peppers, onions, broccoli, carrots |
| 0:30 | Roast a tray of mixed vegetables for bowls and wraps (25 min) |
| 0:35 | Boil 6 eggs for 10 minutes, then cool in cold water |
| 0:45 | Make 2 jars of overnight oats |
| 0:55 | Wash and dry salad greens; store with a paper towel |
| 1:05 | Cool the rice quickly on a tray, then refrigerate |
| 1:10 | Portion the soup into containers. Freeze two portions if you like |
| 1:20 | Clean up and stick the plan on the fridge |

> **Rice safety:** spread cooked rice on a tray to cool quickly and refrigerate within 1 hour. Use it within 1–2 days, and reheat until steaming hot.

# Breakfast

::: recipe
title: Overnight Oats, Three Ways
tag: Breakfast · No cook
intro: Five minutes the night before, zero minutes in the morning.
meta: Serves 1 per jar | Prep 5 min | Chill overnight
ingredients:
## Base, per jar
- 45 g (½ cup) rolled oats
- 120 ml (½ cup) milk or plant drink
- 60 g (¼ cup) Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)
## Choose a flavour
- **Berry:** ½ cup berries, a little lemon zest
- **Peanut butter banana:** 1 tbsp peanut butter, ½ sliced banana, pinch of cinnamon
- **Apple pie:** ½ grated apple, ½ tsp cinnamon, 1 tbsp chopped walnuts
method:
1. Stir the base ingredients together in a jar.
2. Add your flavour, stir, and close the lid.
3. Refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours). Keeps for 3 days.
4. Eat cold, or microwave for 1–2 minutes with a splash more milk.
:::

::: recipe
title: Five-Minute Veggie Scramble Wraps
tag: Breakfast · 5 minutes
intro: Use leftover roasted vegetables from Sunday for an almost instant hot breakfast.
meta: Serves 2 | Cook 5 min
ingredients:
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup leftover roasted vegetables, chopped
- A handful of baby spinach
- 30 g cheddar or feta
- 2 whole-wheat tortillas
- Salt, pepper, hot sauce or salsa
method:
1. Warm the vegetables in a non-stick pan over medium heat for 1 minute. Add the spinach until it wilts.
2. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper and pour into the pan. Stir gently until just set.
3. Sprinkle with cheese, divide between the tortillas, add salsa and roll up.
notes:
- Wrap in foil to eat on the go.
:::

::: recipe
title: Banana Oat Pancakes
tag: Breakfast · Weekend
intro: Three main ingredients, naturally sweet, and ready in 15 minutes.
meta: Serves 2 (about 8 small pancakes) | Prep 5 min | Cook 10 min
ingredients:
- 1 large ripe banana
- 2 large eggs
- 45 g (½ cup) rolled oats
- ½ tsp baking powder, pinch of cinnamon and salt
- Butter or oil for the pan
- Yogurt and berries, to serve
method:
1. Blend everything until smooth. Rest for 5 minutes to thicken.
2. Heat a lightly greased non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
3. Cook 2 tablespoons of batter per pancake for 2 minutes, until bubbles form, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
4. Serve with yogurt and berries.

# Lunch

::: recipe
title: Big-Batch Tomato Lentil Soup
tag: Lunch · Make ahead
intro: Thick, warming and very cheap. Makes enough for four lunches; freezes well.
meta: Serves 6 | Prep 10 min | Cook 30 min
ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, pinch of chilli flakes
- 200 g (1 cup) dried red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (796 ml) crushed tomatoes
- 1.25 L (5 cups) vegetable or chicken stock
- 2 handfuls spinach
- Juice of ½ lemon, salt and pepper
method:
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion and carrots for 6–8 minutes until soft.
2. Add the garlic and spices and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft.
4. Stir in the spinach and lemon juice. Season well. Blend partly for a creamier soup.
5. Cool, then portion into containers. Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
:::

# Dinner

::: recipe
title: One-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken & Vegetables
tag: Dinner · One pan
intro: Everything roasts together on one tray. Make extra for Tuesday's lunch bowls.
meta: Serves 2 + leftovers | Prep 10 min | Cook 35–40 min
ingredients:
- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 500 g baby potatoes, halved
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper
method:
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Toss the potatoes and onion with 1 tbsp oil and salt on a large sheet pan. Roast for 10 minutes.
3. Mix the remaining oil with lemon zest, juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper. Rub over the chicken.
4. Push the potatoes to the sides, add the chicken skin-side up and roast for 20 minutes.
5. Add the broccoli, toss with the pan juices, and roast for 8–10 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 74°C (165°F) and the skin is crisp.
:::

::: recipe
title: 15-Minute Garlic Shrimp Noodles
tag: Dinner · 15 minutes
intro: Faster than takeout. Have everything chopped before you start; it moves quickly.
meta: Serves 2 | Prep 5 min | Cook 10 min
ingredients:
- 150 g egg or rice noodles
- 250 g raw shrimp, peeled
- 1 pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 cups spinach or bok choy
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp oil
## Sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp lime juice, pinch of chilli flakes
method:
1. Cook the noodles according to the packet. Drain and rinse briefly.
2. Mix the sauce ingredients in a cup.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan over high heat. Cook the shrimp for 2 minutes until pink; remove.
4. Add the pepper for 2 minutes, then the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
5. Add the noodles, greens, shrimp and sauce. Toss for 1–2 minutes until glossy.
:::

::: recipe
title: Black Bean & Corn Tacos
tag: Dinner · Vegetarian
intro: Ready in 15 minutes from cans you already have.

- 1 can (540 ml) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup canned or frozen corn

::: recipe
title: Sheet-Pan Sausage, Peppers & Potatoes
tag: Dinner · One pan
intro: Hands-off comfort food. Make enough for Friday's lunch.
meta: Serves 2 + leftovers | Prep 10 min | Cook 30 min
ingredients:
- 4 sausages (about 400 g)
- 500 g baby potatoes, halved
- 2 peppers, cut into strips
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt and pepper
method:
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and seasoning on a sheet pan. Roast for 10 minutes.
3. Add the peppers, onion, remaining oil and the sausages. Roast for 20–22 minutes, turning the sausages once, until cooked through (71°C / 160°F for pork, 74°C / 165°F for chicken sausages).
:::

::: recipe
title: Spinach & White Bean Pasta
tag: Dinner · 20 minutes
intro: A pantry pasta with garlic, lemon and parmesan that tastes far better than its effort.
meta: Serves 2 + 1 lunch | Prep 5 min | Cook 15 min
ingredients:
- 250 g short pasta
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- Pinch of chilli flakes
- 1 can (540 ml) white beans, drained and rinsed
- 3 large handfuls spinach
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 40 g parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
method:
1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, warm the oil and garlic in a large pan over medium heat until the garlic is lightly golden. Add the chilli.
3. Add the beans and cook for 3 minutes, mashing a few.
4. Add the drained pasta, spinach, lemon zest and juice, parmesan and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy.
:::

::: recipe
title: Chicken Fried Rice
tag: Dinner · Leftovers
intro: The best use for cold leftover rice. Use any vegetables you have.
meta: Serves 2 | Prep 5 min | Cook 10 min
ingredients:
- 3 cups cold cooked rice (from Sunday, used within 1–2 days)
- 1–1½ cups cooked chicken, chopped
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots, or chopped vegetables
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp oil
method:
1. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok over high heat. Scramble the eggs quickly and set aside.
2. Add the vegetables, garlic and green onion whites for 2 minutes.
3. Add the rice, breaking up clumps, and stir-fry for 4 minutes until hot and slightly crisp.
4. Add the chicken, eggs, soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss for 2 minutes until steaming hot. Top with green onions.
:::

# Leftovers & budget

## The leftover remix

| You have | Turn it into |
|---|---|
| Roast chicken | Grain bowls, wraps, fried rice, soup |
| Roasted vegetables | Scramble wraps, pasta, blended into soup |
| Cooked rice | Fried rice (within 1–2 days) |
| Soup | Lunches, or a sauce for pasta |
| Half a can of beans | Tacos, salads, mashed on toast |
| Spinach about to wilt | Smoothies, soup, pasta, eggs |
| Overripe bananas | Pancakes, oats, freeze for smoothies |

## Ten ways to spend less

1. Shop with a list and stick to it.
2. Check the fridge and cupboards before you shop.
3. Buy frozen vegetables and fruit: cheaper, no waste, just as nutritious.
4. Buy rice, oats, lentils and pasta in bigger bags.
5. Use beans and lentils for some meals instead of meat.
6. Buy whole chickens or bone-in thighs; they cost less per kilo.
7. Choose store brands for staples.
8. Cook once, eat twice.
9. Freeze bread, leftovers and ripe fruit before they go off.
10. Plan one "use it up" meal every week.
