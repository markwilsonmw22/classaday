Build the store · Day 14 of 30

Brand identity on a budget

3 hours

Create a simple, consistent brand: name, logo, colours, fonts and voice, so the store looks trustworthy from the first second.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 150 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

You don't need a designer. You need consistency. A store that uses the same two colours, one or two fonts and a clear voice everywhere looks professional. One that mixes ten styles looks like a scam.

Name check

If you haven't finalised the name: it should be easy to say out loud, hint at the niche, and have the .com and social handles available. Check Instagram and TikTok handles today.

Logo

A clean wordmark (your name in a good font) beats a clip-art icon. Make one in Canva or Figma:

  • Pick one font and set the name in it. Try lowercase vs title case.
  • Make three versions: full colour, all white, all black.
  • Export PNG with a transparent background, at least 1000px wide, plus a square version for social profiles and the favicon.

Colours

Choose:

  • One main brand colour (used for buttons and highlights)
  • One dark neutral for text (not pure black)
  • One light neutral for backgrounds (not pure white)
  • Optional: one accent for sale badges

Check your button colour has enough contrast with white text. Free contrast checkers online will tell you.

Fonts

Two at most: one for headings, one for body text. Shopify themes include Google-style fonts. Choose a readable body font at 16px or larger.

Voice

Write three words that describe how you talk to customers, e.g. warm, practical, a little funny. Then write how you don't talk: no hype, no fake urgency. Use these in every product description, email and ad.

Brand kit

Put everything in one place: logo files, hex colour codes, font names, voice words and two example sentences. You'll use this for ads, emails and packaging.

Using AI to help

AI tools are great for generating name ideas, taglines and first drafts of copy. Treat them as a starting point: always edit so it sounds like you, and check any name isn't someone else's trademark.

2Worked example

Hearth & Hound brand kit:

  • Wordmark: "hearth & hound" in a rounded serif, lowercase.
  • Colours: forest green #2F4A3A (buttons), ink #1E1E1C (text), oat #F5F0E6 (background), terracotta #C76B4A (sale badges).
  • Fonts: headings in a soft serif, body in a clean sans-serif at 17px.
  • Voice: warm, practical, dog-loving. Never: shouty, fake-scarce, "BUY NOW!!!"
  • Example line: "Five minutes of brushing on the sofa beats an hour of vacuuming. Your dog will think it's a massage."

3Today's tasks

✓By tonight you'll have

A brand kit with logo files, colours, fonts and voice that you'll use everywhere.

★Tip

Before you finalise the name, search it on the Canadian and US trademark databases. It takes five minutes and could save you a rebrand.
