Things I'd tell my younger self carousel

A calm carousel post with one piece of advice to your younger self on each slide. AI helps you find the words and design simple slide backgrounds.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

An Instagram trend report updated October 14, 2026 lists "Things I'd tell my younger self" as a new format that week: a reflective carousel with one realization per slide. This pack uses AI to turn your own notes into short, clear slides.

How to use it

  1. Jot down a few real lessons you've learned, in your own rough words.
  2. Paste the main prompt with your notes. The AI tightens them into one short line per slide.
  3. Use the background prompt to make a matching slide image, add your lines as text in any design app, and post as a carousel.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making an Instagram carousel called "Things I'd tell my younger self". Here are my rough notes on lessons I've learned: [paste your notes]. Turn them into 7 slides. Slide 1 is a short title. Slides 2 to 7 each have one piece of advice in 12 words or less, written in my voice, warm and honest. Don't add lessons I didn't give you. Then suggest a one-line caption for the post.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business] and started it in [year]. Here are my rough notes on what I wish I had known on day one: [paste notes]. Turn them into a 7-slide carousel called "Things I'd tell myself on day one". One short lesson per slide, 12 words or less, friendly and practical. Only use lessons from my notes. End with a caption that invites other owners to share theirs.
Variation: help me find the lessons
Ask me 6 short questions, one at a time, to help me remember what I've learned in the last [5 / 10] years about [work, friendship, daily habits, moving to a new city]. After I answer, turn my answers into 6 short carousel lines starting with "Dear younger me". Keep my words as much as you can.
Variation: slide background image
Make a simple square background for a text carousel slide: soft paper texture in warm cream, a faint hand-drawn doodle of a small plant in one corner, lots of empty space in the middle for text. Calm, cozy and minimal. No words or letters.

Tips

  • Real lessons from your own life land better than general quotes, so start from your notes.
  • Keep one idea per slide and the same font on every slide.
  • Make one background and reuse it so the carousel looks like a set.
  • Leave personal details out: no full names, addresses or private stories about other people.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
