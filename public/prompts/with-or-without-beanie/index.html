With or without? The one-beanie confidence switch

Go from plain and awkward to intense and confident by adding one black beanie on the beat. AI makes the "after" shot and the caption.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

Know Your Meme added the "Skull Cap Trend" on October 27, 2026. It says the trend took off on TikTok in mid-October, with examples from October 16 to 23 reaching 7 to 12 million views each: creators look plain, pull on a black knit cap and switch to an intense stare on the beat drop.

How to use it

  1. Take two photos or clips of yourself in the same spot: one looking plain and a bit awkward, one with a plain black knit beanie and a serious stare. No beanie? Use the main prompt to make the "after" from your own photo.
  2. Put the plain shot first and the beanie shot second. Cut on the beat drop of a trending sound from the app's library.
  3. Add a short caption like "With or without?" or one of the ideas from the caption prompt.
  4. Keep it under 8 seconds so the switch is the whole video.

Prompts

Main prompt: the "after" shot
Use my uploaded photo. Keep my face, hair colour, skin tone and clothes exactly the same. Add a snug, plain black knit beanie pulled low just above my eyebrows, with no logo or text. Change my expression to a calm, intense, slightly smouldering stare straight into the camera, chin a little down. Keep the same background and room, but make the lighting a bit moodier, with soft shadow on one side of my face. Vertical 9:16 photo, phone-camera look, no filters that change my features.
Variation: the "before" shot
Use my uploaded photo. Keep my face and clothes the same and don't add anything to my head. Make it look like an ordinary, slightly awkward phone snap: flat overhead room light, a small polite half-smile, shoulders a little hunched. Same background as the original. Vertical 9:16. It should look like the boring "before" in a before-and-after.
Variation: caption ideas
I'm posting a short before-and-after video. In the first half I look plain and awkward. In the second half I put on a black beanie and suddenly look intense and confident. Write 10 short on-screen captions for it, under six words each. Mix deadpan, self-aware and a little silly. Include "With or without?" as one option. No song lyrics, no names of real people.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business]. I want to make a playful "one thing changes everything" video: a plain before shot, then one item from my shop added on the beat for a confident after shot. Suggest 6 ideas where the item is something I really sell (for example a hat, scarf, mug, candle, frame or apron). For each, describe the before shot, the after shot and a caption under six words. Keep it honest: the item is the only thing that changes, and no claims about results.

Tips

  • Match the framing in both shots. If your head moves a lot, the switch feels less sharp.
  • The joke is the over-the-top seriousness. Hold the stare for a full second before the video ends.
  • If you use the AI "after" shot, check that it still looks like you and doesn't smooth or reshape your face.
  • Pick the sound from the app's own library so it's cleared for your account.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
