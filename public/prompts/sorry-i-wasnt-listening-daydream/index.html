"Sorry, I wasn't listening": turn your holiday daydream into a clip

Caption "Sorry, I wasn't listening. I was thinking about…" and show the daydream. AI turns a photo into the snowy trip, party or market in your head.

GeminiVeoKlingRunwayChatGPT

Why it's trending

New Engen's Instagram trends list for November 2026, updated November 5, includes "Sorry, I Wasn't Listening": text reads "Sorry, I wasn't listening…" and finishes with a daydream, while a classic holiday song plays and the footage shows the trip, party or decorated street. The page suggests one line of text and 7 to 15 seconds, and says brands can tease holiday events or launches.

How to use it

  1. Pick the daydream: a trip, a holiday party, a snowy street, a cosy night in, or your first day off.
  2. Make a still with the image prompt (use your own photo if you want to be in it), then turn it into a short clip with the video prompt.
  3. Add one line of text: "Sorry, I wasn't listening. I was thinking about ___." You can open on a quick real clip of you looking far away first.
  4. Keep it 7 to 15 seconds. Use the trending song if your account has it, or an upbeat original sound.

Prompts

Image prompt: the daydream
Using my photo, place me in this daydream: [your daydream, for example: a small-town holiday market at dusk, light snow, strings of warm lights, a hot drink in my hands]. Keep my face, hair and build exactly as they are. Make it look like a soft, dreamy film still: warm glow, gentle haze at the edges, shallow depth of field, slightly faded colours. Vertical 9:16. No text, no logos.
Video prompt: bring it to life
Animate this image into a 6-second vertical dream clip. The camera drifts slowly forward. Snow falls softly, the lights twinkle and my breath shows in the cold air. I glance up and smile. Keep my face and clothes the same as the image. Dreamy, warm and calm. No text, no logos, no fast cuts.
Variation: no photo of me
Create a vertical 9:16 dream scene of [your daydream, for example: a cabin window looking out on a snowy lake, a mug of cocoa on the sill, a crackling fire reflected in the glass]. Soft focus, warm light, a gentle haze like a memory. No people, no text, no logos. Then describe a slow 6-second camera move to animate it.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business] in [town]. We have [a real upcoming holiday event, product or opening date]. Write 5 lines that start "Sorry, I wasn't listening. I was thinking about…" and tease it. Then write an image prompt for a dreamy, warm, snowy scene of [your shop, product or event setting] with no people, no text and no logos. Don't invent discounts, numbers or customer quotes.

Tips

  • Make the daydream specific. "The first skate on the outdoor rink" beats "the holidays".
  • A quick real clip of you staring into space, then the dream, makes the joke clearer.
  • If the face drifts in the video, use a wider shot or a scene with no people.
  • Business accounts may not have the trending song. An upbeat original sound works too.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
