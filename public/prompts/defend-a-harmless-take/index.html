Defend a harmless take with total seriousness

Pick a silly, low-stakes opinion and argue for it like a lawyer. AI writes the dead-serious on-screen lines for your lip-sync video.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trends roundup updated October 26, 2026 lists "Two Fishes" among the top trends this week: creators lip-sync a sound from a reality dating show while defending a questionable opinion with complete seriousness, with on-screen text naming the take.

How to use it

  1. Pick a harmless opinion you'll stand behind, like cereal before milk or socks with sandals.
  2. Use the main prompt to get a hook line and three serious-sounding points.
  3. Film yourself with a straight face and the trending sound, and put the lines on screen one by one.
  4. Hold eye contact with the camera at the end. Don't smile.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a short video where I defend a silly, harmless opinion with total seriousness. My opinion: [your take, e.g. pineapple belongs on pizza]. Write one opening on-screen line that states the take, then three short lines that defend it like a confident expert. Each line under 10 words. Deadpan, not mean. No insults to anyone, no hashtags, no emojis.
Variation: give me ideas
Give me 10 harmless, slightly divisive everyday opinions I could defend in a funny video. Think food, chores, weather, routines and fall habits in Canada. Nothing about politics, religion, health, money or real people. Rate each from 1 (mild) to 5 (people will argue in the comments).
Variation: small business
I run [your type of business]. Write a deadpan on-screen script defending one harmless opinion about my trade, e.g. [your take, like "winter tires go on before Halloween"]. One line to state the take, then three short, confident reasons, each under 10 words. Fun and true, not salesy. Don't make claims I can't back up.

Tips

  • The joke works best when the take is tiny and your face is very serious.
  • Let each line stay on screen long enough to read, about two seconds.
  • Keep it light. Pick takes about things, never about people or groups.
  • Ask viewers to share their own harmless take in the comments.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
