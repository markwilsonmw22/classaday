Build the store · Day 13 of 30

Shopify setup: the settings that matter

4 hours

Create your Shopify store and complete every setting that affects payments, shipping, taxes and trust.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 210 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

Today you build the foundations. None of it is glamorous, but a store with these settings done right gets fewer payment holds, fewer support emails and more trust at checkout.

1. Create the store

Sign up for Shopify's free trial with your business email. Use your store name, not your personal name, for the store. Pick the plan when the trial ends; the basic plan is enough until you're doing serious volume. Check current pricing on Shopify's site.

2. Domain

Buy a .com (or .ca if you only sell in Canada) inside Shopify or from a registrar like Cloudflare or Namecheap. Rules:

  • Short, easy to spell, no hyphens
  • Matches your store name
  • Set it as the primary domain so customers never see myshopify.com

3. Store details (Settings → Store details)

Store name, legal business name, address, contact email, phone, store currency (choose the currency you'll mostly sell in; Shopify Markets can show local prices), time zone and unit system.

4. Payments (Settings → Payments)

  • Activate Shopify Payments. Enter business and bank details exactly as registered.
  • Turn on Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Fast checkouts convert better on mobile.
  • Add PayPal Express Checkout and connect your PayPal Business account.
  • Set the statement descriptor to your store name so customers recognise the charge on their bank statement. This alone prevents many chargebacks.

5. Shipping (Settings → Shipping and delivery)

Create shipping zones for each country you sell to. Add your rates from Day 12: free shipping, flat rate or free over a threshold. Name the rate clearly, e.g. "Free tracked shipping (8–14 business days)".

6. Taxes (Settings → Taxes and duties)

Add only the regions where you're registered to collect tax. If you're not registered yet, leave tax collection off and revisit as sales grow (Day 3).

7. Notifications

Settings → Notifications: add your logo (Day 14), check the order confirmation email includes your delivery window, and make sure the shipping confirmation includes the tracking link.

8. Connect your supplier app

Install DSers or CJ from the Shopify App Store and connect your account. You'll import products on Day 16.

2Worked example

Priya's settings after 4 hours:

  • Domain: hearthandhound.com, set as primary.
  • Currency CAD, with Shopify Markets showing USD to US visitors.
  • Shopify Payments active, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay on; PayPal connected; statement descriptor HEARTHANDHOUND.
  • Shipping zones: Canada (free, 8–14 business days), United States (free, 4–8 business days).
  • Taxes: not collecting yet, reminder set.
  • Notifications: order confirmation edited to say "Your order ships in 1–2 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email."
  • CJ connected to the store.
