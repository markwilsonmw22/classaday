"Dear Santa, I'm a size…": the wish list that doesn't come in sizes

Start with "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" and finish with the wish you really want, like a size quiet weekend. AI helps you find the funniest ending.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

Later's November 2026 Reels roundup (entry dated November 20) and NewEngen's Instagram trends page (updated November 20, 2026) both list "Dear Santa, I'm a size…": creators put the line on screen, then finish it with a wish that can't come in a size, set to a soft instrumental Christmas sound.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to brainstorm endings that fit your life.
  2. Film 5 to 10 seconds of yourself, your desk or your space looking hopeful.
  3. Put "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" on screen first, then pop in your ending a beat later.
  4. Add a calm holiday sound from the app's library and ask viewers for their own ending in the comments.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a short holiday clip that starts with the on-screen line "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" and ends with a wish that can't come in a size, like "a size whole weekend with no plans". Here's a bit about me: [job, hobbies, home life, pets]. Give me 15 endings that sound like me. Keep each under eight words, warm and a little funny, and nothing about body size or weight.
Variation: a whole family or friend group
Give me 8 "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" endings for a group clip where each person holds up their own card. We are [who is in it, e.g. two parents, a teenager and a dog]. Give each person one ending that fits them, short enough to read in two seconds. Keep it kind, with no inside jokes that could embarrass anyone.
Variation: holiday card text overlay
Design a simple festive title card that says "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" in large, friendly hand-lettered type on a soft red and cream background with a few small snowflakes. Leave the bottom third empty so I can add my own ending later. No logos, no characters, no other text.
Variation for a small business: our service is the wish
I run [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY, PROVINCE]. Write 8 "Dear Santa, I'm a size…" endings where the wish is something we really offer, like "a size fresh haircut before the party". Keep it honest: no made-up prices, deals, countdowns or customer quotes. Add one short caption inviting people to share their own wish in the comments.

Tips

  • The pause before the ending is the joke. Give the first line a full second on screen.
  • Stay away from clothing or body sizes. The fun is that the wish has no size at all.
  • Business accounts: check the sound is cleared for business use, or use an original sound. The text carries the trend.
  • Pin your favourite comment ending to keep the thread going.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
