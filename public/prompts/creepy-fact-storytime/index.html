Creepy-but-true fact over one slow shot

One slow, moody shot with no cuts, and one unsettling true fact typed on screen. Use AI to find the fact and build the shot.

ChatGPTClaudeVeoKling

Why it's trending

Two TikTok trend reports from mid-October 2026 (October 15 and October 18) list "creepy fact storytime" as rising: an unsettling fact over a slow shot with no cuts, set to a quiet eerie sound. Halloween is October 31.

How to use it

  1. Ask a text AI for a few creepy-but-true facts on a topic you like, then check the one you pick on a trusted site.
  2. Film one slow shot yourself (a dark hallway, a foggy field, the bottom of a lake), or make one with an AI video tool using the shot prompt.
  3. Add the fact as on-screen text in two or three short lines. Keep the shot going with no cuts.
  4. Add a quiet, eerie sound from the app's library and post it before Halloween.

Prompts

Main prompt: the fact
Give me 5 creepy but true facts about [topic, for example: the deep ocean, the human body, old houses, or Canadian winters]. Each one must be well known and easy to check, with no gore. Write each as two short lines that work as on-screen text: line 1 sets it up, line 2 is the chilling part. After each fact, name the kind of source I could check it with, like an encyclopedia or a science museum website. Don't include anything you are unsure of.
Shot prompt: AI video
A single slow, steady camera push forward down [scene, for example: an empty wooden hallway lit only by a window at the far end]. Late evening, cool blue light, light dust in the air. Nothing jumps out and nothing moves except the camera. Vertical 9:16, about 8 seconds, no cuts, no people, no text.
Variation: cosy, not scary
Give me 5 strange but true facts about [topic, for example: owls, the moon, or autumn leaves] that feel a little eerie but are fine for kids. Write each as two short on-screen lines. Name the kind of source I could check it with. Nothing gory or frightening for young children.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: an old bookshop in a heritage building]. Give me 3 eerie but true facts about [something real tied to my business, for example: the history of paper, or old buildings like mine], written as two short on-screen lines each. End with a soft caption that invites people in for Halloween week. No made-up history, no ghost claims about my building, no prices.

Tips

  • Check every fact before you post. AI can sound sure and still be wrong.
  • Slow is the point. A steady push or a still shot works better than fast edits.
  • Keep the text on screen long enough to read twice.
  • Leave a pause before the chilling line so it lands.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
