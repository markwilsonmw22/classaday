Build the store · Day 16 of 30

The product page that sells

4.5 hours

Import your products and build a product page that answers every question and objection a buyer has.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 240 min≈ 4.5 h total

1Lesson

Your product page does the selling. Ads bring people here; the page decides whether they buy. A great page can double your conversion rate with the same ads.

Import the product

Use DSers or CJ to import the product into Shopify. Then rewrite everything: supplier titles and descriptions are badly written and identical across hundreds of stores.

The title

Benefit first, then product: "Self-Cleaning Deshedding Brush, Removes up to 90% of Loose Undercoat" only if you've tested that claim. Otherwise: "Self-Cleaning Deshedding Brush for Double-Coated Dogs". Keep it under 70 characters.

Images and video

  • First image: product in use, not on white.
  • 5–8 images total: in use, close-up detail, size in hand, before/after (only real), what's in the box.
  • One short GIF or video of the key moment (hair sliding off at the button press).
  • Square or 4:5, same background style, compressed under 300 KB each.

Copy formula: problem → solution → proof

  1. Problem: one or two sentences about the frustration, in the customer's words (from your 3-star review research).
  2. Solution: how the product fixes it, in plain language.
  3. Three key benefits as short bullet points. Benefits, not features: "Hair slides off with one click" rather than "Retractable pins".
  4. How it works: 3 simple steps with small images.
  5. Proof: real reviews, your own tests, photos from your sample.
  6. What's included and specs: size, material, weight.
  7. FAQ: shipping time, which dogs it suits, cleaning, returns.

Rules you must not break

  • No fake reviews or review-import apps that copy someone else's reviews. Collect real ones from your first customers (Day 27).
  • No fake countdown timers or "only 3 left" when that isn't true.
  • No medical or health claims.
  • A compare-at price only if the product genuinely sold at that price or a fair market comparison supports it.

Platforms and consumer protection agencies take action against these practices, and they destroy trust.

2Worked example

Priya's slicker brush page (excerpt):

Self-Cleaning Deshedding Brush for Double-Coated Dogs

Shedding season shouldn't mean vacuuming twice a day. Our brush lifts loose undercoat in minutes, and one click pushes the fur straight off the pins, so the hair goes in the bin instead of on your sofa.

  • One click releases all the fur, no picking hair out by hand
  • Fine stainless pins with rounded tips, gentle on skin
  • Non-slip handle that doesn't tire your wrist

How it works: 1. Brush in the direction of the coat. 2. Press the button. 3. Wipe the fur away.

Delivery: free tracked shipping, 4–8 business days (US), 8–14 (Canada). 30-day guarantee.

She tested the "rounded tips" claim on her sample before writing it.
