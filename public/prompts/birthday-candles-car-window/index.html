Drive-by birthday: one big breath out the car window

A four-second clip: you lean out of the driver's window and blow out every candle on a birthday cake in one big breath.

KlingVeoHiggsfieldRunway

Why it's trending

Jump on the Trend's weekly page (updated November 9, 2026) lists "Birthday Car" as a format that went live on November 7, with about 1.7 million views. It's a one-person, four-second clip: leaning out of a car window to blow out all the candles at once.

How to use it

  1. Pick a clear photo of the birthday person, face visible, ideally sitting in a car or looking to the side.
  2. Upload it to an image-to-video tool and paste the main prompt. Ask for a vertical clip, 4 to 5 seconds.
  3. Check the ending: every flame should go out in one breath. If some stay lit, run it again.
  4. Post it on the birthday with a short caption, like "Another year, one breath."

Prompts

Main prompt: one breath, all the candles
Use the person in this photo. They sit in the driver's seat of a parked car on a quiet street at golden hour. A friend outside, only their hands in view, holds up a birthday cake covered in lit candles at the open window. The person leans out of the window, takes a big breath, and blows out every candle in one go. Smoke curls up, they grin and throw both hands up. Handheld phone camera, warm light, natural colours, one smooth shot, 4 seconds, vertical. Keep their face and hair the same as the photo.
Variation: Canadian winter drive-by
Use the person in this photo. Snowy evening on a quiet street, warm porch lights, snowflakes falling. They sit in the driver's seat wearing a toque and scarf, window rolled down. A friend in mittens holds up a small cake with lit candles. The person leans out, blows out all the candles in one big puff, and the breath shows as a little cloud in the cold air. They laugh and wave. Handheld phone camera, cosy and bright, one shot, 5 seconds, vertical. Keep their face the same as the photo.
Variation: your pet's birthday
Use the pet in this photo. The pet sits in the driver's seat of a parked car, front paws on the steering wheel, wearing a tiny paper party hat. A hand holds a small pet-friendly cake with two candles at the open window. The pet leans out, gives a big puff, and both candles go out. The pet's ears flop and it looks very proud. Bright afternoon light, playful and cute, one shot, 4 seconds, vertical. Keep the pet's markings the same as the photo.
Variation: small business drive-thru birthday
Make a 5-second vertical video for a small bakery or café drive-thru window. A smiling staff member in a plain apron, no logos, holds a cupcake with one lit candle out to a customer in a car. The customer leans out of the driver's window and blows it out. Both of them cheer. Warm afternoon light, friendly and simple, one shot. Leave the top third clear so I can add text like "Birthday treat on us this week".

Tips

  • Only make a clip of someone else if they agree. Birthday surprises are fun, but ask first.
  • Shorter is better here. The whole joke is one breath, so 4 to 5 seconds is plenty.
  • If the cake looks odd, add "a simple round cake with white icing" to the prompt.
  • Only post a business offer you really run, and say when it ends.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
