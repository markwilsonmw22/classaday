Your own AI micro-drama series

Plan a short drama in numbered episodes with the same characters, a quick recap and a cliffhanger, then make each episode with AI video.

KlingVeoRunwayHiggsfieldChatGPT

Why it's trending

An Instagram trend report covering October 10–17, 2026 lists serialized AI micro-dramas: numbered episodes with recaps, recurring characters and cliffhangers. A TechNode article from October 20, 2026 also reports fast growth in AI-generated short drama series. This pack builds on our 30-second three-shot story pack by turning one clip into a series.

How to use it

  1. Use the series prompt to plan your characters, the setting and five short episodes.
  2. Make one reference image for each character and reuse it in every episode so they stay the same.
  3. Make each episode with the shot prompt: a two-second recap, the new scene, then the cliffhanger.
  4. Add "Episode 1", "Episode 2" and so on as on-screen text in your editor, and post them in order.

Prompts

Step 1: plan the series
Help me plan a short vertical drama series for Reels and TikTok. Genre: [for example: cozy mystery in a small Canadian town]. Give me: 2 or 3 original characters, each with a name, age range, look, outfit and one personality trait; one main setting; and 5 episodes of about 30 seconds each. For each episode write a one-line recap of the last episode, three shots (wide, close-up, payoff) and a cliffhanger line that makes people want the next one. Keep it family-friendly. No real people, brands or existing characters.
Step 2: character reference image
Make a full-body character reference image of [paste one character's description from your plan]. Plain light-grey background, soft even light, standing front-on, neutral expression. Natural, realistic film look. Vertical 9:16. No text.
Step 3: one episode shot
Use the attached character image as the reference and keep the face, hair and outfit exactly the same. Scene: [paste one shot from your plan, for example: she opens a frosty mailbox at dusk and finds an old brass key with a paper tag]. Slow push-in, warm porch light against blue evening snow, natural movement. End on her surprised face looking just past the camera. Cinematic, vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: bakery]. Plan a light, funny 4-episode micro-drama set in my shop, starring two original characters: a staff member and a regular customer. Each episode is about 30 seconds, has a one-line recap, three shots and a playful cliffhanger. Show the shop and products naturally, without prices, claims or sales pitches. No real people or other businesses.

Tips

  • Keep each episode short and end on a question. People follow a series for the cliffhanger.
  • Reuse the same character images and the same setting words in every prompt. That keeps the look steady.
  • Add episode numbers and recap text in your editor, not in the AI prompt. AI video text often comes out garbled.
  • Label it as AI-made in your caption or with the platform's AI label.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
