"Lately on my SD card": a fast dump of your week, with a memory-card title

Make a memory-card title graphic, then follow it with 8 to 15 of your recent clips and photos, each on screen for under a second.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

New Engen's Instagram trends page, updated November 13, 2026, lists "Lately on my SD card": a Reel opens on a memory-card graphic with those words, then runs a quick burst of recent clips and photos. It says photographers, groomers, drone operators and designers have made their own, since it shows recent work without filming anything new.

How to use it

  1. Pick 8 to 15 recent photos or clips: work in progress, behind the scenes, small details, one favourite shot to end on.
  2. Ask ChatGPT or Gemini for the title card with the main prompt.
  3. In your video app, put the title card first for about one second, then each clip or photo for under a second, ending on your favourite.
  4. Use original sound or an upbeat track from the app's library, and post.

Prompts

Main prompt: the title card
Create a vertical 9:16 title card for a short video. In the middle, a close-up of a plain memory card with no brand name or logo, sitting on a [surface, for example: wooden desk next to a camera strap]. Soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, slightly warm colours. Above the card, in clean lowercase white letters with a soft shadow, write: "lately on my sd card". No other text, no logos.
Variation: your own theme
Make the same title card again, but set the memory card on [a surface that fits your life, for example: a snowy porch railing / a kitchen counter with flour / a car dashboard at sunset]. Keep the same lowercase white words "lately on my sd card" in the same spot. Keep it simple and clean. No brand names, no logos.
Variation: put the clips in order
Here is a list of my recent clips and photos: [paste short descriptions, for example: 1. morning coffee, 2. client shoot setup, 3. dog in the snow...]. Put them in an order that feels fast and fun for a 10-second Reel. Start strong, mix close-ups with wide shots, and end on the one that makes people want to see more. Give me the order and one short caption under 10 words.
Variation: small business
Create a vertical 9:16 title card. A plain memory card with no brand name rests beside [our work tools, for example: grooming scissors and a brush / a sketchbook and pencils]. Bright, clean light. In lowercase white letters at the top: "lately on my sd card". In small letters at the bottom: "[business type] · [your town]". No logos and no other text.

Tips

  • Keep every clip under one second. Fast is the whole point.
  • End on your best shot so it's the one people remember.
  • Use only your own real photos and clips after the title card, so it shows your actual work.
  • Ask before posting clips with clients or friends in them.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
