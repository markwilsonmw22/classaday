Refund Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Every Class a Day membership comes with a {{DAYS}}-day money-back guarantee. If Class a Day isn't right for you, you get your money back. You don't have to give a reason.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account, within {{DAYS}} days of your payment.
  2. Tell us which plan you bought (monthly or lifetime). That's all we need.
  3. We'll refund the full amount, including sales tax, to the card or account you paid with, as soon as we can (usually within a few business days). Your bank may take another 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What the guarantee covers

  • Lifetime ({{LIFETIME}}): the full payment, if you ask within {{DAYS}} days of buying.
  • Monthly ({{MONTHLY}} a month): your first payment, if you ask within {{DAYS}} days of joining. After that, you can cancel any time from your account page so you aren't charged again. We don't refund part-months.
  • Switching from monthly to lifetime: the lifetime payment is covered by the same {{DAYS}}-day guarantee.
  • If you were charged by mistake, for example twice, we'll always refund the extra charge, whenever you notice it.

After a refund

Your membership ends when the refund is made, and members-only lessons, downloads and prompt packs lock again. Your account and progress stay, so you can pick up where you left off if you join again later.

Please talk to us first

If something's wrong, email us before disputing the charge with your bank. We answer quickly, and a refund from us is faster for you than a bank dispute.

This policy adds to your rights under consumer law where you live; it doesn't replace them. See also our Terms of Service.
