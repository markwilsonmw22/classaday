Operations & scaling · Day 26 of 30

Order fulfilment and your daily routine

3 hours

Set up a reliable way to fulfil orders, sync tracking and run a 30-minute daily routine.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 150 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

When orders start coming in, consistency becomes everything. A missed order or a missing tracking number turns a happy customer into a refund request.

How fulfilment works with your supplier app

  1. A customer orders on Shopify.
  2. The order appears in DSers or CJ.
  3. You check it and pay the supplier for it (manually or with auto-pay).
  4. The supplier ships and adds a tracking number.
  5. The app syncs tracking back to Shopify, which emails the customer automatically.

Set it up properly

  • Map products in the app so each Shopify variant links to the right supplier variant.
  • Add order notes for the supplier: "No invoice or price in parcel. Ship in box, not bag."
  • Choose the shipping method per country to match your promises.
  • Turn on automatic tracking sync.
  • Keep your supplier account funded (or a card on file) so orders don't sit unpaid.

The 30-minute daily routine

Do this at the same time every day:

  1. Orders (10 min): fulfil all new orders, check any that are unpaid or stuck.
  2. Tracking (5 min): check orders older than 5 days without tracking; message the supplier.
  3. Customers (10 min): answer every email and comment (Day 27).
  4. Numbers (5 min): update your profit sheet (Day 29) and test log.

Problem orders

  • No tracking after 5 days: message the supplier.
  • Tracking stuck for 10+ days: reship or refund, then claim from the supplier.
  • Wrong address: contact the supplier immediately to change it; if shipped, contact the customer.

When to automate more

At 20+ orders a day, consider auto-fulfilment, an agent who fulfils from stock, and a tracking page app with branded updates.

2Worked example

Priya's first week of orders:

  • Day 1 of ads: 3 orders. She fulfils them in CJ by 9pm. Tracking arrives two days later and syncs to Shopify.
  • One customer typed "Mississauga" as the province. She emails the customer, fixes the address before the supplier ships, and adds a note to always check addresses.
  • Her daily routine runs 8:30–9:00pm, after dinner, before checking ads.

★Tip

Pay for orders the same day they arrive. Every day an order sits unfulfilled adds a day to delivery, and delivery time is the number one cause of complaints.
