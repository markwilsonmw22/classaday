1960s witchcore portrait

Turn your selfie into a 1960s-style witch portrait: a cosy parlour, a bubbling cauldron, a rotary phone and a black cat, shot like an old colour film still.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

An AI trend list for TikTok updated October 17, 2026 names "Witchcore": one photo turned into a 1960s witch scene with a rotary phone, cauldron and black cat. A fashion report from October 12, 2026 also calls witchcore a fall 2026 runway trend.

How to use it

  1. Pick a clear, front-facing selfie with soft light and your face fully visible.
  2. Upload it to an AI image tool and paste the main prompt. Ask for a vertical 4:5 image.
  3. If your face changes, say "keep my face exactly the same as the photo" and try again.
  4. Post it as a single image, or make three versions and share them as a carousel.

Prompts

Main prompt
Use my photo and keep my face, skin tone and features exactly the same. Show me as a stylish 1960s witch in a cosy, cluttered parlour. I wear a fitted black dress with a lace collar and a small pointed hat, with neat, glossy period hair. I sit in a velvet armchair holding a cream rotary phone to my ear, with a knowing smile. A small black cat sits on the arm of the chair. Behind me, a copper cauldron bubbles with soft green steam on a stone hearth, next to shelves of glass jars, dried herbs and candles. Warm lamp light, rich jewel colours, soft film grain and gentle colour fading, like a still from an old 1960s colour TV show. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: friends or couple
Use the two people in my photo and keep both faces exactly the same. Show them as a pair of 1960s witches in a tidy suburban kitchen with pastel cupboards. One stirs a small cauldron on the stove with a wooden spoon. The other reads from a thick old spell book with a cup of tea. A black cat watches from the counter. Bright, even studio lighting, saturated colours and light film grain, like an old colour sitcom still. Playful and charming, not scary. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: small business
Use my photo and keep my face exactly the same. Show me as a friendly 1960s witch behind the counter of a small shop that looks like mine: a [type of shop, for example: bakery]. Glass jars, a brass bell on the counter and a small cauldron with soft purple steam beside the till. A black cat naps on a stack of paper bags. Warm, rich colours and soft film grain, like an old colour film still. Leave the area above my head plain so I can add my shop name as a caption. Vertical 4:5.

Tips

  • Ask for "film grain" and "faded colour" to get the old-TV look. Without them it can look too glossy.
  • Only use your own photo, or a photo of friends who have said yes.
  • Keep it charming: the 1960s look works best playful, not scary.
  • Add your shop name as a caption in your editor. AI text inside the picture often comes out wrong.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
