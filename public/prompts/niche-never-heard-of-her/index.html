Niche? Never heard of her

Open with "Niche? Never heard of her", then rush through all your interests with a quick photo dump for each. AI helps you name and order them.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

An Instagram Reels trends roundup updated October 23, 2026 lists a new "refuse to be limited by a single niche" format: creators open with a line like "Niche? Never heard of her", then show each of their interests with a rapid photo dump.

How to use it

  1. List the interests you actually post about, or wish you did. Five or six is plenty.
  2. Use the main prompt to get short, punchy labels and a running order.
  3. Gather three to five photos or clips for each interest from your camera roll.
  4. Edit it as label, quick photos, next label, and finish on your favourite one.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a Reel that opens with "Niche? Never heard of her". My interests are: [list five or six]. For each one, write an on-screen label of 2 to 4 words that sounds fun, like a title card. Then suggest an order that starts strong, mixes up very different interests, and ends on the most surprising one. Also give me 3 short closing lines for the last frame. No hashtags.
Variation: camera roll helper
Here's a list of what's in my camera roll from the last few months: [describe the kinds of photos you have]. Group them into 5 or 6 interests for a "Niche? Never heard of her" Reel, give each group a 2 to 4 word label, and tell me which kinds of photos to use for each one.
Variation: small business
I run [your type of business] and we do more than one thing: [list your products, services or side projects]. Write a "Niche? Never heard of her" Reel plan with a 2 to 4 word label for each, in a good running order, plus one friendly closing line that tells people what to check out first. Don't make up prices, reviews or results.

Tips

  • Keep each photo on screen for a split second. Fast is the point.
  • Big contrast between interests is funnier, like pottery then hockey stats.
  • Use your own photos. Real moments beat AI images here.
  • Put the label on the first photo of each group so people can follow.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
