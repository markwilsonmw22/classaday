Creating content: design and short video

4 hours

Make professional carousels, graphics and short videos with Canva, CapCut and a phone, and learn to turn one shoot into a week of content.

Lesson 35 minExample 20 minTasks 185 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

You don't need a studio. A recent phone, natural light, Canva and CapCut produce most of the content you see from successful small businesses.

Canva for graphics and carousels

Set up once per client:

  • Brand Kit (Canva Pro): logo, colours, fonts. Every design starts on-brand.
  • Templates: create 5–8 reusable templates (quote, tip carousel, review card, offer, announcement).
  • Magic Resize (Pro): turn one design into feed, Story and Pinterest sizes in one click.
  • Background remover and Magic Studio AI tools for quick edits.

Sizes to remember:

Use Size (px)
Instagram feed / carousel 1080 × 1350 (4:5 portrait). The profile grid shows a taller 3:4 crop, so keep key text away from the top and bottom edges
Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
Facebook feed 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080
LinkedIn document carousel 1080 × 1350 PDF
Pinterest pin 1000 × 1500
YouTube thumbnail 1280 × 720

Carousel structure (saves and shares magnet):

  1. Slide 1, the hook: a bold promise or question, big text. ("5 things that make your oat latte taste burnt")
  2. Slides 2–7, the value: one idea per slide, short sentences, consistent layout.
  3. Last slide, the action: save, share, comment, visit, book.

Phone video basics

  • Film vertical, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps; clean the lens.
  • Face a window for soft light; avoid overhead lights that cast shadows.
  • Sound matters more than picture: use a cheap clip-on wireless microphone.
  • Shoot B-roll (close-ups of hands, steam, tools, before/after) for every video.
  • Hold each shot 3–5 seconds; record more than you think you need.

The short video structure

Time Job Example (plumber)
0–2 s Hook: visual + on-screen text that stops the scroll Water spraying from a pipe: "Do this BEFORE you call a plumber"
2–20 s Value: show, don't just tell Finding the main shut-off valve, turning it
20–30 s Payoff + CTA Water stops. "Then call us. Same-day, fixed price."

Editing in CapCut

  1. Cut every pause and "um". Keep it moving.
  2. Add auto-captions, then correct mistakes (most people watch on mute).
  3. Put a text hook on screen in the first frame.
  4. Use the platform's commercial music library for business accounts; trending songs from the personal library aren't licensed for brand use.
  5. Export 1080 × 1920.

Read the licence terms of any editing app, template or music you use for client work.

Repurposing: one shoot, a week of content

From one 10-minute talking-head video, you can make:

  • 3–5 short clips (Reels, TikTok, Shorts). Tools like Opus Clip find the best moments automatically; always check them.
  • 1 carousel summarising the key points
  • 1 LinkedIn post or blog article from the transcript
  • 1 email newsletter section
  • 3 Stories with polls or questions

AI for creative work

Use AI for idea lists, scripts, caption drafts, background removal and resizing. Be careful with AI images of food, products, work or people that look like real photos of the business: customers expect real photos, and misleading visuals damage trust. Never use AI to imitate a real person's face or voice without written consent.

2Worked example

Northfield's shoot day (3 hours).

Riya and tech Jake film at a real job (with the homeowner's permission) and in the van:

  • 6 "fix it or call us?" Shorts (shut-off valve, running toilet, frozen pipe prevention, water heater pilot light, sump pump test, garbage disposal reset)
  • 1 eight-minute YouTube video: "Water heater about to fail? 5 warning signs"
  • 40 photos: team, van, tools, before/after

From that shoot she produces: 6 Shorts, 4 extra clips from the long video, 2 carousels ("5 warning signs", "What to do when a pipe bursts"), 1 blog post from the transcript, 4 Google Business Profile posts and a month of Facebook posts. Everything uses three Canva templates in Northfield's blue and orange.
