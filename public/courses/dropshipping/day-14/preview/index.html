Build the store · Day 14 of 30

Brand identity on a budget

Members
3 hours

Create a simple, consistent brand: name, logo, colours, fonts and voice, so the store looks trustworthy from the first second.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 150 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

You don't need a designer. You need consistency. A store that uses the same two colours, one or two fonts and a clear voice everywhere looks professional. One that mixes ten styles looks like a scam.

If you haven't finalised the name: it should be easy to say out loud, hint at the niche, and have the .com and social handles available. Check Instagram and TikTok handles today.
