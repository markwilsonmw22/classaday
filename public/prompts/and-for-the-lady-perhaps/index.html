"And for the lady, perhaps…": the tiny treat that's made for someone

Write "And for the [person], perhaps…" over a slow, cozy clip of their perfect little treat. AI writes the lines so friends tag each other.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

New Engen's Instagram trends list for November 2026, updated November 5, includes "And For the Lady, Perhaps": an overlay reads "And for the [person], perhaps…" over footage of their ideal small luxury, often set to a classic holiday standard. The page says viewers tag the friend who fits the line, and suggests cafes, shops, spas and travel brands use it as a soft gift guide.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to get a list of "And for the…, perhaps…" lines for different kinds of people.
  2. Pick one and film the treat slowly: the drink being poured, the quiet walk, the blanket on the couch.
  3. Put the line on screen at the start and let the clip play for 7 to 12 seconds.
  4. Make a few for different people and post them as a small series.

Prompts

Main prompt
Help me with a short Instagram Reel. The on-screen text will say "And for the [type of person], perhaps…" and then name their perfect small treat, while the video shows that treat slowly and cozily. Write 12 of these lines. Each one names a type of person (for example: the homebody, the early riser, the group-chat planner, the one who's always cold) and one very specific, affordable treat they'd love. Keep each line under 18 words. Example of the tone: "And for the homebody, perhaps… a fresh blanket, a new candle and absolutely no plans." Add a one-line shot idea under each. No hashtags, no emojis, nothing mean.
Variation: Canadian winter edition
Write 10 "And for the…, perhaps…" lines with a Canadian late-fall and winter feel: first snow, toques, hot chocolate after shovelling, a cabin weekend, warm boots by the door, the first skate of the year. Each line names a type of person and one cozy, everyday treat. Keep them under 18 words, warm and a little funny. Don't mention any real person, brand or company.
Variation: for my friends and family
I want to make a series of these for people in my life who are happy to be tagged. Here are a few of them and what they love: [for example: my sister, who loves quiet mornings and bookstores; my dad, who loves fixing things in the garage]. Write one "And for the…, perhaps…" line for each, using a fun nickname-style description instead of their real name, plus a simple shot I can film at home.
Variation: small business gift guide
I run [type of business] in [town]. Write 8 "And for the…, perhaps…" lines that match real customers to real things we sell or do. Here's what we actually offer: [list your products or services]. Each line names a type of person (the coffee snob, the last-minute shopper, the one who has everything) and one item from my list. Only use items and prices I gave you. Don't invent sales, stock levels, reviews or customer quotes. Add a slow, cozy shot idea for each.

Tips

  • Be specific. "A warm cinnamon bun and a window seat" beats "something nice".
  • Film slow and close. Pouring, steam and soft light do most of the work.
  • Business accounts may not have the classic holiday songs. Check the app or use an original sound.
  • Make several versions for different people. Each one gets tagged by a different crowd.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
