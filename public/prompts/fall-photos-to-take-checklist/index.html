Fall photos to remember to take

Let AI write your own list of small fall moments worth photographing, then post it as a checklist carousel or tick it off as a photo dump.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A weekly Instagram trends report covering October 10 to 17, 2026 lists "pictures to remember to take this fall" photo dumps among the formats doing well. This pack helps you make a list that fits your own life and town.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to get a list of fall photo ideas that fit where you live.
  2. Pick your favourite eight to ten and save them as a checklist on your phone.
  3. Take the photos over the next week or two, in natural light where you can.
  4. Post them as a carousel with the list on the first slide, or one idea per slide.

Prompts

Main prompt
Write a list of 15 small fall moments worth taking a photo of. I live in [town or region, for example: a small town in Ontario] and I like [two or three interests, for example: walks, baking, my dog]. Keep each idea under ten words, make them specific and simple (for example: steam rising off your first hot cider), and include a few that happen indoors on rainy days. No brand names.
Variation: shot tips
For each idea on this list, give me one short phone photo tip: where to stand, what light to look for, or what to put in the frame. Keep each tip to one sentence. List: [paste your list].
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business] in [town]. Write a list of 10 fall photos I should take for our social media this month: behind the scenes, our space, our products and our neighbourhood. Keep each idea under twelve words and add a one-line caption idea for each. Don't invent customers, quotes or numbers.

Tips

  • Fall light is softest in the late afternoon. Plan two or three outdoor shots around that.
  • Use the same edit on every photo so the carousel looks like a set.
  • Put the checklist on the first slide so people save the post.
  • Only include people in your photos if they're happy to be posted.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
