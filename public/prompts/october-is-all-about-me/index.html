October is all about me

A main-character text overlay listing the small ways this month is all about you. AI helps you write lines that sound like you.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trends roundup dated October 16, 2026 lists "October is all about me": creators use a seasonal sound with on-screen text that frames the month as their main-character moment.

How to use it

  1. Film a few seconds of something you love about October: a walk, a warm drink, a cosy corner.
  2. Use the main prompt to get a short list of lines in your own voice.
  3. Pick three or four lines and add them as text on screen, one at a time.
  4. Choose a trending seasonal sound in the app and post.

Prompts

Main prompt
Write 8 short lines for an on-screen text video called "October is all about me". Each line is under 8 words and starts with "October is" or "This month". Make them about small, happy things: cosy mornings, long walks, saying no to plans, treating myself. Here are three things I love about this time of year: [your three things]. Keep it light and confident, not bragging. No hashtags.
Variation: Canadian fall
Write 8 short on-screen text lines for "October is all about me", set in a Canadian fall. Think Thanksgiving leftovers, the first toque of the year, frosty windshields, hot drinks and crunchy leaves. Each line under 8 words, playful and warm. No hashtags.
Variation: small business
I run [your type of business]. Write 6 short on-screen text lines for an "October is all about me" video, written as if my business is talking. Make it about small treats for my customers this month, like [your offer or fall product]. Each line under 8 words. Friendly and fun. Don't make up prices, reviews or deadlines.

Tips

  • Give the AI your own details. Generic lines look like everyone else's.
  • Three or four lines is plenty. Each should stay on screen long enough to read.
  • Match each line to a clip, like your drink for a coffee line.
  • Read the list out loud and cut any line you wouldn't really say.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
