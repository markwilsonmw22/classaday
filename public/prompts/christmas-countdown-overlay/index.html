The Christmas countdown: "6 Fridays left" over your cosiest clip

Count down to Christmas in fun units, like Fridays or snowfalls, over cosy everyday clips. AI writes the countdown lines and plans a weekly series.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

Startups.co.uk's list of social media trends for November 2026, published November 2, includes the Christmas countdown: overlays such as "99 days until Christmas" on cosy clips, with milestone updates like "10 Fridays left." Our earlier holiday packs cover decorating and booking; this one is about the countdown itself.

How to use it

  1. Check today's date and work out your real number. From Friday, November 6, 2026, there are 49 days and 6 more Fridays before Christmas Day.
  2. Use the main prompt to turn that number into fun units and lines.
  3. Film something cosy: a mug by the window, the first snow, a pet under a blanket. Put the countdown line on screen.
  4. Post an update at each milestone with the same text style, so it reads as a series.

Prompts

Main prompt
Today is [date] and Christmas is [number] days away. Turn that into 10 fun countdown lines for short videos, using different units: Fridays left, weekends left, paydays, hot chocolates, snowfalls, Sunday dinners, episodes of my favourite show. Do the maths for each one and show your working in brackets so I can check it. About me: [your routines, where you live in Canada]. Keep each line under 8 words. No emojis.
Variation: weekly series plan
Plan a weekly Christmas countdown series from [today's date] to December 24. For each week give: the countdown line (for example "5 Fridays left"), one cosy clip idea I can film at home or outside in [town], and a short caption. Make each week feel a little more festive than the last, starting with late-fall vibes and ending with full holiday mode.
Variation: countdown card image
Make a square image of a cosy countdown card. A small chalkboard sign leans on a frosty windowsill with a steaming mug and a sprig of evergreen. The sign reads "[number] Fridays left" in neat hand-lettering. Soft morning light, gentle snow outside, warm and calm colours. Leave clear space at the top for a caption.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business] in [town]. Write 6 countdown lines for our account that tie the real date to things we're actually doing, like [for example: the holiday menu starts, the last day we can ship before Christmas, our real holiday hours]. Use only dates I give you. Don't invent deadlines, "last chance" pressure, discounts or limited stock.

Tips

  • Check the maths. People love to correct a wrong countdown in the comments.
  • Pick one text style and colour and keep it for every update, so followers spot the series.
  • Odd units are more fun than days. "7 more snow shovellings" gets a smile.
  • Businesses: count down to real dates only, like real shipping cut-offs or holiday hours. Never fake urgency.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
