Classroom Calendar About

About this course

A 30-day path from "what's a funnel?" to running a real agency. You'll learn SEO, web scraping and lead generation, social media marketing and management, paid ads, analytics and email. Then you'll package it all into services, win clients and deliver work they renew.

What you'll be able to do

  • Read marketing numbers like a pro: CPM, CTR, CPA, ROAS, CAC and lifetime value
  • Run keyword research, on-page, technical and local SEO, and write a full SEO audit
  • Get businesses found in Google's map pack and cited in AI search answers
  • Scrape websites safely with no-code tools and Python, and build clean lead lists
  • Plan, create, schedule and manage social media for clients on every major platform
  • Write hooks, captions and ads that people actually stop for
  • Launch and manage Meta, TikTok and Google Ads campaigns for clients
  • Set up GA4, Tag Manager, UTMs and a Looker Studio dashboard clients understand
  • Build email flows that sell, and stay on the right side of Canada's anti-spam law
  • Find clients, run sales calls, write proposals, price retainers and report results

Who it's for

  • Beginners who want a skill that pays, and a business they can start from a laptop
  • Freelancers who do one thing (design, social, web) and want to offer more
  • Small business owners who want to market their own business properly
  • Anyone who finished the Website Builders guide and wants to sell marketing too

What you'll need

  • A laptop. A phone that films in 1080p for the social media module
  • A free Google account (Search Console, Analytics, Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Business Profile)
  • Free tiers of Canva, CapCut, Buffer or Meta Business Suite, and HubSpot CRM
  • Optional: $50–$150 for a first ad test, and Python (free) for Day 13
