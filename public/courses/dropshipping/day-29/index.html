Operations & scaling · Day 29 of 30

Money: profit tracking, cash flow and taxes

3.5 hours

Build a daily profit dashboard so you always know whether the business is actually making money.

Lesson 30 minExample 20 minTasks 160 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Lots of stores show big revenue screenshots while losing money. Your profit sheet keeps you honest.

The daily profit sheet

One row per day with these columns:

Column Where it comes from
Revenue Shopify (total sales after discounts)
Orders Shopify
Product + shipping cost Supplier app (sum of order costs)
Payment fees Shopify/PayPal fees (or estimate %)
Ad spend Ads Manager (all platforms)
Refunds Shopify
Apps + fixed costs Monthly total ÷ days in month
Profit Revenue − all costs
Profit margin Profit ÷ revenue
Blended ROAS Revenue ÷ ad spend
AOV Revenue ÷ orders

Blended ROAS (all revenue divided by all ad spend) matters more than the ROAS shown per ad, because it includes email sales and returning customers.

Weekly review (Sunday, 30 minutes)

  • Total profit and margin for the week
  • Best and worst products and ads
  • Refund rate (aim for under 5%)
  • One thing to improve next week

Cash flow

Keep a simple forecast of money in (payouts) and money out (ads, suppliers, apps) for the next 14 days. If it goes negative, slow scaling until it doesn't.

Taxes and paying yourself

  • Move 25–30% of profit to your tax savings account monthly (Day 3).
  • Track sales by region for sales tax thresholds.
  • Pay yourself a fixed amount once profit is consistent for three months, not before.
  • Have an accountant review your books before your first tax return.

Priya's week 3 summary:

Week total
