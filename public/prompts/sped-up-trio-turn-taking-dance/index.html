The sped-up trio dance: three of you in a row, taking turns up front

Turn a photo of you and two friends or siblings into the quick trio dance where you spin, swap places and take turns in front of the camera.

KlingVeoRunwayHiggsfield

Why it's trending

New Engen's TikTok trends page, updated November 13, 2026, lists a trio dance to a sped-up, high-pitched '80s pop sound among this week's top trends, with some people using templates to animate a photo. A November 1 explainer traced it to mid-October, with several trio versions passing millions of plays.

How to use it

  1. Pick a clear, well-lit photo of three people standing side by side, full body, with space around them. Only use people who have agreed to it.
  2. Upload it to an image-to-video tool and paste the main prompt. Set the clip to vertical and about 10 to 15 seconds.
  3. Check that faces and outfits stay the same the whole way through. If someone changes, try again with a shorter, simpler set of moves.
  4. Add the trending trio-dance sound from inside your app and line up the first spin with the beat.

Prompts

Main prompt: the trio dance
Animate this photo of three people standing in a row on a backyard court at golden hour. The phone camera stays fixed at chest height, one continuous take. They do a quick, upbeat, slightly goofy routine: all three bounce, twirl one finger in the air and spin together. Then they take turns stepping to the front: the left person moves to the middle, the right person runs up close to the lens and spins, then steps back. Each one gets a hair flip or a little shoulder bounce when they're in front. Big smiles, playful energy. Keep everyone's face, body and clothes exactly as in the photo. Vertical 9:16, about 12 seconds, natural light, no text.
Variation: matching colours
Use this photo of three people. Dress each one in a cosy matching hoodie in a different colour I pick: [colour 1], [colour 2], [colour 3]. Keep their faces and hair exactly the same. Set them in a living room cleared for dancing, with soft holiday lights in the background. Fixed camera, one take. They do a fast, playful trio routine: bounce, spin, swap places, and each one takes a turn up close to the camera with a hair flip. Vertical 9:16, about 12 seconds.
Variation: three pets
Animate this photo of my three pets sitting in a row. They stand up on their back legs and do a quick, cute trio dance: a little bounce, a spin, then each one hops toward the camera in turn, does a happy wiggle and hops back. Keep each pet's markings, colour and size exactly as in the photo. Bright backyard, fixed camera, vertical 9:16, about 10 seconds, playful and gentle.
Variation: small business
Animate this photo of three team members standing in a row in our [type of business, for example: bakery, salon, garden centre]. Keep their faces, uniforms and the shop exactly as they are. They do a quick, upbeat trio dance: bounce, spin together, then each one steps to the front in turn holding one of our products and gives a little shoulder bounce. Fixed camera, bright and friendly, vertical 9:16, about 12 seconds. No text, no logos added.

Tips

  • Three people only. A fourth person in the photo tends to blur or merge into the others.
  • Leave room at the edges of the photo so spins and hair flips don't get cut off.
  • Add the sound inside your app. Don't type out or caption the song's words.
  • Get a yes from everyone in the photo before you post it, and from the team before using staff photos.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
