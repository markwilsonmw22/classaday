Content & marketing · Day 19 of 30

Your marketing plan: customer, angles and channels

3 hours

Define your customer in detail, write ten angles to test, and decide how you'll split paid and organic marketing.

Lesson 25 minExample 20 minTasks 135 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

Ads don't sell products. Angles do. An angle is the specific reason a specific person wants your product right now. The same brush can be sold as "less hair on the sofa", "a calmer grooming routine" or "save $80 a month at the groomer". Each angle attracts different people and needs a different ad.

Your customer avatar

Write a one-page profile of your main buyer:

  • Who they are (age range, life situation, where they live)
  • The problem in their words (from comments and reviews)
  • What they've tried before and why it didn't work
  • What they'd love to feel or have instead
  • What makes them hesitate to buy (price, trust, delivery time)
  • Where they spend time online

Write ten angles

Use these angle types:

  1. Pain: the problem in a vivid moment ("black sweater, white dog")
  2. Money saved: compare with the alternative ("$80 a month at the groomer")
  3. Time saved
  4. Satisfying demo: the "wow" moment itself
  5. Gift: for a person who has the problem
  6. Identity: "for husky people"
  7. Comparison: your product vs the old way
  8. Social proof: customer reactions (once you have them)
  9. Seasonal: shedding season, muddy winter walks
  10. Emotional: bonding, care, calm

Paid vs organic

  • Paid ads (Meta, TikTok) give you speed and data. You pay for every test.
  • Organic content (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) is free but slow and unpredictable. It's also the cheapest way to find which hooks work before you pay to promote them.

A good beginner split: post organic content daily and run one paid test with your best angles. Winning organic videos often become your best ads.

Pick one main ad platform

Start with Meta (Facebook and Instagram) if your customer is 25+; TikTok if your customer is younger or your product is very visual and trending. Don't run both at once with a small budget.

2Worked example

Priya's avatar: "Sarah, 34, lives in a condo in Mississauga with a husky. Works hybrid. Loves her dog, hates the fur on her dark clothes and the couch. Tried a cheap brush from the pet store; it clogged and she gave up. Pays $90 for grooming every 6 weeks. Worried about buying from unknown websites. Scrolls Instagram Reels at night."

Her top 3 angles to test: 1) "Black leggings, white husky" (pain), 2) "The 10-minute groom that saves $90" (money), 3) the satisfying one-click fur release (demo).

Platform: Meta, because her avatar is 30+ and on Reels.
