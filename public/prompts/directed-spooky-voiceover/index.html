Directed spooky story voice-over

Write a short Halloween campfire story with stage directions like [voice drops to a whisper], then let an AI voice read it with real feeling.

Fish AudioChatGPTClaude

Why it's trending

Fish Audio released its Drama 3 voice model on October 13, 2026. You direct the delivery in plain words inside square brackets, and its launch post uses a spooky "do not open that door" line as an example. Halloween is October 31.

How to use it

  1. Ask a text AI for a 30 to 45 second spooky story with directions in square brackets.
  2. Paste the story into an AI voice tool that understands written directions, and pick a stock voice or your own.
  3. Listen once, then tweak the directions where the feeling is off, for example [slower] or [nervous laugh].
  4. Lay the audio over a dark clip of your porch, a candle or a hallway and post it before Halloween.

Prompts

Main prompt
Write a spooky but family-friendly campfire story that takes about 40 seconds to read aloud. Set it [for example: on a quiet street in a small Canadian town on Halloween night]. Add short acting directions in square brackets before lines, like [breathless, speaking fast], [voice drops to a whisper], [slow and serious] or [shaky laugh]. Change the direction at least 5 times so the mood builds. End on a twist that is creepy, not gory. Use short sentences. No real people, places you can find on a map, or famous characters.
Variation: kid-friendly
Write a 30-second not-too-scary Halloween story for kids aged 6 to 9, about [for example: a jack-o'-lantern that won't stop giggling]. Add acting directions in square brackets, like [curious], [tiny gasp] or [cheerful whisper]. End with a funny, happy twist. Keep words simple.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: candle shop]. Write a 30-second spooky story narration set in my shop after closing, with acting directions in square brackets, like [whispering] or [slow, mysterious]. The twist reveals something fun and true about the shop, such as [our new fall scent]. No fake reviews, made-up prices or false claims. Add a short caption for the post.

Tips

  • Short directions work best. Three to five words in the brackets is plenty.
  • Only use your own voice or one that someone agreed to share. Never copy a real person's voice without permission.
  • Read the story out loud once yourself. If you stumble, the AI voice will too.
  • Add quiet background sound like wind or a creaky door, but keep it under the voice.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
