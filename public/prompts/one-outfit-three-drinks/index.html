One outfit, three drinks: let them vote

Show one outfit next to three drinks that match it in different ways, then ask people to vote in the comments. AI picks the drinks and styles the shots.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

A weekly Instagram Reels audio roundup for October 12 to 25, 2026 lists a rising format where creators pair one outfit with three drinks and ask viewers to vote for the best match in the comments.

How to use it

  1. Take one clear photo of your outfit, standing against a plain wall.
  2. Use the main prompt to get three drink ideas that match the outfit's colours and mood.
  3. Make the drinks yourself and photograph each one, or use the styling prompt to make matching drink photos.
  4. Post the outfit first, then the three drinks as 1, 2 and 3, and ask people to vote in the comments.

Prompts

Main prompt: pick the drinks
Look at the outfit in this photo. Suggest three drinks that match it, each in a different way: one that matches the main colour, one that matches the mood, and one surprise pick. They can be coffee, tea, juice, a smoothie or a mocktail. For each, give the name, a one-line description of how it looks in the glass, and a short on-screen label under 6 words. No alcohol and no brand names.
Variation: style the drink photos
Create three matching square photos of drinks, one per image: [drink 1], [drink 2], [drink 3]. Each drink sits on the same light wooden table by a window, in soft morning light, shot from slightly above. Match the colours to this outfit photo: use its main colours in the cup, the napkin and the background. Real, simple glassware with no logos or writing. Make the three photos look like a set.
Variation: the three-panel vote card
Make one tall image split into four parts. The top part shows this outfit photo, unchanged. Below it, three drinks sit side by side, numbered 1, 2 and 3 in a clean, simple font. Each drink matches the outfit's colours in a different way. Plain off-white background, soft shadows, no logos. Keep my face, body and clothes exactly as they are in the photo.
Variation: small business
I run [your café, bakery or shop]. Suggest one item we sell to be the "outfit" (for example our fall scarf, a cake or a candle) and three of our drinks or products that could pair with it. For each pairing, write a short label under 6 words and a one-line reason it works. Then write a caption that asks people to vote 1, 2 or 3 in the comments. Friendly, no pressure to buy, no emojis.

Tips

  • Use the same light and the same table for all three drinks so they look like a set.
  • Make the three picks clearly different, or the vote feels too easy.
  • Reply to the votes. The comments are where this trend grows.
  • If you use AI drink photos for a business, only show drinks you actually sell.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
