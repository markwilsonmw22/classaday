Slow costume reveal walk

A low-angle shot starts on your feet, then you walk slowly toward the camera until the full Halloween costume is revealed.

KlingVeoRunwayHiggsfield

Why it's trending

Two Halloween trend roundups updated on October 14, 2026 list a costume walk: a low shot of the feet, a slow walk toward the camera, and the full costume revealed as the music builds. People film it in person; this pack makes an AI version from a photo of you in costume.

How to use it

  1. Take a clear, full-body photo of yourself in costume, standing in good light.
  2. Make the starting frame with the image prompt, keeping your face and costume the same.
  3. Animate it with the video prompt, then add your own music and time the reveal to the build.

Tips

  • Shoes and the bottom of the costume show first, so make sure they're clear in your photo.
  • AI video can change your face when it gets close. Try a few times and keep the best take.
  • Add music in your editor. A slow build that hits right when your face appears works best.
  • Great for costume shops, salons and makeup artists showing off a finished look.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
