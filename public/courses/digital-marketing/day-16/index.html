Social media marketing & management · Day 16 of 30

Social strategy, content pillars and the calendar

3.5 hours

Turn business goals into a social media strategy with content pillars, a posting rhythm and a month-long content calendar.

Lesson 30 minExample 20 minTasks 160 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

Random posting gets random results. A strategy answers four questions: what are we trying to achieve, who for, what will we post, and how will we know it's working?

1. Set goals that connect to money

Weak goal Strong goal
"Get more followers" "Get 40 people a month to show the Instagram offer in store"
"Go viral" "Get 20 quote requests a month from Facebook"
"Post more" "Grow Reels reach to 15,000 local accounts a month while keeping 4% engagement"

2. Content pillars

Pillars are the 3–5 themes a brand posts about again and again. They keep content varied, on-brand and easy to plan.

Pillar Purpose Café examples Plumber examples
Educate Be useful, earn saves How we brew the perfect oat latte; coffee myths 3 signs your water heater is dying; how to shut off your water
Behind the scenes / people Build trust and personality Meet barista Sam; 6am bake routine A day with tech Jake; van tour
Proof Show results and happy customers Customer photos (reposted with permission), review graphics Before/after jobs, real review highlights
Promote Drive action New seasonal drink; loyalty card Same-day booking; furnace tune-up season
Entertain / culture (optional) Reach and shares Coffee shop trends, local events Plumbing "fails" (respectful), relatable homeowner moments

3. Rhythm

Consistency beats volume. A realistic plan for a small business:

Platform Starting rhythm
Instagram 3–4 feed posts (mix of Reels and carousels) + Stories most days
Facebook 3 posts a week, shared from Instagram plus local and community posts
TikTok / Shorts 3 videos a week
LinkedIn (personal) 2–3 posts a week
Google Business Profile 1 post a week

4. The content calendar

A shared spreadsheet or tool (Notion, Trello, Google Sheets, or your scheduler's calendar) with these columns:

Date · Platform · Pillar · Format · Hook · Caption · Visual / file link · CTA · Status (Idea → Draft → Approved → Scheduled → Posted) · Results notes

5. The batching workflow

Agencies that make content day by day burn out. Batch instead:

  1. Plan day (monthly, 2 hours): ideas, pillars, dates, hooks.
  2. Shoot day (monthly or twice a month, 2–4 hours on site): film and photograph everything at once.
  3. Edit day: design carousels, edit videos, write captions.
  4. Approval: client reviews the month in one go (give a deadline).
  5. Schedule: load everything into the scheduler.
  6. Daily (15–30 min): reply to comments and DMs, post Stories, react to trends.

6. Seasonal and local hooks

Plan around the calendar: holidays, local festivals, school terms, weather (first frost for furnaces, patio season for cafés), and industry events. Add them to the calendar three months ahead.

2Worked example

Corner Grind's November plan.

Goal: "Get 60 new customers to redeem the 'first latte $2 off' Instagram offer this month."

Pillars and mix: Educate 35% · People 25% · Proof 20% · Promote 20%.

Week 1 (sample):

Day Platform Pillar Format Hook
Mon IG + TikTok People Reel "6:02am. The pastry case is empty. Here's what happens next."
Wed IG Educate Carousel "Your oat latte tastes burnt? It's probably this."
Thu TikTok Proof Reel "We asked 20 students where they study in London. 14 said here." (real street interviews)
Fri IG + FB Promote Reel "First latte, $2 off. Show this video at the till."
Sat GBP Promote Post Weekend hours + new maple pecan scone

Shoot day: two hours on a Tuesday morning, producing 12 videos and 40 photos for the month.

★Tip
