Suppliers & fulfilment · Day 11 of 30

Vetting suppliers and ordering samples

3 hours

Contact suppliers with the right questions, order samples, and set up a quality check so you know exactly what customers will receive.

Lesson 25 minExample 15 minTasks 140 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

Never sell a product you haven't held. Samples show you quality, packaging and real delivery times, and give you footage for your ads.

Supplier scorecard

Before ordering, check:

  • Store rating 4.6+ and open for 1+ year
  • Recent reviews with customer photos that match the listing
  • Replies to your message within 24 hours
  • Clear answers on processing time, shipping lines and returns

The message to send

Copy, adapt and send:

Hi, I run an online store in Canada and plan to sell [product] regularly. Before I start, could you tell me: 1. Processing time before shipping? 2. Your best tracked shipping line to Canada and the US, and the usual delivery time? 3. Can you ship without invoices or promotional material in the parcel? 4. What happens if an item arrives damaged? 5. Do you offer lower prices at 20+ orders a day? Thank you!

Question 3 matters: you don't want a supplier's price tag or flyer inside your customer's parcel.

Ordering samples

  • Order one of each product to your own address, using the same shipping method customers will get. This tells you the real delivery time.
  • If possible, order the same product from two suppliers and compare.
  • Track the order from day one and write down how long each step took.

Quality check when it arrives

  • Does it match the photos and size listed?
  • Does it actually do what the ad claims? Test it properly.
  • How is it packed? Any damage?
  • Would you be happy if you'd paid $30+ for it?

Film while you test

When samples arrive (likely around Days 20–24), you'll film unboxing and demo clips. Keep products clean and packaging intact for filming.

2Worked example

Priya's message results:

  • Brush supplier A (CJ): replied in 3 hours, processing 1–2 days, can ship without invoice, replaces damaged items with photo proof. ✓
  • Brush supplier B (AliExpress): replied in 2 days with a generic answer. Backup only.
  • Paw cleaner supplier: replied in 6 hours, ships in plain poly bags. She asks for a box instead and gets one for +$0.40.

She orders 4 samples (both brush suppliers, paw cleaner, kit) for $96 total including shipping and logs the order dates.
