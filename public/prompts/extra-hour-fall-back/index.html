Clocks fall back: what I'll really do with the extra hour

Clocks go back an hour on November 1 in much of Canada. Post the big plans for your bonus hour, then the honest truth.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

Daily Hive reported on October 14, 2026 that daylight saving time ends on Sunday, November 1, in most of Canada, and that a petition to stop changing the clocks comes back every November. It's a timely, shared moment, so this pack is pegged to the date, not to a measured trend.

How to use it

  1. Film two short clips: one where you look ready for a big, productive day, and one of what you're really doing (napping, scrolling, a second coffee).
  2. Use the main prompt to write the plan list and the honest reveal. Pick the pair that sounds most like you.
  3. Put the plan on the first clip and the reveal on the second. Keep each on screen for 2 to 3 seconds.
  4. Post on Saturday night or Sunday morning, and ask people what they did with their extra hour.

Prompts

Main prompt
The clocks go back one hour this Sunday, so I get an extra hour. Write a short text overlay for a two-part video. Part 1: "What I'll do with my extra hour" and a list of 4 ambitious plans, each under 6 words, like "meal prep for the week". Part 2: "What I actually did" and one honest, relatable line. Something about my life: [your routine or hobby]. Write 3 versions: cosy, chaotic and very honest. Keep it light and kind.
Variation: the clock-change confusion
Write 5 short, relatable lines about the morning after the clocks go back, for a text-on-screen video. Examples of the feeling: the microwave clock is still wrong, the pet still wants breakfast at the old time, it gets dark before dinner. Each line under 10 words. Start with a hook like "Things that happen when the clocks fall back:". Canadian spelling, no jokes about anyone else.
Variation: bonus-hour challenge
Give me 6 ideas for using one extra hour on a Sunday in November, for a carousel post called "One bonus hour, 6 better ways to spend it". Mix cosy and useful ideas, like a walk before it gets dark early or swapping the batteries in the smoke alarm. One line each, under 10 words, plus a one-line caption that asks people to pick their favourite.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business] in [town]. Write a short, friendly post for this weekend about the clocks going back one hour. Include a reminder to change the clocks, our real hours for Sunday [your hours], and one warm idea for spending the extra hour that fits what we do, like [example]. Under 60 words. No made-up deals, numbers or customer quotes.

Tips

  • Check your province first. Not every part of Canada changes its clocks, so word your post to fit where you live.
  • Timing matters. This one only works this weekend, so post by Sunday afternoon.
  • Keep the reveal short. One honest line lands better than a second list.
  • Pick the sound inside the app so it's licensed for your post.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
