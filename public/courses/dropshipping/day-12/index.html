Suppliers & fulfilment · Day 12 of 30

Shipping, delivery promises and returns

3 hours

Write shipping and returns policies that are honest, protect your payment account and keep customers calm.

Lesson 20 minExample 15 minTasks 145 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

Most dropshipping complaints are about delivery time. Customers don't hate waiting 10 days nearly as much as they hate being promised 3 and waiting 10. Set expectations clearly and you'll avoid most refund requests.

Your delivery promise

Calculate: processing time + shipping time + 2 days buffer. If processing is 1–2 days and shipping 8–12, promise 10–15 business days for the China line, or 4–8 business days from a US warehouse.

Show that promise:

  • On the product page, under the add-to-cart button
  • In the shipping policy
  • In the order confirmation email
  • In the shipping confirmation email with the tracking link

Shipping prices

Choose one approach:

  • Free shipping with shipping cost built into the price (simplest, converts well)
  • Free shipping over a threshold (e.g. $50) to encourage two-packs
  • Flat rate (e.g. $4.95) if your margin is tight

Returns and refunds policy

A clear policy reduces chargebacks. Include:

  1. Time window: e.g. 30 days from delivery.
  2. Condition: unused, in original packaging.
  3. Damaged or wrong items: a photo by email, replaced or refunded at no cost.
  4. Change of mind: customer pays return shipping, or you offer a partial refund to keep the item (often cheaper than shipping it back to China).
  5. How to start: an email address and what to include (order number, photos).
  6. When refunds are issued: e.g. within 5 business days of approval.

Check your local consumer protection rules; some places require specific rights for online purchases.

Where do returns go?

Never send customers a supplier's address in China. For low-cost items, it's usually best to refund or replace without asking for the item back. For higher-priced items, use a local returns address (your own or a returns service) and resell or check the item.

Lost parcels

If tracking hasn't updated in 10 days, contact the supplier and reship. Keep a "lost parcel" line in your budget; it happens.

2Worked example

Priya's shipping section (product page):

Free shipping to Canada and the US. Orders leave our partner warehouse in 1–2 business days and arrive in 4–8 business days in the US and 8–14 business days in Canada. You'll get tracking by email as soon as it ships.

Her returns summary:

30-day happiness guarantee. If your item arrives damaged or isn't right, email support@hearthandhound.com with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a replacement or a full refund. For change-of-mind returns, contact us within 30 days and we'll find the simplest option for you.

★Tip

Under-promise and over-deliver. If a parcel arrives in 7 days when you said 8–14, customers leave good reviews. If it arrives in 7 when you said 3, they ask for a refund.
