"Building something AI can never replace": the small human moments

A short, warm Reel of the real-life moments no machine can stand in for. AI helps you pick the shots and write the on-screen lines.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

Later's TikTok trends roundup, published November 13, 2026, lists "Building something AI can never replace": creators share footage of things AI can't replace, like human connection. It's the heartfelt cousin of our November 6 pack "Sure, I'd like to see AI…", which played the same idea for laughs.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to get a shot list from your own life: people, places and habits that matter to you.
  2. Film 4–6 short clips over a few days. Ask anyone in the shot if they're happy to be in it.
  3. Open on the line "Building something AI can never replace", then let each clip play with one short caption.
  4. End on your favourite moment and hold it for an extra second.

Prompts

Main prompt
Help me plan a short video for the trend "Building something AI can never replace". It shows real moments of human connection in my life. About me: [e.g. two kids, a weekly hockey game with friends, Sunday calls with my grandma]. Give me 6 shot ideas I can film this week with my phone. For each one, write a caption under 8 words that is warm and specific, not cheesy. Then suggest the best order, ending on the most heartfelt shot. No hashtags, no emojis.
Variation: Canadian winter
Write 6 on-screen captions for a "Building something AI can never replace" video set in a Canadian winter. Ideas to draw from: shovelling a neighbour's driveway, a backyard rink, hot chocolate after tobogganing, a potluck, teaching a kid to skate. Each caption is under 8 words, plain and kind. Suggest one simple shot for each. End with a line about the people who show up when it's cold.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business, e.g. a bakery or a hair salon]. Write a shot list and captions for a "Building something AI can never replace" Reel about the human side of my work. My real moments: [e.g. knowing regulars by name, handwritten notes on orders, the team's morning chat]. 5 shots, each caption under 8 words. Warm and honest, not salesy. Don't invent customer quotes, reviews, numbers or results, and don't knock other businesses or tools.

Tips

  • Real beats polished. Shaky, candid clips feel more human than a perfect setup.
  • Pick specific moments, like one hand-written card, rather than big ideas like "family".
  • Keep it gentle. The trend is about what people bring, not about bashing AI.
  • Use a soft, slow sound and keep the whole video under 20 seconds.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
