Product research · Day 6 of 30

Research method 1: social platforms and ad libraries

4 hours

Use TikTok, Instagram and the Meta Ad Library to find products that are already selling, and to understand why.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 210 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

The best evidence that a product sells is someone else already paying to advertise it. Today you'll learn to find that evidence on the platforms where you'll later advertise.

Meta Ad Library (free, no account needed)

  1. Go to the Meta Ad Library and choose your country, "All ads".
  2. Search keywords from your niche: "dog brush", "deshedding", "grooming".
  3. Click into results and look at the "Started running on" date. Ads running 30+ days, especially with several versions of the same ad, are making money.
  4. Click the advertiser's page and look at their other ads. Successful stores often test variations of one winner.
  5. Visit the store from the ad. Note price, offer and how the page is built.

Search phrases that sellers use in their copy, which find more stores: "free shipping", "50% off", "limited stock", "shop now", plus your niche word.

TikTok

  • Search your niche words and filter by "This month". Products with several viral videos from different accounts are trending.
  • TikTok Creative Center shows top-performing ads by region and industry, plus trending hashtags. Use it to study hooks and formats.
  • TikTok Shop listings show units sold for some products, a strong demand signal.
  • Read comments. "Where do I get this?" is gold. "Mine broke after a week" is a warning.

Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

Search the same keywords. Reels are useful for seeing how the product looks in real homes and how real customers talk about it. Save the phrases they use; they'll go into your product page.

Organise what you find

Every promising product gets a row in your Products tab with: link to the ad, link to the store, price, days the ad has run, number of ad variations, and your notes on the hook.

What you're looking for

A product shows up in at least two places (for example, an ad running 45 days in the Ad Library plus organic videos on TikTok with buying comments). One source can be luck. Two is a pattern.

2Worked example

Priya's research session, 4 hours:

  • Ad Library: "deshedding brush" (Canada) shows 6 advertisers. One store has run 4 versions of the same ad since early August, about 70 days. The store sells it at $34.95 with a two-pack bundle.
  • TikTok: a video of a husky's undercoat coming off has 2.3M views; the top comments ask for the link. Three other accounts posted similar videos this month.
  • Creative Center: the top pet ads all open with a close-up of hair coming off, followed by a satisfied owner.
  • Finds a second product: a paw cleaner cup. Ads running 40+ days from three stores; strong "muddy paws" angle for autumn.

She adds 9 new rows and marks the slicker brush and paw cleaner as "strong signal".

3Today's tasks

✓By tonight you'll have

An updated Products tab with ad links, run times and a "strong signal" flag on the best products.

★Tip

Don't copy someone's ad. Study why it works (the first 2 seconds, the problem it shows, the offer) and make your own version with your own footage. Copying creative gets ads rejected and accounts flagged.
