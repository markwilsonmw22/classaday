"Nobody cares what you wear to…": the outfit montage for your tiny niche

Open mid-task with "nobody cares what you wear to [your niche]", then show every outfit you've worn to it. AI plans the looks and the grid.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

Later's Instagram Reels trends roundup, updated November 27, 2026, lists "Nobody cares what you wear to…": creators open in the middle of a task with that line and a niche activity on screen, then cut to a montage of their outfits for it and an "I do" moment.

How to use it

  1. Pick your niche: curling night, the dog park, choir practice, the farmers' market, your weekly shift.
  2. Use the text prompt to sharpen your on-screen line and plan the order of your outfit clips.
  3. Film yourself mid-task with "nobody cares what you wear to…" on screen, then cut to quick clips of your outfits, ending on a proud "I do" moment.
  4. Want a cover image too? Use the grid prompt with photos of your own outfits.

Prompts

Main prompt (plan the montage)
I'm making a short video for the "Nobody cares what you wear to…" trend. My niche activity is [ACTIVITY, e.g. Tuesday night curling]. Here are the outfits I've worn to it: [list 4 to 8 outfits]. Give me 5 versions of the opening line that sound like me, the best order for the outfit clips (build up to the best one last), and 3 ideas for the final "I do" moment. Keep it fun and kind, under eight words per line.
Variation: outfit grid cover image
Use the attached photos of my real outfits. Lay them out as a clean 3 by 2 grid of flat-lay outfit cards on a soft off-white background, each outfit neatly folded or laid flat with its shoes, like a style diary page. Keep the clothes exactly as they look in my photos: same colours, patterns and shapes. Leave a blank strip across the top for my own title. Vertical 4:5. No logos, no brand names, no people, no other text.
Variation: outfit ideas for next time
I go to [ACTIVITY] every [HOW OFTEN] in [CITY, PROVINCE], and it's [weather or setting, e.g. a cold rink in December]. Suggest 6 outfit ideas I could build from what I already own: [list your main pieces]. Make them practical for the activity and a little over the top, in a way that would be fun to film. No shopping list and no brand names.
Variation for a small business: our team's uniform
I run [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY, PROVINCE]. Write a plan for a "Nobody cares what you wear to work at a [type of business]" Reel where team members who agreed to be filmed show how they style our uniform or work clothes. Give me the opening line, 5 clip ideas and one caption. Keep it honest: no made-up claims, prices, deals or reviews.

Tips

  • The more specific the niche, the better. "Nobody cares what you wear to the recycling depot" beats "to work".
  • Keep each outfit clip short, about one second, and hit the beat with every cut.
  • Business accounts: the sound often used with this trend is a major-label song, so check it's cleared or use an original sound.
  • Only show teammates, friends or family who have agreed to be in it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
