Don't cross this line: your tiny, very serious boundaries

Call out the small things nobody should ever do near you, like jeans on the bed. AI helps you find funny, relatable lines that fit your life or your shop.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A weekly social media trends roundup published October 28, 2026 lists "Don't Cross This Line" in its October 21 section: creators pair a viral sound bite with a small, relatable boundary, like sitting on the bed in jeans that touched the subway floor.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to get a list of small boundaries that fit you, then pick the one people will nod along to.
  2. Film a short clip of the line being crossed, or just about to be: the jeans near the bed, the hand reaching for the fries.
  3. Add your boundary as on-screen text and pick the trending sound in the app.
  4. Ask viewers in the caption to share their own line.

Prompts

Main prompt
Help me with a short video where I call out a tiny boundary that people cross. Give me 10 boundaries, each under 12 words, written as on-screen text. Make them small, everyday and relatable, like "Outside jeans on my bed". Base them on my life: I live [your living situation], I work as [your job], and my pet peeves are [two or three]. Keep it light and playful, never mean. No hashtags, no emojis.
Variation: friends and family edition
Give me 8 funny "don't cross this line" boundaries about sharing things with friends or family, like borrowing a hoodie and never giving it back, or eating the last slice without asking. Each under 12 words. Warm, teasing, nothing that names or shames a real person.
Variation: shot ideas
I picked this boundary for my video: "[your boundary]". Suggest 3 simple ways to film it in under 10 seconds with just a phone, at home. For each one, say what's in the frame, where the camera sits and the exact moment the text should appear.
Variation: small business
I run [your type of business] in [your town]. Write 8 playful "don't cross this line" boundaries from our side of the counter, about small things customers or staff do, like [one or two examples]. Each under 12 words. Friendly and in on the joke, never rude to customers. Don't make up prices, reviews or deals.

Tips

  • Small and specific wins. "Shoes past the doormat" lands better than "being rude".
  • Keep it about habits, not about a real person. Nobody should feel called out.
  • Show the line being crossed in the first second, then let the text do the work.
  • Only film friends or family who have said yes.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
