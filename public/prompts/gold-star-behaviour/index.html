Gold star behaviour list

List the small habits you think deserve a gold star, and add a star on screen for each one. AI helps you write the list and make the star graphic.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

An Instagram trend report updated October 14, 2026 lists "Gold star behaviour" in its October 7 roundup: people list habits they think deserve a gold star and add one on screen for each. This pack helps you write a list that feels true to you.

How to use it

  1. Pick a theme: home life, work, school, fitness, your pet or your shop.
  2. Paste the main prompt into the AI and pick your favourite 5 or 6 lines.
  3. Film a simple shot of yourself, add each line as on-screen text, and drop a gold star sticker next to it as it appears.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a "Gold star behaviour" Reel: a list of small, real habits that deserve a gold star. My theme is: [for example: surviving a Canadian winter]. Give me 10 options, each under 10 words, written in first person, warm and a little funny. Mix tiny wins (like warming up the car for someone else) with honest ones. Keep it kind, never mean about any person or group.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: dog grooming salon]. Write 8 "gold star behaviour" lines about customers or my team, under 10 words each, warm and playful. For example: booking ahead, saying thank you, bringing back the treat jar. Make it feel like a friendly thank-you, not a list of rules. No claims about prices, results or other businesses.
Variation: gold star sticker image
Make a single shiny gold five-point star sticker, like the ones teachers put on school work. Slightly raised foil look with soft highlights and a thin white sticker border. Centred on a plain, flat bright green background so I can remove it in my editor. No text.

Tips

  • Keep each line short so it fits on screen and can be read in two seconds.
  • Add one star per line, timed to the beat, so the stars pile up by the end.
  • Pick the lines that are true for you. Real details get more shares than generic ones.
  • Shops can end with "Gold star for you if you read this far" as a friendly sign-off.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
