Halloween sticker sheet as editable vectors

Make a sheet of cute Halloween stickers as a clean vector file you can recolour, resize and print, cut or post without blurry edges.

Ideogram

Why it's trending

On October 22 and 23, AI news digests reported that Ideogram can now make editable vector graphics (SVG files) from a text prompt, and turn existing images into vectors. That makes print-ready stickers, labels and decals much easier than with normal AI pictures.

How to use it

  1. Open the vector option in the tool and paste the main prompt.
  2. Pick the sheet you like best and download it as an SVG file.
  3. Open the SVG in any design app to change colours, move pieces or delete ones you don't want.
  4. Print it on sticker paper, send it to a cutting machine, or post the designs as a sticker pack.

Prompts

Main prompt
A flat vector sticker sheet of 9 cute Halloween stickers on a plain white background: a smiling pumpkin, a small ghost with rosy cheeks, a black cat with a curled tail, a bat, a witch hat, a candy corn, a full moon with stars, a spooky little house and a cauldron with green bubbles. Bold, clean outlines, a thick white border around each sticker, simple shapes, a limited palette of orange, purple, black, lime green and cream. No gradients, no shadows, no text, evenly spaced in a 3 by 3 grid.
Variation: your pet as a sticker
A flat vector sticker of a small fluffy dog wearing a pumpkin costume, sitting and smiling, with a thick white border. Simple shapes, bold outlines, flat colours in orange, brown and cream. No gradients, no text, plain white background.
Variation: cosy Canadian fall
A flat vector sticker sheet of 6 cosy fall stickers: a red maple leaf, a steaming mug with a marshmallow, a plaid scarf, a pair of mittens, a pumpkin on a hay bale and a canoe on a calm lake. Bold outlines, thick white borders, flat colours in rust, mustard, forest green and cream. No gradients, no text, evenly spaced.
Variation: small business
A flat vector shop window decal for a small [type of business, for example bakery]: a friendly ghost holding [your product], with a few bats and stars around it. Bold, clean outlines, simple shapes, 3 flat colours only: orange, black and white. No gradients, no text, no logos, plain background, designed to be cut from vinyl.

Tips

  • Say "flat", "no gradients" and "bold outlines". Simple shapes make cleaner vector files.
  • Leave text out of the prompt and add your own words in a design app. AI lettering often has typos.
  • Use fewer colours for vinyl cutting. Every colour is a separate layer to cut.
  • Only sell designs you're happy are your own idea, and never ask for logos or famous characters.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
