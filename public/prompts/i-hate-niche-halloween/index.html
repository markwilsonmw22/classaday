"I hate ___ Halloween": the too-niche costume joke

Joke about the costumes only your hobby, job or friend group would understand. AI helps you brainstorm the in-jokes and write the caption.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trend report lists "I hate _ Halloween" on October 28, 2026: "I hate _ Halloween because what do you mean…", used to joke about very niche costumes that only one group would get. It's a fresh last-minute twist before Halloween on October 31.

How to use it

  1. Pick your group: your job, your hobby, your town, your family or your friend group.
  2. Use the main prompt to brainstorm costume ideas that only that group would understand.
  3. Pick your favourite and either wear it or show a photo of it. Film a short clip with the text over it.
  4. Ask people in the comments what the most niche costume in their world would be.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a short video for the trend "I hate [group] Halloween because what do you mean you're dressed as…". My group is [job, hobby, town or friend group]. Brainstorm 10 costume ideas that only people in that group would understand, like an inside joke, a common problem or a famous moment in our small world. Keep them kind and fun. Then write the full on-screen line for my top 3, each under 20 words.
Variation: make it from what I own
I want to dress up as this niche idea: [your idea]. I have [list of clothes and things at home]. Suggest the simplest way to make the costume tonight with what I have, in 5 steps or fewer. Then give me one short line of on-screen text that explains the joke to people outside my group, to add at the end.
Variation: friend group edition
Write 6 lines for a carousel post: "I hate our friend group's Halloween because what do you mean you're dressed as…". Base each line on these harmless in-jokes from our group: [list 3 to 6 shared memories or sayings]. Keep every joke friendly, with nothing that would embarrass anyone. Suggest a closing slide that tags everyone.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business]. Write 5 lines for the trend "I hate [our trade or industry] Halloween because what do you mean you're dressed as…", based on things only people in our line of work or our regulars would get, like [examples of common moments or tools]. Keep the jokes about the work, not about customers. Add a caption that invites people to share their own trade's version.

Tips

  • The more specific, the better. A joke only 50 people get often gets the most comments from those 50.
  • Keep jokes about situations and things, not about real people or customers.
  • A cardboard sign or one prop is enough. The text does most of the work.
  • Post today or tomorrow. This one is tied to Halloween on October 31.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
