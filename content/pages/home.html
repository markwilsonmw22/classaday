Do I need any experience?

No. Every course starts from the beginning and explains new words the first time they appear.

Can I go slower or faster?

Yes. In each course's Calendar tab, choose your start date, which days you study and your pace. Every date updates instantly.

Where is my progress saved?

On the device you're using for now. When membership opens, progress will sync to your account across phone and laptop.

Will I need to spend money besides the course?

Dropshipping needs a store plan, samples and a small ad-testing budget, and Day 1 shows how to keep it lean. Most AI tools have free tiers. The website templates publish for free.

Can I use the templates for client work?

Yes, for your own sites and unlimited client projects. You can't resell the templates themselves as a template pack.
