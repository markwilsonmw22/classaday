How a day works

Each program is cut into days of roughly three to four hours. Start whenever you like; the calendar plans the rest around the days you can study.

  1. 1

    Read the lesson

    One focused topic in plain English, with diagrams where a picture explains it faster.

    30–45 MIN
  2. 2

    Watch it done

    A worked example with real numbers, screens, templates and the exact words to use.

    20–30 MIN
  3. 3

    Do the tasks

    A short checklist sized to an evening. This is where most of the day goes, and where the skill sticks.

    2–3 H
  4. 4

    Tick it off

    Your calendar moves forward. Fall behind and it shows you where you are, so you can pick up again.

    1 MIN

Questions

How long does each course take?

{{FAQ_HOURS}} The Recipe Vault and the template pack are downloads, so you use them at your own pace.

Do I need any experience?

No. Every program starts from the beginning and explains terms the first time they appear. If you can use a phone and a web browser, you can follow along.

Can I study slower or faster?

Yes. On each course's Plan tab you choose your start date, the days of the week you can study, and your pace. The calendar recalculates every date, and shows what to do today.

Where is my progress saved?

For now, in the browser on the device you're using. When membership opens, your progress will sync to your account so you can switch between phone and laptop.

Will I need to spend money besides the course?

Dropshipping needs a store plan, product samples and a small ad-testing budget; Day 1 shows a starter budget and how to keep it lean. Most AI tools have free tiers, and the course points out when a paid plan is worth it. The templates publish for free.

Can I print the recipe books?

Yes. Each volume is a letter-size PDF designed to print cleanly at home, with a planner page you can photocopy.

Can I use the templates for client work?

Yes. Use them for your own sites and for unlimited client projects. You can't resell or give away the templates themselves as a template pack.
