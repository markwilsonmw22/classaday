POV: busy until 5, dinner at 6: the two-lives glow-up

One POV line about your busy day, then a quick switch to your off-duty self. AI helps you write the line and plan the two shots.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

SocialPilot's TikTok trends report, published October 30, 2026, lists a "POV" glow-up format for its October 27 to November 10 cycle: a POV line on screen, like a demanding job until 5 but dinner at 6, while the creator lip-syncs a trending pop song released in mid-October. The report says that sound is at its peak.

How to use it

  1. Think of the busiest part of your day and the plans you still have after it.
  2. Use the main prompt to get short POV lines that put the two side by side.
  3. Film two quick shots: you in work mode, then you dressed up and ready to go. Put the POV line on screen over both.
  4. Add the trending sound from the app's own library and post.

Prompts

Main prompt
Help me with a short POV video. Write 8 on-screen lines in the pattern "pov: you [busy thing] until [time] but [fun plan] at [time]". Each line under 14 words. Here's my life: I work as [your job or what you study], and after it I like to [your plans, for example: dinner with friends, hockey, a date night]. Make the gap between the times tight and a little funny. No hashtags, no emojis.
Variation: plan the two shots
I'm filming a two-shot POV glow-up. Shot 1 is me in work mode as a [job]. Shot 2 is me ready for [evening plan]. Give me a simple plan for each shot: what to wear, where to stand, what to hold, and one small action to do. Then tell me the best moment to cut between them. I only have my phone and a 30-minute window.
Variation: everyday life
Write 8 funny POV lines about squeezing a fun plan into a full day, for someone who isn't working: a parent, a student or a retiree. Use the pattern "pov: you [thing] until [time] but [plan] at [time]". Keep each line under 14 words and kind, not mean about anyone.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business] in [town]. Write 6 POV lines for a short video in the pattern "pov: you [busy part of running the business] until [time] but [what we do next] at [time]". Show real parts of our day, like [one or two examples]. Then add one short caption that mentions our hours or a real event we have coming up. Don't make up sales, numbers or customer quotes.

Tips

  • Real times make it funnier. "Until 5:45 but dinner at 6" beats a vague "busy all day".
  • Do the switch on a beat of the song so the change feels snappy.
  • Keep the line short enough to read in two seconds and leave it on screen the whole time.
  • Use a sound from the app's library. Business accounts should pick from the commercial sound library.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
