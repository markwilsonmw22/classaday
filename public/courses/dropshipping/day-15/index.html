Build the store · Day 15 of 30

Theme and homepage

4 hours

Install a fast theme, apply your brand, and build a homepage that tells visitors in five seconds who you are and what to buy.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 210 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

Most visitors from ads land on a product page, not your homepage. But people who are deciding whether to trust you click the logo to check you're real. Your homepage is your shop window: clean, fast and focused.

Choosing a theme

Start with Shopify's free Dawn theme or another free Online Store 2.0 theme. Free themes are fast, well maintained and work with most apps. Paid themes can come later. Avoid themes that load lots of animations; speed matters more on mobile.

Apply your brand

Theme settings: upload your logo and favicon, set colours and fonts from your brand kit, set button style (rounded or square, but consistent), and make sure body text is at least 16px.

Homepage structure that works

  1. Announcement bar: one useful line, e.g. free shipping or your guarantee.
  2. Header: logo, 3–5 menu links (Shop, Best seller, About, Contact, Track order), cart.
  3. Hero: a real image or short video of your best product in use, a clear headline about the benefit, and one button.
  4. Best sellers: your three products.
  5. Why us: three short benefit blocks (guarantee, tracked shipping, support).
  6. The problem you solve: a short section with an image.
  7. Reviews: real reviews only. If you have none yet, leave this out; never invent reviews.
  8. FAQ: shipping, returns, contact.
  9. Footer: policies, contact email, newsletter signup, social links.

Pages to create now

  • About: who you are and why you started. Real and short.
  • Contact: email, a form, and your response time.
  • Track your order: a tracking app or a link to the carrier tracker.
  • FAQ.

Mobile first

Over 75% of your visitors will be on a phone. After every change, check the page on your actual phone, not just the desktop preview.

2Worked example

Hearth & Hound homepage, top to bottom:

  • Announcement: "Free tracked shipping to Canada & US · 30-day guarantee"
  • Hero: photo of a sheltie on a sofa next to a pile of brushed-out fur. Headline: "Less fur on the floor. More time on the sofa." Button: Shop the brush.
  • Best sellers: slicker brush, paw cleaner, grooming kit.
  • Why us: Tested on our own dogs · Tracked shipping · Real humans reply within 24h.
  • Problem section: "Shedding season doesn't have to mean vacuuming twice a day."
  • FAQ with five questions; footer with policies.

★Tip

Use one hero image that shows the product being used, not a product on a white background. People buy the outcome, not the object.
