"And for my final trick…": a tiny promise to yourself

Over a quiet, moody clip, write "and for my final trick" and finish it with a funny or heartfelt promise. AI helps you find the line.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

New Engen's Instagram trends list for November 2026, updated November 5, includes "And For My Final Trick": text that starts "and for my final trick" and ends with a relatable or sincere promise, over a simple clip. The page says some creators add a fisheye effect for a magic-show feel, suggests 6 to 10 seconds, and notes small business owners have picked it up to share the why behind their work.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt to get a list of endings, from silly to sincere.
  2. Film a simple, moody clip: you at your desk, on a walk, or doing your work with your hands.
  3. Add the text "and for my final trick" and finish the sentence. Try a fisheye effect if your editor has one.
  4. Keep it 6 to 10 seconds and leave the text up long enough to read twice.

Prompts

Main prompt
Help me with a short video. The on-screen text starts with "and for my final trick" and ends with a promise to myself. Write 12 endings: 4 funny, 4 relatable, 4 sincere. Examples of the tone: "I'll turn one errand into a whole day of treats" or "I'll stop waiting until I feel ready." About me and what I'm working on: [your goals, habits, this season of life]. Keep each ending under 14 words. No hashtags, no emojis, nothing preachy.
Variation: match the clip
I've filmed this clip: [describe it, for example: me pouring coffee at 6 a.m. on a dark November morning]. Write 6 "and for my final trick…" endings that fit what's on screen, from playful to heartfelt. Then pick the one you think lands best and say why in one sentence.
Variation: the series
Plan a 5-part "and for my final trick" series for the rest of November. Each part gets one promise, one simple shot idea and a one-line caption. The promises should build on each other, from small ("I'll drink a glass of water before coffee") to bigger. About me: [your goals]. Keep it realistic and kind to myself.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business] in [town]. Write 8 "and for my final trick…" endings that share why we do this work or what we're promising our customers this season, like [one or two real things you care about]. Mix one or two light, funny ones with sincere ones. For each, suggest a behind-the-scenes shot. Don't invent sales, numbers, discounts or customer quotes.

Tips

  • Sincere often works better than funny. A real goal said plainly is what people share.
  • Choose a calm, slightly dark clip. Moody light makes the text feel like the big reveal.
  • Leave the text on screen for the whole clip so nobody misses it.
  • Business accounts may not have the trending song. Use an original sound if it isn't available.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
