SEO from zero to advanced · Day 6 of 30

On-page SEO: pages that rank and convert

3.5 hours

Learn every element of on-page SEO and optimise a real page so it's clear to Google and convincing to people.

Lesson 35 minExample 20 minTasks 155 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

On-page SEO is everything you control on the page itself. It's where most quick wins live, because many small business sites skip the basics.

What people see in Google

The on-page checklist

Element Best practice Example
Title tag Main keyword near the start, benefit, brand. About 50–60 characters so it isn't cut off Emergency Plumber in London, ON · 24/7 · Northfield Plumbing
Meta description A short ad for the page, about 150–160 characters. Google may rewrite it, but write one anyway Burst pipe or no hot water? Fixed prices, on-time techs and 400+ five-star reviews. Call 24/7 or book online.
URL Short, readable, keyword included /emergency-plumber/
H1 One per page, matches the search Emergency Plumber in London, Ontario
H2/H3 headings Break the page into scannable sections that answer real questions How fast can you get here? · What does it cost?
Opening paragraph Confirms the searcher is in the right place within two lines
Body content Answers every question the searcher has, in plain language, better than competitors
Images Compressed, descriptive file names, alt text plumber-fixing-burst-pipe-london.jpg
Internal links Link to and from related pages with descriptive anchor text "See our water heater replacement service"
Calls to action Clear, repeated, mobile-friendly (tap to call)
Trust signals Reviews, licences, guarantees, real photos, author or company details

E-E-A-T, in plain words

Google's quality guidelines talk about Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trust. For a plumber that means: real photos of real jobs, licence numbers, years in business, named technicians, honest prices and genuine reviews. For a blog post, it means written or reviewed by someone who actually knows the subject. You can't fake it; you can show it.

Writing for people first

  • Lead with the answer, then explain.
  • Use the customer's words from your voice-of-customer sheet.
  • Add specifics competitors don't: prices or price ranges, timelines, process, photos, FAQs.
  • Make the page easy to act on: phone number at the top, booking button, clear next step.

Where to edit these on common platforms

  • WordPress: install Yoast SEO or Rank Math to edit titles, descriptions and see a checklist.
  • Shopify: "Search engine listing" section at the bottom of each product, collection and page.
  • Wix and Squarespace: SEO settings in each page's settings panel.
  • Hand-coded / templates: edit the <title> and <meta name="description"> in the <head>.

Keyword stuffing is dead

Repeating a keyword 30 times doesn't help and reads badly. Use the main keyword in the title, H1, URL and naturally in the first paragraph. Then write naturally, using related words and answering related questions.

Try it yourself

Write a title and description for a real page, and watch the character counts.

2Worked example

Riya rewrites Northfield's drain-cleaning page.

Before: - Title: Services | Northfield - H1: Our Services - 90 words of text: "We offer quality drain services for all your needs." - No photos, no prices, phone number only in the footer.

After: - Title: Drain Cleaning in London, ON · Same-Day · Northfield Plumbing (58 characters) - Description: Slow or blocked drain? Same-day drain cleaning with a fixed price before we start. Camera inspection included. Book online or call. - H1: Drain Cleaning in London, Ontario - Sections: Signs you need drain cleaning · How we clear drains · What it costs (price ranges) · Same-day service areas · FAQs · Reviews from local customers - Photos of real jobs (with alt text), a sticky "Call now" button on mobile, links to the water heater and emergency pages.

Six weeks later, Search Console shows the page moved from position 34 to position 9 for "drain cleaning london ontario". Not page one's top spot yet, but now it's getting calls.
