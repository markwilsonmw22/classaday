Copywriting: hooks, captions and calls to action

3 hours

Write hooks that stop the scroll, captions that build trust and calls to action that get people to act, for every platform.

Lesson 35 minExample 25 minTasks 120 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

The best video in the world fails if the first line is boring. Copywriting is the skill that makes every other marketing skill work better.

15 hook formulas

Formula Example
The mistake "You're storing your coffee beans wrong."
The number "3 signs your furnace won't make it through winter."
The question "Why does your shower go cold after 5 minutes?"
The bold claim "This $12 part saves you a $400 plumber visit."
The POV "POV: you just opened a café and the espresso machine breaks on day one."
The before/after "This basement flooded on Monday. Here's Friday."
The secret "What plumbers check first (and don't tell you)."
The comparison "Tankless vs tank: which is cheaper over 10 years?"
The challenge "I tried every latte on Richmond Row. Here's the ranking."
The story "At 2am on Christmas Eve, Mrs. Patel's pipe burst..."
The warning "Don't pour this down your drain this weekend."
The insider "Baristas hate this order. Here's why."
The result "We doubled our weekday regulars with one sign."
The relatable "When the landlord says 'just jiggle the handle'..."
The direct "Need a plumber in London today? Watch this first."

Caption frameworks

PAS: Problem, Agitate, Solution

Cold shower again? 😩 If your hot water runs out faster every month, sediment is probably building up in your tank. Left alone, it can crack the tank and flood your basement. A 30-minute flush fixes most of these. We do them every day, fixed price, same week. Book in bio.

AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

New on the menu: the maple pecan latte. 🍁 Real Ontario maple syrup, toasted pecans, our house espresso. It tastes like the first cold morning of fall, in a cup. Here until the leaves are gone. Come try it this week.

BAB: Before, After, Bridge

Before: a basement nobody wanted to go into. After: a dry, bright family room (swipe →). The bridge: a new sump pump, a waterproof membrane and 6 days of work. Thinking about your basement? Message us for a free assessment.

Calls to action

Every post needs one clear next step. Match it to the goal:

  • Engagement: "Save this for later." · "Send this to someone who needs it." · "Which one would you pick? Comment A or B."
  • Traffic: "Link in bio." · "Tap the link in Stories."
  • Action: "Show this post at the till." · "Call or book online today." · "DM us 'QUOTE'."

Platform tone

  • Instagram: short, warm, scannable. First line matters most (it's all people see).
  • TikTok: casual, spoken, on-screen text does the heavy lifting.
  • Facebook: friendly and local; questions and community.
  • LinkedIn: a strong first line, short paragraphs, a real lesson or story, no hashtag piles.

Hashtags and keywords

Use 3–5 relevant hashtags (Instagram itself recommends a small number) and put real keywords in the caption and on-screen text: "best latte in London Ontario" helps social search far more than #coffeelover.

Accessibility

Add alt text to images, captions to every video, use emojis sparingly (screen readers read each one aloud), and keep text on images readable (high contrast, large size).

2Worked example

Rewriting a Corner Grind caption.

Before: "Come try our new drink!!! ☕☕☕ #coffee #cafe #latte #coffeetime #coffeelover #instacoffee #yum..." (24 hashtags)

After:

Your new 3pm habit. 🍁 The maple pecan latte: real Ontario maple, toasted pecans, our house espresso, and oat milk if you like. Here until the end of November. 📍 Corner Grind, Richmond Row · Show this post for $2 off your first one.

londonontario #ldnont #maplelatte

Result: the post was saved 4× more than the previous drink launch, and 38 people showed it at the till in the first week.

★Tip

Read your first line out loud and ask, "Would I stop scrolling for this?" If the answer is "maybe", rewrite it. Hooks are where 80% of the result is decided.
