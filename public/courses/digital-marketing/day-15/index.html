Social media marketing & management · Day 15 of 30

The social media platform playbook

3.5 hours

Know what every major platform is good for, how its algorithm decides who sees a post, and which platforms each client actually needs.

Lesson 40 minExample 30 minTasks 140 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

"We need to be on every platform" is how small businesses burn out. Each platform has a different audience, format and purpose. Your job is to choose the two or three that matter for each client and do them properly.

How algorithms decide what people see

Modern feeds are mostly interest-based: platforms show posts to people likely to enjoy them, even if they don't follow the account. They test a post with a small group, measure the reaction, and show it to more people if it does well.

The signals that matter most across platforms:

  • Watch time and completion: did people watch to the end, or re-watch?
  • Shares and sends: did people send it to a friend? (One of the strongest signals.)
  • Saves: did they want to come back to it?
  • Meaningful comments: real conversation, not "nice pic".
  • Early engagement: how the first test group reacted.

The lesson: make content worth watching to the end, worth sending to a friend, or worth saving. Follower counts matter less than they used to.

A note for Canadian agencies: TikTok remains available to Canadian users and advertisers. A 2024 federal order to wind up TikTok's Canadian business was set aside in early 2026 and later allowed to proceed under conditions, so check current news before advising clients who are sensitive about the platform.

Choosing platforms for a client

Ask three questions:

  1. Where are their customers? (Check competitor accounts and ask current customers.)
  2. What can they realistically create? A plumber can film quick fix videos; a lawyer might prefer LinkedIn posts.
  3. What's the goal? Awareness (TikTok, Reels), trust (YouTube, LinkedIn), local action (Facebook, Google Business Profile), traffic (Pinterest).

Most local businesses do best with Instagram + Facebook (via Meta Business Suite) + Google Business Profile, adding TikTok or YouTube Shorts if they're comfortable on camera.

Social search

People increasingly search inside TikTok, Instagram and YouTube for recommendations ("best brunch london ontario"). Use clear keywords in captions, on-screen text, spoken words and alt text, just like SEO.

Riya's platform choices.

Client Platforms Why
Corner Grind Café Instagram, TikTok, Google Business Profile Visual product, students and young professionals nearby, people search "cafés to study in London" on TikTok
Northfield Plumbing Facebook, YouTube Shorts, Google Business Profile Homeowners 35–65 on Facebook, quick "fix it yourself or call us?" videos work on Shorts, reviews drive calls
Maple & Pixel (own agency) LinkedIn, Instagram Business owners check LinkedIn; Instagram shows the creative work

She deliberately says no to X and Pinterest for both clients: not where their customers are.

★Tip

Before deciding on a platform, ask the owner how many hours a week they can give you (for filming, approvals and photos). The best platform is the one the client can actually support.
