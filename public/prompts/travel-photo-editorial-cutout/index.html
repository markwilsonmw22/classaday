Travel photo to surreal magazine cutout

Turn an ordinary trip photo into a bold, surreal magazine-style cutout: you, cut out cleanly, on a flat colour page with one giant dreamlike detail.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

An Instagram trend report covering October 17–24, 2026 names a ChatGPT workflow that turns ordinary travel photos into surreal editorial cutouts as the strongest AI tutorial of the week. Those posts show the finished pictures first, then the quick how-to.

How to use it

  1. Pick a travel photo where you are clearly visible, head to toe if you can, with a landmark, beach or street behind you.
  2. Paste the main prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini and attach the photo. Ask for a 4:5 portrait image.
  3. Make two or three versions with different colours, then post them as a carousel.
  4. For a Reel, show the finished pictures first, then a few seconds of your screen with the prompt.

Prompts

Main prompt
Turn my travel photo into a surreal fashion magazine page. Cut me out cleanly, keeping my face, hair, outfit and pose exactly as they are, with a thin white paper border around me like a hand-cut sticker. Place me on a flat, bold background colour taken from the photo. Cut out one detail from the place I visited, like a building, a palm tree or a boat, and make it oversized and floating beside me, slightly out of scale. Add a soft drop shadow so the cutouts look like paper laid on the page. Keep it clean and minimal, with lots of empty space. Add a small, neat caption in the bottom corner with the city name and year. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: dreamy collage
Make a surreal collage from my travel photo. Cut me out with a clean white border and keep my face and clothes exactly the same. Put me on a soft pastel background. Around me, float a few cutout pieces of the trip at odd sizes: a giant coffee cup, a tiny version of the main landmark, a stamp-like ticket and a cloud. Some pieces overlap my shoulders like layered paper. Gentle shadows, matte paper texture, calm and playful. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: friends' trip
Use this photo of me and my friends (they've agreed to be in it). Cut each of us out with a white paper border and keep every face, outfit and pose exactly the same. Stand us in a row on a flat warm-coloured background, like a magazine cover. Behind us, place one huge cutout of the place we visited, cropped at the top of the page. Add soft shadows and a short caption with the place name and year. Clean, bright, editorial. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: small business
Turn this photo of our product (or our team at an outdoor event) into a surreal magazine-style page for [type of business]. Cut it out cleanly with a white paper border and keep it exactly as it is, including any labels. Place it on a flat background in our brand colour [colour]. Add one oversized cutout detail that hints at where it's made or used, like a mountain, a wheat stalk or a coffee bean, floating beside it. Soft shadows, lots of empty space, a small caption with our town name. Vertical 4:5.

Tips

  • A photo with your full outfit visible and a simple pose gives the cleanest cutout.
  • If your face changes, add "do not change my face at all" and try again.
  • Pick the background colour from the trip itself, like the sea, a wall or a sunset.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
