Finished dish first, recipe second

Open your food video on the best bite of the finished dish, then rewind to the recipe. AI writes the hook, the order of shots and the captions.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A weekly UGC trend report for October 17 to 24, 2026 names "payoff-first food" as a top format: the video opens on the finished dish before the recipe starts. The same report lists "the result-first command" as one of the hook styles working that week.

How to use it

  1. Use the main prompt with your recipe to get a hook line and a shot list that starts at the end.
  2. Film the payoff shot first while the food is hot: the cheese pull, the crunch, the steam, the first bite.
  3. Film the recipe steps in short, quick clips, then edit so the payoff plays in the first 2 seconds.
  4. Add the on-screen text from the prompt and end with the payoff shot once more.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm filming a short vertical recipe video that opens on the finished dish and only then shows how to make it. The recipe is: [paste your recipe or describe it]. Write: 1) three options for a one-line hook that tells viewers what to do, for example "Make these tonight" style, under 8 words; 2) the single best payoff shot to open on and how to film it with a phone; 3) a shot list of 6 to 8 quick recipe clips, each under 3 seconds; 4) short on-screen text for each clip. Use Canadian measurements and spelling.
Variation: fall comfort food
Suggest 8 easy fall or early-winter comfort dishes that look amazing in the first 2 seconds of a video (a stretch, a crunch, a pour or steam rising). For each, give the payoff shot, a 6-word hook and the number of ingredients. Keep them cheap and doable on a weeknight in Canada.
Variation: leftover Halloween candy
Give me 5 simple treats I can make with leftover Halloween candy. For each one, describe the payoff shot to open the video with, a short hook line and the steps in 5 lines or less. Keep it kid-friendly and safe for a home kitchen.
Variation: small business
I run a [café, bakery, food truck, restaurant or catering business]. My dish is [name and short description]. Write a payoff-first video plan: the opening shot of the finished dish, a 6-word hook, 5 quick behind-the-counter clips showing how we make it, and a closing line that tells people where and when they can get it. Keep it honest: no prices I haven't given you, no claims like "best in town".

Tips

  • The first 2 seconds decide everything. If the payoff shot isn't mouth-watering, reshoot it before you film the rest.
  • Use daylight from a window and film close. Food looks best with soft side light.
  • Keep the recipe clips fast. Viewers already know the ending, so they want the how, not the waiting.
  • Put the full recipe in the caption so people can save the post.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
