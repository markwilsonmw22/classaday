Social media marketing & management · Day 21 of 30

Social analytics, reporting and growth experiments

3.5 hours

Measure what matters on social media, report it clearly to clients, and run small experiments that improve results every month.

Lesson 40 minExample 30 minTasks 140 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

Clients renew when they see progress. Today you'll learn which numbers matter, how to report them simply, and how to keep improving with experiments instead of guesswork.

Metrics that matter (and vanity metrics)

Goal Metrics to watch Less useful on their own
Awareness Reach, views, non-follower reach, video watch time Impressions without reach
Engagement Saves, shares/sends, comments, engagement rate Likes
Traffic Link clicks, profile visits, website sessions from social (GA4) —
Leads / sales DMs that became bookings, offer redemptions, form leads, sales with UTMs Follower count

Engagement rate by reach = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach × 100. It's fairer than dividing by followers because it measures how people who actually saw the post reacted.

Where to find the numbers

  • Meta Business Suite insights (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok analytics (business account), YouTube Studio, LinkedIn page and post analytics.
  • Your scheduler's reports (Metricool, Later, Sprout).
  • GA4 for visits and conversions from social, using UTM links (Day 24).
  • Offer codes and "show this post" counts at the till: simple, honest offline tracking.

The monthly social report

One page plus a short Loom:

  1. Headline result: "Instagram drove 74 offer redemptions, up from 41."
  2. Key numbers vs last month: reach, engagement rate, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, leads/sales.
  3. Top 3 posts and why they worked.
  4. What didn't work and what we're changing.
  5. Next month's plan and experiments.

Growth experiments

Treat every month as a lab. Pick one thing to test at a time:

Hypothesis Test Measure
Face-to-camera hooks beat product shots 4 Reels each way Average 3-second retention and reach
Carousels with 8 slides get more saves than 5 Alternate for 4 weeks Saves per reach
Posting at 7am beats 12pm for the café Same content type, two time slots Reach in first 24 hours
A local keyword in captions increases non-follower reach Half the posts with, half without Non-follower reach

Write each test down: hypothesis, what you'll change, how long, and the result. After six months you'll have a playbook nobody else has.

Growth tactics that work for local businesses

  • Collabs: joint posts with neighbouring businesses (the café + the bookshop next door).
  • Local giveaways with a clear, legal set of rules (in Canada, contests have specific legal requirements; check them before running one).
  • Respond with video: answer popular comments with short videos.
  • Pin the best three posts to the top of the profile.
  • Stories with polls and questions to boost interaction with followers.

Try it yourself

Pull the numbers for one of your posts from Instagram or TikTok insights.

2Worked example

Corner Grind, month two report (headline section).

74 people redeemed the Instagram offer this month (up from 41). Reach was 48,200 (+31%), with 62% from non-followers. Saves rose 2.4× thanks to the "study spots" carousel.

Top post: "We asked 20 students where they study" Reel: 21,000 views, 340 shares. Flop: the latte art time-lapse: good views, almost no saves or visits. Pretty, but no reason to act. Experiment result: face-to-camera hooks had 38% higher average watch time than product shots across 8 Reels. We'll open most Reels with a person next month. Next month: holiday gift card push, two creator collabs, test 7am vs 12pm posting.

The owner forwards it to her business partner with one line: "Keep going."
