Content & marketing · Day 20 of 30

Filming and editing ads with your phone

4 hours

Film and edit your first three UGC-style videos, each built on one of your test angles.

Lesson 20 minExample 15 minTasks 205 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

The ads that work best in this business don't look like ads. They look like a real person showing a real product on their phone. You can make them yourself today.

The five beats

  1. Hook (0–3s): a moment, question or bold statement that stops the scroll. Show the most visual part of the problem or result immediately.
  2. Problem (3–8s): show the pain clearly.
  3. Demo (8–18s): the product solving it, close up.
  4. Proof (18–24s): the result, a reaction, a comparison.
  5. Offer + CTA (24–30s): what to do and why now (guarantee, bundle, season).

Hook ideas

  • "POV: you own a husky and own black clothes"
  • "I was paying $90 a month for this"
  • Start mid-action: the button press with fur sliding off
  • "Things I wish I'd bought sooner as a dog owner"
  • A surprising number: "This came off ONE dog"

Write at least ten hooks for each video. You can film one body and swap hooks to create several ads.

Filming setup

  • Vertical 9:16, 1080p or 4K, 30fps
  • Natural light from a window in front of you
  • Clean background; real home settings are good
  • Tap to lock focus and exposure on the product
  • Film each shot 3 times; film more close-ups than you think you need
  • Record 2–3 seconds before and after each action, for easy cutting

If samples haven't arrived yet, script and storyboard today, and film the moment they arrive.

Editing in CapCut

  1. Cut every pause. Pace should feel fast.
  2. Add captions (auto-captions, then correct mistakes). Most people watch without sound.
  3. Add a text hook on screen in the first frame.
  4. Use a trending or royalty-free sound at low volume under any voiceover.
  5. Export at 1080×1920.

Creators

When you have budget, pay creators on platforms that connect brands with UGC creators to make videos with your product. Send them a short brief with your angle, hook ideas and a shot list. Make sure you have rights to use their videos as ads.

2Worked example

Priya's script for angle 1 ("black leggings, white husky"):

Time Shot On-screen text / voice
0–3s Close-up of white fur all over black leggings "POV: husky owner who only owns black clothes"
3–8s Lint roller giving up, fur on the sofa "I was vacuuming twice a day"
8–18s Brushing the dog, then the click, fur sliding off "Then I tried this. Watch the button"
18–24s Big pile of fur, clean sofa, happy dog "This is from ONE session"
24–30s Product in hand, store name "Shedding season kit, link in bio · 30-day guarantee"

She films three versions, each with a different hook, using the same middle section.

3Today's tasks

✓By tonight you'll have

At least one finished ad video with two hook variations, plus scripts for the other two.

★Tip

Film yourself or your hands, not a polished product spin. Videos that feel shot by a real customer consistently outperform glossy studio footage on social platforms.
