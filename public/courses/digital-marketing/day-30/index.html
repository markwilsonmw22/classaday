Systems, AI and scaling your agency

3.5 hours

Turn your work into systems, use automation and AI responsibly, build a team, track agency health, and write your 90-day growth plan.

Lesson 35 minExample 25 minTasks 150 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

You've learned every core service an agency offers. The final skill is building a business that doesn't depend on you doing everything yourself.

SOPs: write it once, delegate forever

A standard operating procedure is a step-by-step checklist for a repeated task. Easiest method: record a Loom while you do the task, then write the steps underneath. Start with:

  • New client onboarding · GBP optimisation · Monthly SEO report · Social content month · Google Ads weekly check · Review reply routine · Offboarding

Store them in Notion or Google Docs, one page each, and update them when something changes.

Automation

Use Zapier or Make to remove busywork:

When this happens Do this automatically
A contract is signed Create the client folder, board from template, and send the welcome email
A lead fills a client's form Add to their CRM, text the owner, send the customer a confirmation
A new 5-star review arrives Post it to the client's Slack and add it to a "testimonials" sheet
The 1st of the month Create report tasks for every client
An invoice is overdue Send a polite reminder

AI in agency work

AI speeds up research, outlines, first drafts, ad variations, reporting summaries, scripts and code. Rules that protect your clients and your reputation:

  • A human checks everything before it reaches a client or goes live.
  • Confidentiality: don't paste client private data into AI tools unless your agreement and the tool's business terms allow it.
  • Be open with clients about how you use AI, and charge for outcomes, not keystrokes.
  • Originality: add real experience, data and voice. Generic AI content is easy to spot and easy to replace.

Building a team

Hire in this order, usually as contractors first: a video editor or designer → a virtual assistant (scheduling, reporting, admin) → specialists (ads, SEO, copywriting) → an account manager. In Canada, whether someone is a contractor or an employee depends on the real working relationship (control, tools, chance of profit or loss, integration), not just what the contract says, so check the CRA's guidance or ask an accountant.

White-label partners (other agencies who do work under your brand) can fill gaps, such as web development or link building, while you focus on clients.

Agency health metrics

Metric Meaning Healthy target
MRR Monthly recurring revenue from retainers Growing month on month
Churn % of clients lost per month Under 3–5%
Average revenue per client MRR ÷ clients Rising as you upsell and raise prices
Gross margin (Revenue − delivery costs) ÷ revenue 50%+
Utilisation Billable hours ÷ available hours 70–80% (leave room for sales and systems)
Client lifetime Average months a client stays 12+ months

Raise prices for new clients as demand grows, and review existing clients' pricing once a year with notice.

Your 90-day plan

Three blocks of 30 days. Each with one main goal, three key actions and one number. Keep it on one page and review it every Friday.

2Worked example

Maple & Pixel at month nine.

  • 7 clients, MRR $10,900, churn 1 client in 9 months (a café that closed).
  • Team: a part-time video editor (contractor) and a virtual assistant 10 hours a week for scheduling and report prep.
  • 23 SOPs in Notion; onboarding fully automated through Zapier.
  • AI: first drafts of reports, ad variations and blog outlines; every output checked by Riya.
  • Gross margin: 58%. Riya's own hours: down from 50 to 38 a week.

Her next 90 days: (1) Land 3 more home-service clients through the mini-audit method → target MRR $15,000. (2) Package a "Local Services Ads setup" offer. (3) Raise prices for new clients by 15%.
