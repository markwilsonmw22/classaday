The LOVE headstand: your legs make the V between big wall letters

You go upside down in front of the letters L, O and E, open your legs into a V, and the wall spells LOVE. No real headstand needed.

KlingVeoHiggsfield

Why it's trending

AI Trend Club lists a LOVE handstand trend traced to a TikTok post from November 14, 2026. A November 21 check counted about 13.6M views and 1.6M likes on the original. A person holds a headstand in front of the letters L, O and E and opens their legs into the V. People now make it from one photo with AI, so anyone can do the pose. We haven't covered it before.

How to use it

  1. Pick a clear, full-body photo of yourself in comfy clothes you can move in.
  2. Paste the main prompt into an image-to-video tool with your photo.
  3. Check the letters read L, O, then the V, then E from left to right. Regenerate if they shuffle.
  4. Post it and say it's made with AI.

Prompts

Main prompt: plain room, LOVE on the wall
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face, hair, body and clothes exactly the same. Make a 12-second vertical clip with a fixed camera in a plain, bright room with a soft carpet. On the wall behind them are three large red letters, left to right: L, O, a wide empty gap, then E. Seconds 0 to 3: the person stands in the gap, smiles and places their hands and head on the carpet. Seconds 3 to 7: they rise slowly into a steady headstand, legs together, pointing straight up. Seconds 7 to 10: they open their legs into a wide V, so the wall now reads LOVE with their legs as the V. They hold it still. Seconds 10 to 12: they bring their legs together and come down gently, then sit and grin at the camera. Smooth, realistic motion. No other text, no logos.
Variation: holiday living room
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face and features the same. Make a 12-second vertical clip with a fixed camera in a cosy living room with a decorated tree in the corner and warm string lights. Large gold letters on the wall read, left to right: L, O, a wide gap, then E. The person, in matching holiday pyjamas, goes into a calm headstand in the gap and opens their legs into a V so the wall spells LOVE. They hold it for two seconds, then roll down and laugh. Warm evening light. No other text, no logos.
Variation: the wobbly try
Use the adult in this photo. Keep their face and features the same. Make a 10-second vertical clip with a fixed camera in a plain room with large red letters on the wall: L, O, a wide gap, then E. The person goes into a headstand in the gap and opens their legs into a V to spell LOVE. They hold it for one second, wobble, and tip slowly sideways onto a big soft cushion. They lie there giving a thumbs up to the camera. Gentle, funny, nobody gets hurt. No other text, no logos.
Variation for a small business: yoga or fitness studio
Use this photo of our instructor [upload]. Keep their face exactly the same. Make a 12-second vertical clip with a fixed camera in a bright studio with wooden floors and mats. Large letters on the wall read, left to right: L, O, a wide gap, then E. The instructor rolls out a mat in the gap, goes into a steady headstand and opens their legs into a V so the wall spells LOVE. They hold it, come down smoothly and wave the camera in. Calm, clean light. No other text, no logos or brand names.

Tips

  • Spell out the letter order and the gap in your prompt. AI tools often mix up letters on walls.
  • A fixed camera keeps the word lined up. Ask for it every time.
  • Don't try a real headstand for the clip unless you've trained for it. That's what the AI is for.
  • Only use photos of adults who said yes, and say it's made with AI.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
