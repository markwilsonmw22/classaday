Our story

A kitchen built around one fire

We started with a single wood oven and a promise to cook only what the farms around us were growing. Seven years later, the oven is still lit every day.

Our cooks work with whole animals, heritage grains and vegetables picked that week. Nothing fancy, just good ingredients and patience.

— Mara & Theo, owners
