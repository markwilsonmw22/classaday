On-brand posts from your saved brand style

Save your colours, fonts and logo once, then ask AI for a week of matching posts: Halloween, fall sale and behind-the-scenes.

GeminiChatGPT

Why it's trending

On October 20, 2026, Google announced a small business hub in the Gemini app. One part lets you save your brand style (website, colours, fonts) once so its image model reuses it for on-brand visuals. Google says it is rolling out to Gemini app users worldwide in the coming days.

How to use it

  1. Gather your brand basics: your logo, two or three colours (hex codes if you have them), your fonts and your website link.
  2. In Gemini, look for the business area and save your brand style there. If you don't see it yet, upload your logo and a screenshot of your website at the start of the chat instead.
  3. Use the main prompt for one post, then the series prompt for a full week.
  4. Check every image: spelling, prices, dates and your logo. Fix one thing at a time.

Prompts

Main prompt
Use my saved brand style: my colours, fonts and logo. Make a square Instagram post for my business, [what you sell]. Theme: a cozy fall weekend. Show [one real product or service] on a wooden table with a few maple leaves and a warm drink nearby. Soft afternoon window light. Put a short headline at the top in my brand font: "[your headline]". Place my logo small in the bottom corner. Lots of clean space. Do not change my logo or invent new colours.
Variation: one week of posts
Use my saved brand style. Plan and make five square posts for next week for my business, [what you sell]. 1) A cheerful Halloween post with a pumpkin and my product, no scary faces. 2) A behind-the-scenes shot of hands at work. 3) A simple quote card in my brand colours with this line: "[your line]". 4) A product close-up on a plain background in one of my colours. 5) A thank-you post for my customers. Keep the same fonts, colours and logo placement on all five so they look like a set.
Variation: no saved style yet
Here is my logo and a screenshot of my website. Pick out my main colours and the feel of my fonts, and list them back to me first. Then make a square post for my business, [what you sell], announcing [your real news or offer]. Use only those colours, keep my logo exactly as it is, and use plenty of clean space. Short headline, no more than six words.

Tips

  • Only announce real offers and real prices. Never make up reviews, star ratings or customer numbers.
  • Ask for a set of five at once. Matching posts make your feed look planned.
  • AI can still bend logos and misspell words. Zoom in before you post.
  • Keep your brand colours to two or three. Too many makes every post look busy.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
