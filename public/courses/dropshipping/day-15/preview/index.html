Build the store · Day 15 of 30

Theme and homepage

Members
4 hours

Install a fast theme, apply your brand, and build a homepage that tells visitors in five seconds who you are and what to buy.

Lesson 20 minExample 10 minTasks 210 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

Most visitors from ads land on a product page, not your homepage. But people who are deciding whether to trust you click the logo to check you're real. Your homepage is your shop window: clean, fast and focused.

Start with Shopify's free Dawn theme or another free Online Store 2.0 theme. Free themes are fast, well maintained and work with most apps. Paid themes can come later. Avoid themes that load lots of animations; speed matters more on mobile.

Theme settings: upload your logo and favicon, set colours and fonts from your brand kit, set button style (rounded or square, but consistent), and make sure body text is at least 16px.
