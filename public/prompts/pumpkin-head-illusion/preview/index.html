Pumpkin head illusion photo

A forced-perspective trick photo: a pumpkin held up close lines up perfectly so it looks like your friend's head.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

A TikTok trend roundup updated on October 14, 2026 lists a pumpkin head illusion: one friend holds a pumpkin close to the camera so it sits where the other friend's head should be. People shoot it in person; this pack makes an AI version from a photo of you and a friend.

How to use it

  1. Take a photo of two people outside, one standing close to the camera and one a few steps back. Both people should agree to the edit.
  2. Upload it and use the main prompt. Say clearly who holds the pumpkin and whose head it replaces.
  3. Check the edges. If the pumpkin floats or the neck looks odd, ask for a fix in a follow-up message.

Tips

  • The trick works best when the back person stands still and faces the camera, so pick a photo like that.
  • Ask for the pumpkin to be a little bigger than the head it covers. It hides the edges better.
  • Turn it into a short reveal: post the trick photo, then a real photo of both of you laughing.
  • Pumpkin farms, garden centres and bakeries can use their own pumpkins or pies as the prop.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
