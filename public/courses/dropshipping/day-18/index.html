Build the store · Day 18 of 30

Pricing, offers and a full test order

3.5 hours

Set prices with psychology in mind, add offers that raise average order value, and test the whole buying journey.

Lesson 15 minExample 10 minTasks 185 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

With the same ad spend, a store with a $45 average order makes much more than one with a $33 average order. Today you build offers that raise order value, then test everything end to end.

Pricing tips

  • Charm prices ($32.99, $29.95) still help on impulse products.
  • Price in round brackets with competitors, but compete on offer, not on cutting price.
  • Use a compare-at price only when it's honest (Day 16).

Offers that raise order value

  1. Quantity breaks: Buy 1 $32.99 · Buy 2 save 15% · Buy 3 save 20%. Great for consumables, gifts and multi-pet households.
  2. Bundles: pair complementary products (brush + paw cleaner).
  3. Free shipping threshold: set it just above your single-item price (e.g. $45 when the item is $32.99).
  4. Post-purchase upsell: a one-click add-on on the thank-you page, e.g. the paw cleaner at 20% off.
  5. Free digital bonus: a PDF guide costs you nothing to deliver and adds real value.

Check every offer in your Day 2 calculator so a discount doesn't wipe out margin.

The full test order

Remove the store password temporarily (or use Shopify's test mode / a bogus gateway) and place a real order with your own card. Check:

  • The page loads fast on mobile data
  • Variant and quantity selection work
  • The offer price applies correctly
  • Shipping shows the right rate and wording
  • Taxes are correct for your setup
  • Confirmation email arrives with your branding and delivery window
  • The order appears in your supplier app ready to fulfil

Then refund the order to yourself.

Speed check

Run your store through PageSpeed Insights or Shopify's speed report. Big images and too many apps are the usual causes of slow stores. Compress images and remove unused apps.

Launch checklist

Before going live: all links work, no "lorem ipsum" or supplier text left, policies linked, contact email works, favicon set, and you've checked every page on your phone.

2Worked example

Hearth & Hound offers:

  • Brush: 1 for $32.99, 2 for $55.99 (save 15%).
  • Bundle: "Shedding Season Kit" brush + paw cleaner for $54.99 (separately $62.98).
  • Free shipping over $45 shown in the announcement bar (all orders are free anyway in her setup, so she replaces it with the guarantee).
  • Post-purchase: paw cleaner at 20% off, one click.
  • Bonus: "The 10-Minute Home Grooming Guide" PDF in the confirmation email.

Her test order revealed one problem: the US shipping rate name still said "8–14 days". She fixes it to "4–8 business days".
