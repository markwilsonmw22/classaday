Pink fur coat photoshoot: an overhead glam shoot in your own living room

Turn one photo into a glossy, shot-from-above fashion moment: you on the living-room floor in an oversized pink faux-fur coat and dark sunglasses.

ChatGPTGeminiKlingHiggsfield

Why it's trending

FIKKU's roundup of November 2026 TikTok trends (published November 7, 2026) lists the pink fur coat pose among the ten trends people are remaking most this month: a living-room photoshoot shot from above, in an oversized pink coat and sunglasses. The page gives no view counts.

How to use it

  1. Pick one clear photo of yourself where your face is easy to see.
  2. Paste the main prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini to make the still photo.
  3. Want it to move? Put the finished photo into an image-to-video tool with the video variation.
  4. Post the photo, the clip, or both, and label it as made with AI.

Prompts

Main prompt: the overhead glam shot
Using the person in this photo, make a fashion photo shot from directly above. They lie back on a cream rug in a bright, cosy living room, wrapped in a huge, fluffy, pale pink faux-fur coat that spreads out around them. They wear dark oval sunglasses and look up at the camera with a relaxed, confident half-smile, one hand resting near their face. A coffee mug and a stack of magazines sit on the rug beside them. Soft window light, gentle shadows, crisp detail in the fur. Keep their face and hair exactly like the photo. Vertical, magazine-cover feel, no text.
Variation: make it move
Make a vertical video from this photo, shot from directly above. The person in the pink faux-fur coat lies still for a beat, then slowly lowers their sunglasses with one finger, raises an eyebrow at the camera and pushes the glasses back up. The camera drifts down a little closer. The fur moves softly. Keep their face, coat and room exactly like the photo. Soft window light, 6 seconds, no text.
Variation: best friends shoot
Using the two people in these photos, make an overhead fashion photo of them lying head to head on a cream living-room rug, both in oversized fluffy faux-fur coats, one pale pink and one mint green. Both wear dark sunglasses. One gives a cool stare, the other is mid-laugh. Bright, soft daylight, crisp fur detail. Keep both faces and hair exactly like the photos. Vertical, no text.
Variation: small business
Using the person in this photo, make an overhead fashion photo of them lying on a cream rug in a bright living room, wearing [our coat, scarf or sweater, described in colour and texture] and dark sunglasses. Around them on the rug, place [three of our products] in a neat curve so they're easy to see. Soft window light, magazine-cover feel. Keep their face exactly like the photo. No logos, no text, vertical.

Tips

  • Say "shot from directly above". It's what makes the look.
  • Ask for "crisp fur detail". Without it, the coat can look like a blurry blob.
  • Pick a coat colour that pops against a light rug: pale pink, mint or lilac.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
