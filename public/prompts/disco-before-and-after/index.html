Disco before-and-after: your ordinary room, turned into a 1970s dance floor

Snap a plain everyday photo, then have AI turn the same scene into a glittering 1970s disco version for a quick before-and-after Reel.

ChatGPTGeminiKlingVeo

Why it's trending

Buffer's list of trending Instagram sounds for November 2026, published November 3, names a classic disco song used for "disco before-and-after transitions". This pack does the "after" with AI, using your own photo as the "before".

How to use it

  1. Take a plain, well-lit photo of an ordinary spot: your living room, kitchen, desk or a selfie in a regular outfit.
  2. Attach it to ChatGPT or Gemini and paste the main prompt to get the disco version.
  3. Optional: upload the disco image to Kling or Veo as the first frame with the video prompt to make it move.
  4. In your editing app, show the plain photo for 1 to 2 seconds, then cut to the disco version on the beat of a sound from the app's library.

Prompts

Main prompt
Turn the attached photo into a 1970s disco version of the same scene. Keep the room layout, the furniture and any people in the same places. Do not change faces or body shapes. Add a big mirror ball on the ceiling throwing little spots of light everywhere, a glowing light-up floor in pink, orange and purple squares, shimmering streamers and a little haze in the air. Warm, saturated colours, soft glow on everything, slight film grain like an old photo. No text, no logos.
Variation: me in disco clothes
Edit the attached selfie into a 1970s disco portrait. Keep my face, hair colour and expression exactly as they are. Dress me in a shiny sequined outfit with wide flared trousers or a wrap dress, and big confident jewellery. Put me in front of a mirror ball with sparkles of light on my face and a hazy, glowing pink and gold background. Slight film grain. No text.
Video prompt
Use the attached image as the first frame. Five seconds. The mirror ball turns slowly and spots of light sweep across the room. The floor squares light up in a simple pattern. A little haze drifts through. Keep the room and any people the same. No text, no new people, no fast camera moves.
Variation: small business
Turn the attached photo of our [shop counter / salon chair / café table / product shelf] into a 1970s disco version. Keep the products and layout exactly the same so customers recognise them, and keep our own sign as it is without changing the wording. Add a mirror ball overhead throwing sparkles across everything, a glowing light-up floor in pink, orange and purple, shimmering streamers and a soft haze. Warm, saturated colours, slight film grain. No extra text, no other logos, no people.

Tips

  • The plainer the "before" photo, the bigger the payoff. A messy Monday kitchen works great.
  • Shoot the before photo straight on and keep the same framing so the cut lines up.
  • If faces change, say "keep every face exactly as it is in the photo" and try again.
  • Only include friends or family who are happy to be posted.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
