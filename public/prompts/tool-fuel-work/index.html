The tool, the fuel, the work: your day in three photos

Three slides: the tool you use, the fuel that keeps you going, then the work you made. AI turns your phone snaps into a matching set.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

SocialBee's Instagram trends roundup, updated October 29, 2026, lists "The tool, the fuel, the work" as a new trend: three clips in a row showing the tool you work with, the fuel that keeps you going (often coffee or a snack) and the finished work. It adds a fuel shot to the tools-and-process format from October.

How to use it

  1. Take three quick phone photos: your main tool, your go-to drink or snack, and something you finished today.
  2. Attach all three to ChatGPT or Gemini and paste the main prompt.
  3. Check that each object still looks like yours, then post the three images as a carousel or a short Reel with the labels "the tool", "the fuel", "the work".

Prompts

Main prompt
I've attached three photos: my tool, my fuel and my finished work. Turn them into a matching set of three vertical 4:5 images. Keep every object exactly as it is in my photos: same shape, colour, wear and details. Give all three the same look: shot from slightly above on the same warm wooden table, soft morning window light from the left, a little shadow, a calm cream background. Add a small, clean lowercase label in the top-left corner of each one: "the tool", "the fuel", "the work". Same font, size and spot on all three. No other text.
Variation: no photos yet
I'm a [your job or hobby, for example: home baker, student, painter]. Make three matching vertical 4:5 photos. 1: the tool I use most, close up on a tidy desk. 2: my fuel, a mug of tea steaming beside a half-eaten muffin. 3: the work, the finished thing I make, shown proudly in the centre. Same soft daylight, same neutral colours and same camera height in all three, so they look like one set. Small lowercase labels in the top-left: "the tool", "the fuel", "the work".
Variation: small business
I run a small [type of business, for example: flower shop]. I've attached photos of our main tool, the team's coffee order and a finished product we sold today. Make three matching vertical 4:5 images. Keep our products and tools exactly as they look in the photos. Bright, friendly daylight, the same light-grey counter in all three, a few loose ingredients or offcuts at the edges. Small labels in the top-left: "the tool", "the fuel", "the work". No logos or extra text.
Caption helper
Write 5 short Instagram captions for a three-slide post called "the tool, the fuel, the work". My tool is [tool], my fuel is [drink or snack] and the work is [what I made]. Keep each caption under 15 words, friendly and a little funny. End with a question that asks people to share their own fuel.

Tips

  • Be honest with the fuel. A slightly sad granola bar gets more comments than a perfect latte.
  • Shoot all three photos from the same height and in the same room so the AI has less to change.
  • If a label comes out misspelled, ask for the image again without text and add the labels in your editing app.
  • As a Reel, cut on the beat: one second for the tool, one for the fuel, then hold longer on the work.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
