About this course

A 30-day build in six modules. You'll pick a niche, prove a product, line up suppliers, build a Shopify store that converts, run your first ads and learn to read the numbers before you spend more.

What you'll be able to do

  • Work out profit per order and your break-even ad cost before spending a dollar
  • Find products with real demand using TikTok, Meta Ad Library and marketplaces
  • Vet suppliers, order samples and set honest delivery promises
  • Build a Shopify store with policies, payments, shipping and taxes set up properly
  • Write product pages that answer every objection a buyer has
  • Film and edit short UGC-style ads with only a phone
  • Launch a structured test campaign and decide what to kill or scale
  • Set up email flows that recover abandoned carts
  • Process orders, handle refunds and keep chargebacks low
  • Track daily profit and plan your next 90 days

Who it's for

  • Complete beginners who want a structured first business online
  • People with a job who can give 3–4 hours a day, or half that on the slower pace
  • Anyone who tried before, got lost in random videos and wants an order to follow

What you'll need

  • A laptop and a phone that films in 1080p or better
  • A Shopify account (free trial to start, then a paid plan)
  • Around $500–$1,500 for samples, apps and ad testing. Day 1 shows how to split it
  • A separate bank card for the business
