"News alert: toggle on": tease your announcement

A big on/off switch flicks on to tease something new. AI designs the switch graphics in your colours and writes the reveal lines.

ChatGPTGeminiCanva

Why it's trending

Later's Instagram Reels trends roundup, updated October 30, 2026, lists "Toggle on" as a new trend that week: creators tease an announcement with the line "News alert: toggle on."

How to use it

  1. Decide what you're announcing. It can be big or small: a new hobby, a move, a new menu item, holiday hours or a launch date.
  2. Use the main prompt to make two matching images: the switch off, then the switch on with your news. Use the writing prompt if you want a few reveal lines to choose from.
  3. In a Reel or TikTok, show the "off" image for a moment, then cut to "on" on a beat. Add a third slide with the details if you need it.
  4. Keep the caption short and let the switch do the work. Answer questions in the comments.

Prompts

Main prompt: off and on
Make two matching square images for an announcement tease. Image 1: a large, rounded on/off toggle switch in the centre of a clean [your colour] background, switched off and grey, with small text above it that reads "News alert". Image 2: exactly the same layout, but the switch is now on and glowing [accent colour], and below it in bold, friendly letters it says "[your news in five words or fewer]". Flat, modern style. Keep the switch, fonts and position identical in both images. No phone brand, logo or app icons.
Variation: switch in real life
Make a realistic close-up photo of a hand flicking a chunky, glossy toggle switch on a wooden table. Soft daylight, shallow depth of field. The switch is mounted on a small panel with a tiny label that reads "[your news]". Make two versions: one with the switch off, and one with it on and a small warm light glowing beside it. Same angle and lighting in both. No logos or brand names.
Variation: write my reveal lines
I'm posting a short "news alert, toggle on" video to tease some news: [describe your news]. Write 5 options for the on-screen text that appears when the switch flips on. Each one should be under eight words, plain and upbeat, with no hype, fake urgency or made-up numbers. Then write a one-line caption that invites questions.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business]. Make three matching vertical images for a Reel. Image 1: a big rounded toggle switch, switched off, on a [brand colour] background, with "News alert" above it. Image 2: the same switch flipped on and glowing, with "[your real news, like 'Holiday hours are here']" below it. Image 3: the same background with a simple list of the real details: [dates, hours or item names]. Clean, flat style. Don't add prices, discounts, countdown timers or any logo.

Tips

  • Check the words on every slide. AI still misspells short text now and then, so fix it before you post.
  • Keep the switch in the same spot on every slide. If it jumps around, the flick won't feel smooth.
  • Only announce real news with real dates. A tease works because people trust the reveal.
  • Canva is handy for swapping in your exact brand colours and fonts after the AI makes the first version.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
