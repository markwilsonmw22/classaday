1% to 100%: the recharge before-and-after

Show yourself, your pet or your space at 1% battery, then fully charged at 100%. AI makes the battery overlays and the "after" shot.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

Later's Instagram Reels trends roundup, updated October 30, 2026, lists "Recharged" as a new trend that week: a before-and-after Reel that goes from 1% to 100%, opening on a low-battery hook.

How to use it

  1. Pick your before-and-after. It can be you before and after coffee, your dog before and after a walk, a messy desk before and after a tidy, or a shop before and after opening.
  2. Take the real "before" photo or clip. Then use the main prompt to add a low-battery icon, or film the real "after" and add a full-battery icon to it.
  3. Put the two shots back to back in a Reel or TikTok. Show the 1% shot for a second, then cut to 100% on a beat.
  4. Add a short hook on screen, like "Down to 1%. Watch this." Keep the caption short.

Prompts

Main prompt: add the battery overlay
Here are two photos of me. Photo 1 is my "before": tired, slumped, low energy. Photo 2 is my "after": awake and ready to go. Don't change my face, body or the room. Add a large, simple phone-style battery icon in the top corner of each photo. On photo 1, the battery is almost empty, with a thin red sliver and "1%" next to it. On photo 2, the battery is full and green with "100%" next to it. Use a clean, flat icon with no phone brand or logo. Keep everything else exactly as it is.
Variation: make the 100% shot
This photo shows me at the end of a long day, looking drained, with a small red battery icon reading "1%" in the top corner. Make a matching "100%" version: same person, same face, same outfit, same room and camera angle, but now I'm sitting up straight, smiling, with a hot drink in my hand and warm morning light through the window. Change the battery icon to full and green, reading "100%". Keep it looking like a real photo.
Variation: pet edition
Here is a photo of my [dog or cat] flopped out asleep. Add a simple flat battery icon above its head, nearly empty and red, labelled "1%". Then make a second image of the same pet, same markings and same room, now wide awake and mid-zoomie with its ears up, with a full green battery icon labelled "100%". Realistic photo style, no logos.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, like a café, salon or repair shop]. Here are two real photos of our space: one before we open, dark and empty, and one during the day, open and busy. Don't change the space or add customers who aren't there. Add a simple flat battery icon in the top corner of each: red and "1%" on the before photo, green and "100%" on the open photo. Leave any signs as they are and don't add prices, sale banners or extra text.

Tips

  • Use your own real before-and-after where you can. AI is best used for the battery icons, not for faking results.
  • Shoot both photos from the same spot and angle. The cut feels much more satisfying when only the energy changes.
  • If the AI changes your face, upload the original again and say "keep my face exactly the same, only add the icon".
  • Business accounts should pick a sound from the app's commercial library so the Reel isn't muted.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
