I took the chance: a montage of the leaps that changed things

Cut your own clips of big decisions, like a move, a new job or opening a shop, into a fast montage, with AI writing the short on-screen lines.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

CreatorDB's Instagram audio chart for the week of November 4, 2026 lists a rising "Take a Chance Growth Montage" format with about 111,000 Reels: a lyric about taking a chance over clips of career changes, moves and new businesses. It says brands and entrepreneurs use it to show risks taken and milestones reached.

How to use it

  1. Collect 5 to 8 short clips or photos of real turning points: the day you decided, the first step, the hard part and where you are now.
  2. Paste the main prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude and fill in your story to get one short line per clip.
  3. Put the clips in order in Reels or CapCut, add one line on each and cut on the beat of an upbeat trending sound.
  4. Keep the whole thing under 20 seconds and end on today.

Prompts

Main prompt (text)
Help me write on-screen text for a short Reel montage about a risk I took. My story: [what I decided, e.g. moved from Winnipeg to Halifax / left my job to go back to school / started baking for money]. I have [6] clips: [describe each clip in a few words, in order]. Write one line per clip, under 8 words each. The first line is the moment I almost said no. The middle lines show the small, real steps. The last line is where I am now. Warm and honest, no bragging, no promises about money. Canadian spelling.
Hook ideas
Write 10 short opening lines for a Reel that shows a risk I took and what came after. Each under 10 words. Mix styles: a question, a plain fact, a small confession. Examples of the tone I want: "A year ago I almost didn't send the email." Keep them true to a normal person's life, not a movie. Canadian spelling.
Variation: small business
Write on-screen text for a montage about how my small business started. Business: [type, town]. Clips in order: [e.g. kitchen table sketches, first market stall, empty shop on signing day, opening morning, a regular customer's order today]. One line per clip, under 8 words. Show real steps and real dates if I give them. Don't invent sales numbers, awards or customer quotes. End with a soft thank-you to the people who came by. Canadian spelling.

Tips

  • Use only your own footage. Old phone clips with bad lighting make it feel more real.
  • Ask before showing friends, family or customers in your clips.
  • Keep the numbers true. If you share a date or a milestone, make sure it really happened.
  • Business accounts should pick a sound that's cleared for commercial use.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
