World Kindness Day: "pass it on" cards to leave for someone

Design a small printable card to tuck under a windshield wiper, in a lunch bag or on a neighbour's door for World Kindness Day, Friday, November 13.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

World Kindness Day falls on Friday, November 13, 2026, according to awareness-day calendars. This is a date to plan for, not a viral format. It's a different idea from our thank-you list pack: these cards are for leaving a small surprise for someone else.

How to use it

  1. Pick who the cards are for: a neighbour, a co-worker, a stranger at the bus stop, or your customers.
  2. Paste the main prompt into an image tool and ask for a sheet of four cards on one page.
  3. Print on card stock, cut them out and write a short line by hand on the back.
  4. Leave them somewhere they'll be found. If you post about it, share the card design, not the person who found it.

Prompts

Main prompt: a sheet of four kindness cards
Design a printable letter-size sheet with four matching small cards, each the size of a postcard, with thin dashed cut lines between them. Style: warm and hand-made, soft watercolour shapes in cream, sage green and blush pink, with small doodled hearts, leaves and stars. Each card has one short line in friendly hand-lettered text: "Someone hopes your day goes well.", "This is your sign to take a break.", "You're doing better than you think." and "Pass it on: do one kind thing today." Leave a blank area at the bottom of each card for a handwritten note. Clean white margins, easy to read, no logos.
Variation: Canadian winter neighbour card
Design one printable card, postcard size, to leave on a neighbour's door after a snowfall. Show a cosy snowy street at dusk with a shovelled front walk, warm lights in the windows and a small toque hanging on a fence post. Hand-lettered text: "I shovelled your walk. No need to thank me. Just pass it on." Soft blues and warm yellow, simple flat illustration style, plenty of empty space at the bottom for a short handwritten note. No logos.
Variation: 13 small acts for your week (text)
World Kindness Day is November 13. Write me 13 small acts of kindness I could actually do this week. My week looks like this: [for example: office job, bus commute, two kids, a dog, a neighbour who lives alone]. Each one should take under 10 minutes, cost under $5 or nothing, and need no social media. Write each in one short sentence. Mix in a few for strangers, a few for people I know, and one for myself.
Variation: small business order insert
Design a small printable card, 4 by 6 inches, to tuck into customer orders this week for World Kindness Day. Style: clean and warm, off-white background, simple line drawings of a small gift box and a heart in [my brand colour]. Big hand-lettered text: "Thank you for shopping small." Smaller text underneath: "Pass the kindness on: hold a door, send a nice text, or say hi to someone new." Leave space at the bottom for my shop name, which I'll add myself. No logos, no discount codes.

Tips

  • Check every word on the card before you print. Image tools still misspell short phrases sometimes.
  • Print on thicker card stock so the cards survive a windshield or a lunch bag.
  • Keep the message about the other person. Cards that ask for a follow or a sale feel less kind.
  • Don't film strangers finding your cards without asking. Share the cards, not their reactions.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
