On the house: the ice cream counter skit

Two photos become a short skit at a retro ice cream counter: a server in a bright yellow uniform hands over a cone and says it's on the house.

KlingVeoSeedanceHiggsfield

Why it's trending

Starrd's monthly roundup of AI video trends lists an ice cream counter scene restaged from a 2000s comedy film, posted on TikTok on November 8, 2026. People upload two photos, one as the customer and one as the server in a yellow uniform, and either role can be a pet. The page gives no view counts.

How to use it

  1. Pick two clear, front-facing photos: one for the customer and one for the server. A friend, a family member who agreed, or your pet all work.
  2. Upload both to an image-to-video tool that takes more than one reference photo, then paste the main prompt.
  3. Write your own short lines. Keep it to two or three lines so the lip-sync stays clean.
  4. Add music or sound in the app you post to, and label the post as made with AI.

Prompts

Main prompt: the free scoop
Make a vertical video of the two people in these photos at a small retro ice cream parlour with a glass counter full of colourful tubs, pastel walls and bright, cheerful light. Person one is the server, behind the counter in a bright yellow short-sleeve uniform and a paper hat. Person two is the customer, in their own clothes from the photo, leaning on the counter. The server scoops a huge pink cone, leans in and says with a warm, slightly too intense smile: "For you? It's on the house." The customer looks stunned, slowly takes the cone and whispers: "Wait. Really?" The server nods once, very serious. Keep both faces, hair and outfits exactly like the photos. Handheld film look, 10 seconds, no text.
Variation: your pet runs the counter
Make a vertical video of the person and the pet in these photos at a retro ice cream parlour with pastel walls and a glass counter of colourful tubs. The pet stands behind the counter on a stool, wearing a tiny yellow uniform and a paper hat. The person, in their own clothes, steps up to order. The pet slides a small cone across the counter with one paw and gives a slow, proud blink. The person gasps and says: "On the house? You shouldn't have." Keep the person's face and the pet's markings exactly like the photos. 8 seconds, no text.
Variation: the pet is the customer
Make a vertical video of the person and the pet in these photos at a retro ice cream parlour. The person is the server in a bright yellow uniform and paper hat. The pet sits on a stool at the counter, staring hopefully. The server looks around, leans down and whispers: "Don't tell anyone. This one's on the house." They hand over a tiny cup with one small scoop. The pet's ears perk up. Keep the person's face and the pet's markings exactly like the photos. Bright, cheerful light, 8 seconds, no text.
Variation: small business
Make a vertical video of the two team members in these photos at my [café / bakery / ice cream shop]. One stands behind the counter in a bright yellow apron. The other plays the customer. The server hands over [our signature treat] with a big smile and says: "First one's on us this week." The customer takes a bite and gives a happy little shoulder shimmy. Warm, bright light. Keep both faces and outfits exactly like the photos. No logos, no text, 10 seconds.

Tips

  • Write your own lines instead of copying film dialogue. The joke works with any short, over-serious offer.
  • A plain wall behind each person in the photos helps the tool keep faces steady.
  • If you run a real free-scoop offer, say the actual terms in your caption. Don't promise anything you won't honour.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
