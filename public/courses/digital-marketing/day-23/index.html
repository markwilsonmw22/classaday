Google Ads

4 hours

Build a Google Search campaign that captures people actively looking for a service, and understand Performance Max, Demand Gen and Local Services Ads.

Lesson 50 minExample 30 minTasks 160 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

Social ads interrupt people. Google Search ads reach people already searching for what the client sells. For emergency and high-intent services, it's often the most profitable channel.

Agency setup

Create a free Google Ads Manager Account (MCC) for your agency and link each client's account to it. The client owns their account and billing; you manage it from one login.

Account structure

Account → Campaigns (budget, location, bidding) → Ad groups (a theme) → Keywords + Ads

Keep ad groups tightly themed: one for "emergency plumber", one for "water heater replacement", one for "drain cleaning". Each gets ads written for that theme and sends people to the matching landing page.

Keyword match types

Match type How you write it Shows for Use
Broad water heater repair Searches related to the meaning, including variations With smart bidding and good negatives
Phrase "water heater repair" Searches that include the meaning of the phrase A good default for small budgets
Exact [water heater repair] Searches with the same meaning Your proven best keywords

Negative keywords: your money saver

Negatives stop ads showing for irrelevant searches. Common ones for local services: free, diy, how to, jobs, salary, course, training, cheap (if premium), manual, parts, youtube, and competitor brand names (unless deliberate). Check the Search terms report weekly and add new negatives.

Responsive Search Ads

You give Google up to 15 headlines (30 characters each) and 4 descriptions (90 characters each); it tests combinations. Include the keyword, the benefit, proof and a call to action:

  • Emergency Plumber London ON · Fixed Price Before We Start · 412 Five-Star Reviews · Same-Day Service, 7 Days · Call Now, Real People 24/7
  • Burst pipe or no hot water? Licensed techs at your door fast. Fixed price, no surprises.

Assets (formerly extensions)

Add every relevant one: sitelinks (other services), callouts ("Licensed & insured", "Fixed pricing"), call asset (phone number), location (linked Google Business Profile), structured snippets (services list), images. They make ads bigger and usually improve CTR.

Settings that waste money if you get them wrong

  • Location options: target "Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations", not people merely "interested in" the area.
  • Networks: untick the Display Network for Search campaigns.
  • Ad schedule: match the client's hours (or 24/7 for emergency services that answer the phone).
  • Conversion tracking: calls from ads, calls from the website (via Google forwarding numbers or tracking tel: clicks), and form submissions. Without it, smart bidding is guessing.

Bidding

Start with Maximize clicks (with a max CPC limit) or Maximize conversions if tracking is solid. Once you have roughly 15–30 conversions a month, try Target CPA.

Quality Score

Google rates keywords 1–10 based on expected CTR, ad relevance and landing page experience. Higher scores mean lower costs. Improve it with tight ad groups, the keyword in the ad, and a fast, relevant landing page.

Other campaign types

Type What it is When
Performance Max One campaign across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Maps, using Google's AI E-commerce with product feeds; local businesses with strong conversion tracking
Demand Gen Visual ads on YouTube (incl. Shorts), Discover and Gmail Awareness and consideration with good creative
Local Services Ads Pay-per-lead ads at the very top for trades and home services, with a Google verification badge Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, cleaners and more, where available in the client's city and category

Budget maths

Estimate before you launch, and tell the client honestly:

Monthly budget ÷ average CPC = clicks Clicks × conversion rate = leads Leads × close rate × average job value = revenue

2Worked example

Northfield's emergency campaign (example numbers).

  • Ad groups: Emergency plumber · Burst pipe · Water heater replacement · Drain cleaning.
  • Phrase and exact match, 140 negative keywords (from a starting list plus weekly search term reviews).
  • Location: presence in London and 30 km around. 24/7 schedule (they answer at night).
  • Call tracking and form tracking in Google Ads and GA4.

Budget maths before launch: $1,500 a month ÷ ~$9 average CPC ≈ 165 clicks. At a 12% conversion rate ≈ 20 leads. With a 60% close rate ≈ 12 jobs. At an average of $450 per job ≈ $5,400 revenue, about 3.6× the ad spend, before any repeat business.

Month two actuals: 23 leads, 14 jobs, $6,900 in revenue. Riya also applies for Local Services Ads; once the Google verification is approved, it adds another 9 leads a month at a fixed cost per lead.
