Build the store · Day 17 of 30

Policies, trust and checkout

3 hours

Publish your legal pages, configure checkout, and add the trust signals that make strangers comfortable paying you.

Lesson 25 minExample 15 minTasks 140 min≈ 3.0 h total

1Lesson

Payment providers, ad platforms and customers all check the same things before they trust a new store. Today you make sure all of them find what they're looking for.

Required policy pages

Settings → Policies. Shopify can generate templates, but edit every one:

  • Refund policy: paste your Day 12 policy.
  • Shipping policy: your delivery windows and costs.
  • Privacy policy: what data you collect (email, address), why, and how customers can contact you about it. Required by law in most countries.
  • Terms of service: use the template, add your business name and governing province/state.
  • Contact information: business name, address, email, phone. Required for Meta ads approval and many payment providers.

Link all of them in the footer.

Honest trust signals

  • Real contact details and a quick response time.
  • A clear guarantee written in plain words.
  • Payment logos for methods you actually accept.
  • A real About page with a photo of you or your pets, if you're comfortable.
  • Order tracking page.

Avoid fake "as seen on" badges, made-up stats and copied reviews. Smart shoppers spot them instantly.

Checkout settings

Settings → Checkout:

  • Require email or phone; allow guest checkout.
  • Ask for only what you need (no company field unless B2B).
  • Turn on the email marketing checkbox (unticked by default, so consent is clear).
  • Enable abandoned checkout emails (send after 1 hour). You'll improve these on Day 25.
  • Add your logo and brand colours to checkout.

Apps: keep it lean

Every app slows your store and costs money. To start you need at most:

  • A reviews app (for collecting real reviews later)
  • Your supplier app
  • An email app (Shopify Email or Klaviyo; Day 25)

That's it until you've made sales.

2Worked example

Priya's trust checklist, completed:

  • Five policies edited and linked in the footer.
  • Contact page with email, a contact form and "We reply within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday."
  • Guarantee badge under the add-to-cart: "30-day happiness guarantee".
  • About page: a photo of her and her friend's sheltie with three short paragraphs.
  • Checkout: guest checkout on, marketing consent unticked by default, abandoned checkout email after 1 hour.
  • Apps: CJ, Judge.me (reviews, free plan), Shopify Email. Nothing else.

★Tip
