From your door to outer space

One continuous camera move from your storefront or street up through the clouds to the night side of Earth.

RunwayKlingVeo

Why it's trending

Impossible camera moves, like a single zoom from street level to space, are a popular cinematic AI video effect this season.

How to use it

  1. Take a straight-on photo of your storefront, house or street.
  2. Use it as the first frame and paste the prompt.
  3. Reverse the clip in your editor for a space-to-your-door version.

Prompts

Main prompt
Use the image as the first frame. One continuous camera move with no cuts: the camera rises smoothly from street level, up past the rooftops, high above the city, through a layer of clouds, until the curve of the Earth is visible at night with city lights glowing below. Smooth, steady and fast toward the end. 10 seconds, 9:16.
Variation: reveal the business
Start in space looking down at the night side of the Earth. One continuous dive with no cuts: through the clouds, down over a snowy Canadian city, along a main street, and stopping in front of a small café with warm light in the windows. 10 seconds, 9:16.

Tips

  • Using your own photo as the first frame keeps the starting point real.
  • Ask for "one continuous move, no cuts", or the model may jump between shots.
  • End the reversed version on your logo or opening hours.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
