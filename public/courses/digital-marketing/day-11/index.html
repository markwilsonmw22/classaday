SEO from zero to advanced · Day 11 of 30

The SEO audit and monthly reporting

4 hours

Combine everything into a professional SEO audit with a 90-day roadmap, and set up the monthly report you'll send clients.

Lesson 35 minExample 20 minTasks 185 min≈ 4.0 h total

1Lesson

The SEO audit is one of the most powerful things an agency can make. It shows clients exactly what's wrong and how to fix it, and it's often how you win the client in the first place (Day 27 shows how to use a mini audit to get meetings).

The audit structure

  1. Executive summary (one page): the three biggest problems, the three biggest opportunities and the expected impact, in plain English.
  2. Current performance: organic traffic trend, top pages, top keywords, map pack visibility, conversions from organic.
  3. Technical: indexing, speed, mobile, broken links, schema (Day 7).
  4. On-page: titles, headings, content quality and gaps for key pages (Day 6).
  5. Content: keyword map, missing pages, refresh list, cluster plan (Days 5 and 8).
  6. Local: Business Profile, reviews, citations (Day 9).
  7. Authority: links compared with competitors (Day 10).
  8. Competitors: who ranks, why, and what they do better.
  9. Roadmap: prioritised actions for 90 days.

Prioritising: impact vs effort

Sort every recommendation into four boxes:

Low effort High effort
High impact Do first (quick wins) Plan (projects)
Low impact Do when convenient Skip or later

Quick wins build trust. A client who sees results in month one stays for month twelve.

Setting expectations

SEO takes time. Be honest: technical fixes and local SEO can show movement in weeks; new content and links usually take three to six months to show clear results; competitive keywords can take longer. Never guarantee rankings. Promise the work and the reporting, not a position.

The monthly SEO report

Clients don't want 40 pages of charts. They want to know: is it working, what did you do, what's next? One page (or a short dashboard) with:

  1. Headline: "Organic calls up 24% vs last month."
  2. Key results: organic sessions, leads/calls from organic, map pack calls, top keyword movements, new reviews.
  3. What we did: 4–6 bullet points.
  4. What's next: 3–5 bullet points.
  5. Anything we need from you: approvals, photos, access.

Build it in Looker Studio (free) with the Search Console and GA4 connectors (Day 24 covers GA4). Add Business Profile numbers from the Performance section of the profile.

Record a 3-minute Loom video walking through it. Clients watch videos far more often than they read PDFs.

2Worked example

The Northfield audit summary (page 1).

Biggest problems: 14 service pages were hidden from Google by a noindex tag. No page existed for water heaters, your most profitable service. 400+ reviews had no replies.

Biggest opportunities: Fixing indexing and creating 5 service pages could put you on page one for searches with an estimated 3,000+ local searches a month. Your reviews are already better than every competitor's; showing them off on the site could lift calls from the website.

First 30 days: remove noindex, add water heater and emergency pages, compress homepage images, start review replies.

The audit was 18 pages; the owner read page one and said, "Start on Monday." Riya's monthly report is a one-page Looker Studio dashboard plus a Loom video.
