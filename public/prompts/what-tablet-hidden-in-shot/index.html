"What tablet?": deny it while it's hiding in the shot

Swear to camera that you don't own a thing while it sits somewhere in the frame. AI writes the straight-faced denial and helps you plan the hiding spot.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trends roundup updated October 26, 2026 lists a new format spotted that day: creators insist they don't own a certain tablet while one is hidden in the shot, and viewers hunt for it in the comments.

How to use it

  1. Pick the object you will "not own". It can be anything: a tablet, a second coffee mug, a pile of yarn, a new pair of boots.
  2. Use the main prompt to write a short, serious denial and three hiding spots that are hard but fair to find.
  3. Film one take of you talking to camera, with the object tucked into the background, a reflection or your hand.
  4. Post with a caption that dares people to find it. Reply to the first person who spots it.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm filming a short video where I look at the camera and deny owning [object], while it is hidden somewhere in the shot. Write 3 versions of a denial I can say in under 10 seconds: calm, offended and over-the-top serious. Each one should get more specific, like I'm trying too hard to convince people. Then suggest 3 places in [room or setting] where I can hide the object so it's tricky but fair to spot. Keep it light and silly.
Variation: the slow give-away
Write a 15-second script where I deny owning [object] three times. After each denial, the object should become a little easier to see: first hidden, then half in the frame, then right in my hand while I keep denying it. Give me the three lines and a short note on where the object sits in each shot. End with one line that admits nothing.
Variation: caption and comments
My video shows me denying that I own [object] while it's hidden in the shot. Write 5 short captions that invite people to find it without giving away where it is. Then write 5 friendly replies I can use when people find it, and 3 replies for when people guess wrong. Keep every line under 12 words.
Variation: small business
I run [type of business]. I want to film the "I don't own that" trend, where I deny having something while it's hidden in the shot. Make the hidden thing our new [product or menu item]. Write a 10-second denial that sounds fun, not salesy, and 3 hiding spots in our [shop, kitchen or workspace]. Add a caption that asks people to spot it and mentions where they can find it for real. No made-up prices, sales or customer quotes.

Tips

  • Keep a straight face. The joke works because you sound sure of yourself.
  • Hide it in a fair spot. If nobody can find it, people stop looking.
  • Check the frame on your phone before you film, so the object isn't cut off.
  • Pick any object you like. You don't need to show a brand for the joke to land.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
