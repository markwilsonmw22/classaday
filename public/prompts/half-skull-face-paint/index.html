Half skull face paint portrait

A clean editorial portrait with half your face painted as a skeleton and the other half natural.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

Half-face skeleton looks are a classic Halloween make-up idea, and AI versions are popular this month because you get the look without an hour in front of a mirror.

How to use it

  1. Use a straight-on selfie with your hair away from your face.
  2. Paste the main prompt with the photo attached.
  3. Try the glow version for a darker, party feel.

Prompts

Main prompt
Use my attached photo and keep my face identical. Paint the left half of my face as a detailed skeleton: white base, black eye socket, black nose cavity, and teeth drawn over the lips and cheek. Leave the right half natural with light everyday make-up. A clean split exactly down the middle of the face. Dark charcoal backdrop, one soft light from the right, sharp focus on the eyes, editorial portrait photography. Vertical 4:5.
Variation: glow in the dark
Same portrait, but under blacklight: the skeleton paint glows bright white and pale blue, the room is almost dark, faint purple light on the natural side of my face. Keep my face identical. 4:5.
Make it move (video)
Use the finished portrait as the first frame. The person slowly turns their head toward the camera and blinks once, the light flickers slightly, slow push in, 5 seconds, 9:16.

Tips

  • Say which side to paint. If you don't, the AI may paint both.
  • Ask for "face paint", not a mask, so it follows the shape of your face.
  • For the video version, use Runway, Kling or Higgsfield image-to-video and describe only the motion.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
