30-second three-shot mini story

Write one 30-second clip in three shots: a wide opening, a close-up, then a payoff. The same character stays the same in every shot.

KlingVeoRunway

Why it's trending

Kling 4.0 launched worldwide on October 16, 2026. Its announcement says it can make 30-second clips in one go, direct several shots in a row, and keep a character consistent using up to 15 reference images, videos or audio clips. That makes short three-shot stories possible without stitching clips together.

How to use it

  1. Pick one character: yourself (from your own photo), your pet, or an object like a coffee cup. Upload 2–4 clear photos as references.
  2. Paste the main prompt and change the details in each shot to fit your story.
  3. Generate 2 versions and keep the one where the character looks the same in all three shots.
  4. Add your own caption or music in your editor before you post.

Prompts

Main prompt
A 30-second vertical video in three shots, using the person in the reference photos as the main character. Keep their face, hair and clothes the same in every shot. Shot 1 (0–10 s): wide shot of a quiet street at dawn in late autumn. They step out of a front door with a travel mug and pull their scarf tighter. Slow push-in. Shot 2 (10–20 s): close-up of their hands as they find a small paper note tucked under the mug lid. Soft morning light, shallow focus. Shot 3 (20–30 s): medium shot as they read it, laugh, and look back at the house. The camera slowly orbits around them. Smooth cuts between shots, natural colours, gentle street sound.
Variation: small business product story
A 30-second vertical video in three shots for a small candle shop. Use the candle in the reference photos and keep its jar, label colour and size the same in every shot. Shot 1 (0–10 s): wide shot of a cosy shop on a rainy evening, warm light in the window. Slow pull-out from the window to the street. Shot 2 (10–20 s): close-up of a hand lighting the candle, the flame catching, a thin curl of smoke. Shot 3 (20–30 s): medium shot of the candle on a kitchen table at home, a blanket and a mug beside it, rain on the window behind. Smooth cuts, warm amber tones, soft rain sound.
Variation: pet adventure
A 30-second vertical video in three shots starring the dog in the reference photos. Keep its fur colour, markings and collar the same in every shot. Shot 1 (0–10 s): wide shot of a backyard covered in fallen maple leaves. The dog spots something and freezes. Shot 2 (10–20 s): low tracking shot as the dog runs through the leaves, which fly up around it. Shot 3 (20–30 s): close-up as the dog proudly drops a tennis ball at the camera and tilts its head. Bright autumn colours, playful energy, smooth cuts.

Tips

  • Give every shot a time range and one simple action. Crowded shots are where the character starts to drift.
  • Repeat the details you want kept ("same jacket", "same collar") in each shot, not just once at the top.
  • Use photos of yourself, your pet or your own products only. Never use photos of other people without their OK.
  • Label it as AI-made where the platform asks, especially if it shows a person.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
