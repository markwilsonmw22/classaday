Content & marketing · Day 21 of 30

Organic content on TikTok and Reels

3.5 hours

Set up your social accounts properly and publish your first organic videos, so you learn which hooks work for free.

Lesson 15 minExample 10 minTasks 185 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

Organic content is free reach. It's also your testing lab: a hook that gets strong watch time organically is a strong candidate for paid ads.

Account setup

  • Use a business account on TikTok and Instagram (free; gives you analytics).
  • Profile photo: your logo or a photo of you with the product.
  • Bio: who it's for, the benefit, and your link. "Home grooming tools for dog people 🐕 30-day guarantee ↓"
  • Link: your store or a simple link page.

What to post

Rotate between these formats:

  1. Demo: the product doing its thing, satisfyingly.
  2. Problem/solution: the before, then the after.
  3. Tips: useful advice for your niche (grooming tips, not just product).
  4. Behind the scenes: packing orders, testing samples.
  5. Trend formats: popular sounds or formats adapted to your niche.
  6. Answering comments: reply to questions with a video.

Aim for one to three posts a day for the first month. Volume beats perfection while you learn.

What to measure

  • Average watch time and % watched to the end: the best signal that your hook and pacing work.
  • Shares and saves: strong interest.
  • Comments asking for the link: buying intent.
  • Views alone can be misleading.

After 10 posts, look at which ones had the best watch time. Those hooks go into your paid test.

Rules

  • Disclose that you sell the product; don't pretend to be a random customer.
  • Follow platform rules on commercial content and music use (use the platforms' commercial sound libraries for business accounts).
  • Never buy followers or views.

2Worked example

Priya's first five posts:

  1. Satisfying fur release, no talking (14 sec) → 8,200 views, 31% finished.
  2. "3 grooming mistakes husky owners make" tip video → 2,100 views, lots of saves.
  3. Packing her first sample with her friend's dog sniffing it → 900 views.
  4. Black leggings POV → 19,400 views, 42% finished, 37 comments asking for the link.
  5. Reply to a comment: "does it work on cats?" → 3,300 views.

The black leggings hook goes into her paid test as the lead creative.

3Today's tasks

✓By tonight you'll have

Business accounts set up, at least one organic post live and a log to track what works.

★Tip

Post at the same times every day for two weeks, then check your analytics to see when your audience is active. Consistency teaches the algorithm who to show you to.
