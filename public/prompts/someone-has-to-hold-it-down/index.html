"Someone has to hold it down": a love letter to your town

Not everyone can live in a big city. Someone has to hold it down in your town. AI writes the line and plans the clips.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

NapoleonCat's TikTok trends roundup, published November 1, 2026, lists "Someone's gotta hold it down" among its October trends: people film the one place or person still going while everyone else is away, set to a trending sound. An October 7, 2026 write-up on Vadbav describes the same format as picking the underrated option on purpose.

How to use it

  1. Paste the main prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude and fill in your town and three things you love about it.
  2. Pick one on-screen line and film the 5 or 6 quick clips from the shot list. Two or three seconds each is plenty.
  3. Put the line on the first clip, add the trending sound in the app, and post.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm joining the "someone has to hold it down" trend on TikTok and Reels. The joke: not everyone can live in a big famous city, so someone has to hold it down in my town. My town is [town, province]. Three things I love about it: [for example: the coffee drive-thru line at 7 a.m., the hockey rink, the lake in October]. Give me 5 short on-screen lines in the style "Not everyone can be in [big city]. Someone has to hold it down in [my town]." Make them warm and a bit funny, never mean about anyone. Then give me a shot list of 6 clips, 2 to 3 seconds each, that I can film on my phone this week. Plain words, short lines.
Variation: the underrated pick
Write 6 on-screen lines for the "someone has to hold it down" trend, but about choices instead of places. The idea: everyone picks the popular option, so someone has to represent the underrated one. My underrated picks are [for example: plain toast, November, the middle seat, board games over going out]. One line for each, under 15 words, proud and a little dramatic. Suggest one simple clip to film for each line.
Variation: small business
I run a small [type of business, for example: hardware store] in [town, province]. Write 4 on-screen lines for the "someone has to hold it down" trend from the business's point of view, for example: everyone's on vacation, but someone has to keep the coffee hot on Main Street. Make it friendly and local, no prices, no sales pitch. Then give me a 6-clip shot list (2 to 3 seconds each) showing the shop opening, the regulars' favourite corner, the team at work and the street outside. End with one short caption that thanks the people who shop local.

Tips

  • Real, ordinary moments work best: the empty main street at dawn, the snowbank by the post office, the one stoplight.
  • Keep it affectionate. The joke is that you're proud to be there, not that the place is bad.
  • Ask before filming people's faces, and skip licence plates and house numbers.
  • Add the trending sound inside TikTok or Instagram; don't download it from elsewhere.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
