It's officially fall: the cosy switch

Start with your room, porch or shop window as it is, then cut to a cosy fall version on the beat. AI makes the fall look from your own photo.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

An Instagram trends roundup lists "It's officially fall" under October 13, 2026: creators lip-sync the opening of the audio and use the switch in the sound to reveal something fall-themed.

How to use it

  1. Take a clear, straight-on photo of the space you want to change: a room corner, porch, desk or shop window.
  2. Use the main prompt to make the fall version from that photo.
  3. Film a few seconds of you in the real space, then cut to the fall image right on the sound switch.
  4. If you decorate for real after, add that as a final clip.

Prompts

Main prompt
Using my photo, make a cosy fall version of this exact space. Keep the same room, layout, furniture and camera angle. Add warm late-afternoon light, a knit throw, a few small pumpkins and gourds, dried leaves in a vase, a lit candle and a mug of something hot. Use rust, mustard, deep green and cream. Keep it tidy and realistic, like a real photo, not a painting. No text or logos.
Variation: outdoor or porch
Using my photo of my porch or front steps, show the same spot in mid-October in Canada. Keep the house, door and steps exactly as they are. Add red and orange maple leaves on the ground and steps, a few pumpkins, a straw bale, a wool blanket on a chair and soft golden light. Realistic, no text.
Variation: small business
Using my photo of my shop window or counter, make a fall version with the same layout and the same products, kept exactly as they are. Add warm light, a few pumpkins, dried leaves and a small chalkboard with the words "[your short message, for example: Fall hours start Monday]". Don't change the shop sign or add any other text or logos.

Tips

  • The cut has to land right on the switch in the sound. Line it up in your editor before you post.
  • Keep the camera angle the same in the before clip and the AI image, so it feels like a magic switch.
  • Say in your caption that the fall version is AI, especially for a business post.
  • If the AI moves your furniture, ask it again to keep everything in the same place.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
