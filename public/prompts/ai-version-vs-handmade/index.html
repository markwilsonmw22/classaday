AI version vs. handmade: show both, side by side

Show the polished AI version of something you make, then the real one you made by hand. Let people compare, laugh or pick a favourite.

ChatGPTGemini

Why it's trending

SocialPilot's TikTok trends roundup, published October 30, 2026, lists "AI vs. Handmade" as a current format: a polished AI version first, then the real handmade one, both bouncing to the same beat.

How to use it

  1. Pick something you made by hand: a cake, a pumpkin carving, a drawing, a knitted hat, a haircut, a shelf you built.
  2. Use the main prompt to make the AI version from a photo of your real piece or from a short description of it.
  3. Make a Reel or TikTok: the AI image first with "AI version" on screen, then your real one with "Handmade". Cut on the beat so both get the same rhythm.
  4. End with a question in the caption, like "Which one would you rather eat?" Let the comments do the rest.

Prompts

Main prompt: the AI version
Here is a photo of something I made by hand: [what it is]. Make the "AI version" of it: the same object, same colours and same idea, but polished to look perfect, like a studio product photo. Smooth edges, even lighting, a clean light background, soft shadow underneath. Don't add any text, logos or brand names. It should be clearly recognizable as the same thing, just impossibly neat.
Variation: from a description
Make a realistic photo of a [describe your handmade thing, for example "three-layer chocolate cake with orange icing and a wobbly piped pumpkin on top"], made perfectly by a professional. Bright, clean kitchen counter, natural window light, sharp focus, no people, no text and no logos. Make it look almost too perfect, so it contrasts well with a real home-made version.
Variation: on-screen labels
I'm making a short side-by-side video: an AI-made picture of [thing] and the real one I made by hand. Write 6 pairs of short on-screen labels for the two shots. Keep each label under 6 words. Mix funny and proud, for example "AI: flawless" / "Me: made with love (and panic)". Then suggest 3 short captions that end with a question for comments. No hashtags.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, like a bakery, florist or woodworking shop]. Here is a real photo of one of our handmade pieces: [item]. Make an "AI version" of the same item: same shape, colours and size, but machine-perfect and glossy, on a plain light background. Don't add a logo, sign, price or any text. I will post it next to the real photo so customers can see the difference handmade makes.

Tips

  • Always label which one is AI. The trend only works if people know which is which, and it keeps you honest.
  • Shoot the real piece in the same angle and light as the AI image. The gap is funnier or prouder when the setup matches.
  • For a business, let the handmade one win: show the little details, the texture and the hands that made it.
  • Business accounts should pick a sound from the app's commercial library so the post isn't muted.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
