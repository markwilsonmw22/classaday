Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

This policy explains what Class a Day collects, why, who helps us run the site, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address. If you log in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture with us.
  • Your membership: your plan, its status, renewal date, the price group you paid, the country used to set your price, and the ID numbers Stripe gives your customer record, subscription and payment.
  • Payments: Stripe collects your card or payment details and your billing address (needed to work out sales tax). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your progress: finished lessons, ticked tasks, quiz scores, notes, check-ins, streaks, badges, the name you put on certificates and your course schedule. This is saved in your browser, and in your account when you're logged in.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider tells us which country a visit comes from (from its IP address) so we can show the right price. Like any website, our providers keep short-term server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and to fix problems.

How we use it

  • To run your account, unlock what you've paid for and keep your progress in sync across devices.
  • To take payments, give refunds, charge the right sales tax and keep the records tax law requires.
  • To send emails you need, such as sign-in links, receipts and important changes to your membership. We don't send marketing emails unless you've agreed to them.
  • To answer your questions and keep Class a Day secure.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • sa_m is a cookie that tells our site you're a member. It lasts up to 24 hours and is renewed while you use the site. It's needed for the membership to work.
  • Your login (from our login provider, Supabase) and your progress are kept in your browser's local storage.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

Who helps us run Class a Day

We share information only with the companies that run parts of the service for us, and only what they need:

  • Supabase stores accounts, memberships and synced progress. Our database is in Canada (Montreal region).
  • Stripe processes payments and calculates sales tax.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs our server code.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in links.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".

Some of these companies store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is protected by their security measures and contracts but may be subject to local law. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and your progress. We keep payment and tax records for as long as Canadian tax law requires (usually six years).

Your choices and rights

  • You can see your membership on your account page, and log out at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Canada, the EU or the UK), you may have more rights under privacy law. We'll honour them. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Class a Day isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Logins use one-time links or codes instead of passwords, connections are encrypted, and each member can only read their own records in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell members by email or on the site.
