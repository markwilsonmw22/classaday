Sorry, I won't be walking the runway this year

Announce, very seriously, that you won't be walking the big fashion show this year, then give a tiny, everyday excuse. AI writes the excuses.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

A TikTok trend report published October 21, 2026 lists this in its October roundup: creators apologize for not walking a famous fashion show runway, then give a mundane or oddly specific reason, like being at a pumpkin patch every Sunday in October.

How to use it

  1. Think about what fills your calendar right now: a hobby, a chore, a seasonal outing or your pet.
  2. Paste the main prompt into the AI and pick the excuse that makes you laugh most.
  3. Film a short clip of you doing that exact thing, with the apology as on-screen text first and the excuse second.
  4. Keep a straight face. The joke is how seriously you take it.

Prompts

Main prompt
I'm making a funny TikTok. The on-screen text starts: "Sorry, I won't be walking the big fashion show runway this year." Then I give a very ordinary, oddly specific reason. My life right now includes: [for example: soccer practice drop-offs, raking leaves and a sourdough starter]. Write 10 short excuses, each under 15 words, that sound polite and official but are clearly about everyday life. Make a few of them about fall. Don't name any real people, shows or brands.
Variation: pet version
Write 8 excuses from my pet's point of view for why it won't be walking the big fashion show runway this year. My pet is a [type of pet, for example: lazy orange cat] who loves [for example: the sunny spot on the couch]. Each excuse is under 15 words and sounds like a formal statement. Add 3 short captions to go with the video.
Variation: small business
I run a [type of business, for example: bakery in a small town]. Write 8 excuses for why my team won't be walking the big fashion show runway this year, each about real, honest work we're busy with this season, like [for example: Thanksgiving pie orders]. Under 15 words each. Keep it playful. No made-up discounts, prices, customer numbers or claims. Add a short caption with a friendly invite to visit.

Tips

  • Film the excuse, not the runway. A shot of you up to your elbows in leaves is funnier than any fashion shot.
  • Use a formal font or a press-release style for the apology line, then plain text for the excuse.
  • Leave out the name of any real fashion show or brand. "The big fashion show" gets the joke across.
  • Short, specific excuses beat long ones. "Every Sunday in October" is better than "I'm busy".

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
