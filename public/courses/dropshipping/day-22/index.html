Setting up Meta ads the right way

3.5 hours

Set up your Meta business tools, connect tracking with Shopify, and get your ad account ready to launch.

Lesson 25 minExample 15 minTasks 170 min≈ 3.5 h total

1Lesson

Getting the setup right is the difference between ads that learn and optimise, and ads that burn money blindly. Meta needs to know when someone buys, so it can find more people like them.

1. Business portfolio

At business.facebook.com, create a business portfolio for your store (this used to be called Business Manager). Use your real name and business details. Add:

  • Your Facebook Page (create one for the store if you haven't)
  • Your Instagram business account
  • An ad account in your currency and time zone. These can't be changed later.

Turn on two-factor authentication on your personal Facebook account. Accounts without it get restricted more often.

2. Connect Shopify with the Facebook & Instagram app

In Shopify, install Meta's Facebook & Instagram sales channel app. Connect your business portfolio, page, ad account and pixel. Set data sharing to the highest level (it uses the Conversions API, sending purchase events from your server as well as the browser, which makes tracking more reliable).

3. Domain verification

In Meta's Business Settings → Brand safety → Domains, add your store's domain and verify it (usually via a meta tag Shopify adds, or a DNS record).

4. Check events

In Meta Events Manager, open your dataset/pixel and use Test events. Browse your store, add to cart, start checkout. You should see PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart and InitiateCheckout arrive.

5. Payment method and spending limit

Add a business card to the ad account. Set an account spending limit equal to your test budget so you can never overspend by mistake.

6. Ad policies

Read Meta's advertising standards for your category before you launch. Common rejections: exaggerated claims, before/after body images, personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"), and landing pages without contact information or policies.

A note on new accounts

New ad accounts sometimes get reviewed or temporarily restricted. Don't panic and don't create multiple accounts. Use the official appeal process, keep your page and store consistent, and start with modest budgets.

2Worked example

Priya's setup notes:

  • Business portfolio "Hearth & Hound", Facebook Page and Instagram connected.
  • Ad account in CAD, Toronto time zone, spending limit $620.
  • Facebook & Instagram app installed in Shopify, data sharing set to Maximum.
  • Domain verified with the meta tag.
  • Test events: all four events received within a minute.

She also reads the pet category rules and removes "vet-recommended" from her copy; she has no vet endorsement.
