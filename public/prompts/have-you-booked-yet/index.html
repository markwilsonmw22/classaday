"It's time. Have you booked yet?": the holiday booking nudge

The holiday music is back, so remind people to book early. AI writes a short, friendly "it's time" nudge for your Reel and caption, with no fake urgency.

ChatGPTGeminiClaude

Why it's trending

SocialPilot's Instagram Reels audio list, published October 30, 2026, says the most famous holiday pop song returns every November 1 with "it's time" memes. It names service businesses using it for booking pushes, like a salon asking "have you booked yet?", and lists another business posting "November bookings are open".

How to use it

  1. Pick what people should book before the holidays: an appointment, a table, a class or a gift order.
  2. Use the main prompt to write the on-screen lines and a caption.
  3. Film one short clip: you or your space going from calm to festive, with the lines appearing in turn.
  4. Add a holiday song from the app's own library and post it early in November.

Prompts

Main prompt
I run [type of business, for example a hair salon, barber, nail studio, restaurant or photography studio] in [town]. Write a short Instagram Reel for early November about booking before the holidays. Give me: 1) 4 on-screen lines, each under 8 words, starting with "It's time." and ending with "Have you booked yet?", 2) a caption under 60 words that says how to book: [online, phone, DM], 3) one line for the end screen. Friendly and festive, a little funny. Only use these real details: [your holiday hours or dates]. No fake countdowns, no "only 2 spots left", no made-up discounts.
Variation: "bookings are open" post
Write 3 short versions of a "Holiday bookings are open" post for my [business]. One cosy, one funny, one very short for a Story. Each under 40 words. Mention [what people can book] and [how to book]. Don't make up prices, numbers, reviews or deadlines I didn't give you.
Variation: friends and family
Write 5 funny "It's time." lines for a group chat or Reel reminding my friends and family to lock in holiday plans: who's hosting, the secret gift exchange date, the potluck list, the photo day. Each under 12 words. Warm and teasing, not bossy.
Variation: plan the clip
I want to film a 10-second Reel for my [business] that switches from normal to holiday mode on a beat, then shows a "Have you booked yet?" line. Give me a shot list of 3 to 4 quick shots I can film on my phone in 15 minutes in my [shop, studio or restaurant], plus where each line of text should appear.

Tips

  • Be honest about availability. Say when you're really booking up, and never invent a countdown or "last spots".
  • Put how to book in the first line of the caption, so people don't have to hunt.
  • Use a holiday song from the app's library. Business accounts should use the commercial sound library so the post isn't muted.
  • Pin the post to your profile until mid-December so new visitors see it.

Results vary between tools and runs. Make a few versions and keep the best. Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed, and label AI content where the platform asks.
